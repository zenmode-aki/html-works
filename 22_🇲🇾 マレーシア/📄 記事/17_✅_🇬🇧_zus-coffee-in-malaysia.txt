If you go to Malaysia, get a drink at ZUS COFFEE

This is ZUS COFFEE.
It is written "ZUS," and I do not know how to read it, but maybe it is read "Zus."
I had heard from someone who stayed in Malaysia that ZUS COFFEE is really delicious.
It is a shop whose trademark is a picture of an older man.
I ordered something called a "CEO Latte."
It was really delicious.
My sister, who went with me, also liked the ZUS COFFEE drinks.
If you go to Malaysia, I recommend buying a drink at ZUS COFFEE.
