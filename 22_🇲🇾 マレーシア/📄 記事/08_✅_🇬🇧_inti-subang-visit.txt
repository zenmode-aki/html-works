University visit, day 1 afternoon: INTI

University visit, day 1 afternoon! 🐧
I went to INTI International College Subang.
It was in a place with a fairly local feel.
This is really just my first impression, but I felt there were many students who speak Chinese.
The school has hospitality courses as its strength, and inside the school there were real hotel beds and a reception desk.
My sister was interested in that kind of tourism industry, so I thought she could do interesting studies.
They say the tuition is reasonable too.
