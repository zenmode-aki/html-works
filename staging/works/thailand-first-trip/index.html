🛫First Time Abroad

My first time abroad was when I was 19. I went to Thailand alone for about six nights and seven days. I spent about five days in Bangkok and two days in Kanchanaburi.

🌏Another View

One reason I went to Kanchanaburi was the history of World War II. The war history I learned in Japan felt centered on Japan's side.

❓My Question

So I became interested in this question: “What was Japan like then, when seen from overseas?”

📷What I Saw

I visited places related to the Death Railway and a museum with exhibits about World War II. When I saw the photos and exhibits from that time, I felt the history was much harsher than I had imagined.
