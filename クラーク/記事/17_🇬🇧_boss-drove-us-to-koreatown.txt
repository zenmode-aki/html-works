The owner of my language school often drove me to Koreatown in Angeles

About 20 minutes by car from the language school where I was, there was a Koreatown.
It is in an area called Angeles.
The Korean owner (the boss) of the language school often took me there.
In Koreatown, he took me to many different restaurants.
I still faintly remember the smell inside the boss's car and the Korean music that often played in the car.
That is how often he took me somewhere every weekend and let me have many experiences.
