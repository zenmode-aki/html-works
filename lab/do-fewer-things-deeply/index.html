Doing too many things makes days boring, so do fewer things deeply 🍢

🧠 Psychology😊 Happiness✅ Productivity
🥱 Sound Familiar

You do many things every day, but do you somehow feel bored?

🎯 The Point

It is said that 1 reason is doing too many kinds of things.

🌀 The Reason

This is because when you do many things at once, you have no time to enjoy each one.

Your mind always looks at the next thing, and what you are doing now does not stay in your memory.

🍢 For Example

For example, one person slowly grills yakitori over charcoal, and another eats yakitori while looking at a phone.

The one having a good time is the person grilling slowly.

Trips are also more memorable when you go to fewer places and enjoy 1 place slowly.

🔥🍢Grill slowly
📱🍢📺Eat while scrolling
⚡ TRY IT
Grill 1 yakitori
Turn it 6 times. Then try "everything at once" and compare the fun.
💨
📱 Ping! A new message!
📺 Oh, this TV show is funny…
💬 Someone posted a photo!
😋 Fun🤍🤍🤍🤍🤍🤍
Smell the smoke. Hear the sizzle.
🔥 Sizzle… What a nice smell!
👀 Eyes on the phone… The yakitori is just there.
🍢 Juicy and smoky! I will remember this taste.
🤔 Wait… what did I just eat?
🍚 Try This

Today, try putting your phone down, even just while you eat.

🐧 In Short

Do fewer things, and enjoy them more deeply.
