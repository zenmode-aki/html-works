Keep 2 or 3 compliments ready, and they come out easily 🎯

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
🎯 Good Praisers

It seems people who are good at praising keep 2 or 3 compliments ready.

💬 My Three

For example, "Great job!", "I see!", and "That's nice!"

👏 Great job!💡 I see!✨ That's nice!
💨 Comes Out Easily

If you have them ready, they come out of your mouth easily without thinking.

⚡ TRY IT

📅 21 Days

It is said that if you praise for 10 seconds a day for 21 days, you start to see people's good points naturally.
