Give yourself a flower stamp, even for a small step 💮

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity🚶 Walks
📅 Sound Familiar

Do you ever decide "I will walk every day," but stop after 3 days?

After you rest once, you often think "It's over" and just stop.

💮 The Point

Try to record the things you want to continue with a flower stamp on a calendar.

And make your goal only "to continue," not "to do it well."

👟 For Example

For example, if you only put on your shoes and went outside, that day gets a stamp too.

Even after 4 days off, if you start again the next day, it is a flower stamp.

💡 The Reason

This is because a line of stamps lets you see your success.

Even small stamps, when they connect, grow a feeling of "I don't want to stop."
