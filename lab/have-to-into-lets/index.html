Changing "I have to" into "let's" gives you more energy ☀️

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💡 Tips
😩 Have To

When you say "I have to," you feel like someone is making you do it.

⏰ Try This

Instead of "I have to get up at 6," say "Let's get up at 6."

🪨I have to…
🎈Let's!
🧽 Cleaning Too

"I have to clean" becomes "OK, I will make it shiny!"

⚡ TRY IT
Flip your to-do list
Tap each heavy "have to" card. The job is the same. Only the words change.
🐧😩
🪫 heavy🔋 full energy
🔄 Flipped: 0 / 4
🤩 Same to-do list. Way more energy!
✨ More Fun

Just by changing your words, the same thing becomes a little more fun.
