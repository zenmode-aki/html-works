Link a new habit to a habit you already have 🔗

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity💡 Tips
📅 Sound Familiar

Do you ever decide, "I will do it every day," but forget it in about 3 days?

🔗 The Point

A new habit is easier to keep if you link it right after a habit you already have.

💡 The Reason

This is because your usual action tells you "This is next," so you do not need to think "When?" each time.

🚌 For Example

For example, decide, "When I get on the bus, I open a book."

At night, you can link them like, "After I brush my teeth, I stretch, then I sleep."

Your usual habit becomes the switch for the next habit.
