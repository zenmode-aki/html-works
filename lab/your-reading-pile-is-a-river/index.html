Your reading pile is not a bucket to empty, it is a river 🌊

📖 Books📚 Learning💡 Tips
📚 Sound Familiar

Do books and articles you want to read "someday" keep piling up?

When you see the pile, you think, "I must read them all," and you panic.

🌊 The Point

But try thinking of the pile as a river, not a bucket to empty.

🪣 The Reason

Your pile grows because there are too many things in the world you want to read.

New books come out every day, so no one can read them all.

⚡ TRY IT
Bucket or river?
📕
📚 In the bucket: 0/10
🎣 Scooped: 0/3
🐧
New books keep coming. Try to empty the bucket!
Read, read, read…! 😰
😵 It overflows! You can never empty it.
Books float down the river. Tap the ones you like.
😌 The rest just flows by. That is fine.
🎣 You scooped 3 good ones. That is enough! 🎉
🎣 Just Scoop

If it is a river, it is enough to scoop up the one you like sometimes.

🏛️ Library Calm

For example, when you see unread books at a library, you do not panic, right?

This is because you do not think reading them all is your job.

🐧 Try This

Today, pick up just the 1 book you are most curious about from the pile.
