Stop 4 heavy things, and life gets lighter 🎈

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset😊 Happiness💡 Tips
🎒 Sound Familiar

Do you ever feel that your heart is somehow heavy and you get tired easily every day?

🪨 Four Things

It is said there are 4 things to stop for an easy life.

Regret, worry, caring what people think, and blaming others.

This is because these 4 things sit on your heart like heavy rocks you cannot see.

😩Regret
😰Worry
💬What people think
💢Blaming others
⚡ TRY IT
Put down the heavy rocks
Tap a rock to change it into something light. Then walk to the 🏁.
🏁
🪨🪨🪨🪨
🐧 😩
🎒 Your load: 40 kg
🐾 Steps: 0
So heavy… Each step is tiny.
A little lighter! Your steps are getting bigger.
So light! You can almost fly 🎈
🏁 Goal! 0 steps✨ Light rocks, big steps. Easy!😅 That was hard. Try putting down the rocks first!
📝 End Regret

You can end regret right there by deciding, "Next time, I will do this."

🧳 Let Worry Go

For worry, do what you can to get ready, like an emergency bag, and then let it go.

🌱 Your Own Score

Instead of what people think, look at "Am I growing more than 6 months ago?"

🐧 Your Part

When you want to blame others, think, "What can I do?"

Even putting down just one heavy rock makes every day much lighter.
