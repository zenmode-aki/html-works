Decide one question before you meet, and you will be less nervous 📝

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🫧 Feelings
😰 Sound Familiar

When you meet a friend after a long time, do you ever get nervous about what to talk about?

When you are nervous, your head goes blank, and only talk about the weather comes out.

📝 The Point

So before you meet, decide just 1 question for that person.

😊 The Reason

This is because when your first words are ready, that alone calms you down.

Also, the other person will be happy and think, "You remembered me."

🥶 "Um… nice weather…" Your head froze.
🙂 You talked well with the friends you prepared for. But you froze with the others.
🎉 Every talk started smoothly! No nerves at all.
🐶 For Example

For example, "How is golf these days?" or "How is your dog?" is enough.

You just remember 1 thing the person talked about last time.

🚃 Try This

On the train before you meet, try thinking for just 1 minute.

🐧 In Short

Just taking 1 question with you makes small talk much easier.
