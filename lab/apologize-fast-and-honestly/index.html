Say sorry fast and from your heart, and a mistake becomes trust 💛

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😅 Mistakes
😣 Sound Familiar

When you are late to meet a friend, do you ever start with an excuse, like "The train was crowded"?

Maybe you cannot say sorry because you think, "Saying sorry is not cool."

⚡ The Point

When you make a mistake, say sorry right away from your heart, with no lies or excuses.

💡 The Reason

This is because when someone says sorry quickly, people feel, "This person is honest."

On the other hand, people who do not say sorry are seen as "people you cannot trust."

✨ Sorry Is Brave

Saying sorry is not uncool; it is brave and clean.

⚡ TRY IT
Oops! What do you say?
☕ You spilled juice on your friend's notebook. 📕 You forgot to give back a book you borrowed. ⏰ You were 15 minutes late.

⚡ Speed bonus!

📕 For Example

For example, if you forget to give back a book, say clearly, "I'm sorry, I forgot."

💎 In Short

Then your mistake turns into a chance to be seen as "a person you can trust."
