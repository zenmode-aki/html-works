For deep sadness, a sad song may help more than a bright one 🎻

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💡 Tips
🎺 Sound Familiar

When you are very down, has a bright song ever made you feel even worse?

🎻 The Point

When you are very down, I recommend first matching your feeling with a sad song.

🌧️ The Reason

This is because when your feeling and the song are too far apart, you feel "left behind alone," and you feel empty.

If you are only a little down, a bright song can give you energy.

⛅ + 🎺A little down: OK!
⛈️ + 🎺Very down: empty…
⚡ TRY IT
Match the song to your heart
Set your sadness, then pick a song.
🌧️Sadness4
🙉 Not matching🎵 In sync
Which song fits your heart now?
🎺 In sync! The bright song lifts you up.
😶 Too bright… You feel a little empty.
🥱 A little too slow for today. A bright song is OK!
🎻 In sync. The song understands you.
🎶 …and little by little, brighter songs.
🌤️ Now a bright song feels good!
🌙 For Example

For example, on a rainy night, start with a quiet song.

When your feeling matches, change to brighter songs little by little.

🎧 Try This

Next time a hard day comes, try to choose a song that "fits how you feel now."

🌤️ In Short

When a song stays close to your feeling, your heart slowly starts to look up.
