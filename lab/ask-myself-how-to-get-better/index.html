People who stand out always ask themselves "How can I do better?" 👟

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💪 Fitness
👟 Two Runners

There is a person who just runs for 3 years, and a person who runs for 3 years while thinking.

🤔 Good Questions

The thinking runner asks, "How can I get faster?", "What about my form?", "What about my shoes?"

⚡ TRY IT
👟 The 3-year race
Before the race, ask yourself questions. Each one makes you faster!
Just runs 🐧 🏁
You (asks "How?") 🐧 🏁
0 years
🤝 Same speed, same result. Just running does not make a gap.
🚀 You are ahead! Keep asking "How?"
🏆 Way ahead! Asking "How?" made a big gap.
🚀 Big Gap

After 3 years, the gap between the 2 is very big.

❓ Ask Yourself

Whatever you do, try asking yourself, "How can I?"
