Praise people when they are not there, and it reaches them bigger ✈️

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😊 Happiness
🗣️ To Your Face

If someone says to your face, "Your photos are great," you may think, "You are just being nice."

✈️ From A Friend

But if you hear, "Our friend said your photos are great," your heart jumps.

This is called the "Windsor effect."
