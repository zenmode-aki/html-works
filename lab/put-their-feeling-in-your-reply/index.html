Put their feeling into your reply, and the talk keeps going 💬

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🫧 Feelings
💤 Sound Familiar

Do you ever reply "Oh" to a friend's story, and the talk stops there?

💬 The Point

When you reply, put their feeling into it, and the talk keeps going.

🌱 The Reason

This is because when you touch their feeling, they remember that time, and more things they want to say come out.

⏰ For Example

For example, to "My alarm battery died, so I was late," reply not "Oh, I see," but "Wow, you must have been shocked!"

Then the story grows bigger and bigger: "I was! I ran to the station with my hair sticking up!"

😐"Oh, I see."
😲"Wow, you must have been shocked!"

💛 More Words

"I know!" and "Right?!" are also words that stay close to their feelings.

🎧 Try This

Next time you listen, try to reply while you imagine, "How did they feel then?"

One word with a feeling in it brings out the rest of the story.
