Before you sleep, praise yourself for just 20 seconds 🌙

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings😴 Sleep
🌙 Sound Familiar

Before bed, do you ever remember only the things you could not do today, and cannot sleep?

🛏️ The Point

For just 20 seconds before bed, praise yourself: "You did well today too."

🌱 The Reason

When you get the habit of praising yourself at the end of the day, you start to feel, "I am doing OK."

It is said that as this feeling builds up, a heart that is strong against worry slowly grows.

You can do 20 seconds even on a very tired day.
