People are not perfect from the start, so you do not need to expect too much 🧩

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💭 Mindset
⏰ Sound Familiar

When a friend comes late to a meetup, you may get annoyed without meaning to.

🧩 The Point

But people are not perfect from the start.

🎒 The Reason

Everyone lives each day with worries or a body that does not feel well.

So if you always expect 100%, you will feel let down more often.

⚡ TRY IT
The meetup of 10 penguins
🐧
Everyone, meet at 10:00! Hmm… 1 penguin is missing. "Why are you late?!" 💢 "1 late out of 10. Normal!" "It's OK! Good to see you!" 💗
Meetups0
Someone was late0
Your stress😐
Pick a mode. Then call 10 friends.
😡 Someone is late every time. Your stress goes up!
😌 10 penguins, 1 late. That is just normal!
🐧 Tap the late penguin and say "It's OK!"
💗 You forgave them. Now it is easier to forgive yourself too.
🔔 For Example

For example, you think beforehand, "If 10 people meet, about 1 will be late."

Then, even if someone is late, you can smile and say, "Well, that is normal."

And when you can forgive others, you can also forgive your own mistakes.

💗 Try This

Next time someone comes late, try saying, "I am happy to see you."

🐧 In Short

For others and for yourself, not expecting too much is just right.
