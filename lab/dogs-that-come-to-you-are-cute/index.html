People who come over and talk to you first look cute 🐶

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
🐶 Sound Familiar

At a pet shop, don't you think a puppy that wags its tail and comes to you is cute?

Somehow it pulls your heart more than a puppy sleeping in the corner.

🐧 The Point

People are the same, and it seems people who come over and talk to you first look cute.

💖 The Reason

This is because when someone comes over, you feel, "They are happy to see me."

People easily like people who like them.

⚡ TRY IT
Which puppy feels cuter?
Tap each window to wave at the puppy.
🐶 Puppy 1 ran over, wagging its tail. It feels cuter, right?
🐧 Now it is your turn!
🔒 Wave at both puppies first.
… Hi! 😊 🐧 🐧🐧🐧
💖💖💖💖💖
😶 You waited… but nobody noticed you. 💖 "So cute! Come join us!"
☕ For Example

For example, if you see someone you know at a cafe, you go and say "Hello" with a smile.

You can become friends much faster than if you wait for them to talk to you.

➡️ Try This

Next time you meet someone, try taking 1 step closer first.

🐾 In Short

Instead of waiting, go and wag your tail first.
