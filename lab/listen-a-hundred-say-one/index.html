Listen 100 times first, and then say only 1 thing 🎧

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
💬 Sound Familiar

When you listen to a friend, do you ever want to say, "You should do this"?

But if you give advice right away, your friend may feel, "You do not understand me."

🎧 The Point

When you want to say something, first listen a lot with "uh-huh," and then say only 1 thing.

🔑 The Reason

This is because people want to listen to someone who listened to them.

If you listen well first, the door of their heart opens.

⚡ TRY IT
