Wanting something just because it is popular is not a real want 🏷️

🧠 Psychology💴 Money💭 Mindset
🏷️ Sound Familiar

When a shop sign says "Big hit!" or "Selling fast!", do you somehow want it?

But at home, you often do not use it much.

Big hit!Selling fast!
🎭 The Point

That "I want it" may be a want you borrowed from others, not your own.

A real want still fills your heart after you get it.

📚 Only the book still shines. That is your real want.
💛 The Reason

This is because things that everyone chooses look good to us.

But if it fits you or not is a different story.

🔍 For Example

For example, peel off the "No. 1" sticker in your head, and think if you still want it.

If you still want it without the sticker, it is probably a real want.

🐧 Try This

Next time, before you buy, ask yourself, "Do I want it without the sticker?"

When you choose real wants, you stay happy long after you buy.
