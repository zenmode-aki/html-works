Walk a little faster every day, and many things get faster 💨

🧠 Psychology🚶 Walks✅ Productivity
🐢 Sound Familiar

Does everything take you a long time, and do you think, "I am slow"?

It is said that people who can do everything fast have a fast everyday pace.

⏩ The Point

Just by raising your everyday pace a little, many things become faster.

🐶 The Reason

This is because your body's pace is made by your daily habits.

There is also a story: a puppy raised in a slow place becomes a slow dog.

🪜 For Example

For example, walk to the station a little faster, and go up stairs with quick steps.

When your body moves, your head wakes up, too.
