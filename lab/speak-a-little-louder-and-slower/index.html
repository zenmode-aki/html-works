Speak a little louder and slower, and people will not take you lightly 🔊

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
🍩 Sound Familiar

Have you ever ordered at a shop, and the staff said, "Sorry?"

When you talk with friends, sometimes only your story gets passed over.

🔊 The Point

If you just speak a little louder and slower, people take your words differently.

🐭 The Reason

This is because a small, fast voice sounds like you have no confidence.

On the other hand, a person who speaks slowly looks calm.
