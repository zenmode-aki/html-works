You cannot erase the past, but you can change "because of" into "thanks to" 🐻‍❄️

🧠 Psychology😅 Mistakes💭 Mindset
🌀 Sound Familiar

Do you ever remember a bad memory again and again?

🐻‍❄️ White Bear

It is said that when people hear "Do not think about it," they think about it more.

So the more you try to forget a bad memory, the more it stays in your head.

👓 The Point

You cannot erase the past, but you can change how you see it.

The trick is to change "because of" into "thanks to."

😣Because of…
☀️Thanks to…
⚡ TRY IT
Try to forget the white bear
🐻‍❄️🐻‍❄️🐻‍❄️🐻‍❄️🐻‍❄️🐻‍❄️
Bears in your head: 1
😅 See? The harder you try to forget, the more bears come.
🔄 Now flip the words. Tap a card!
☀️ The past did not change. Only the words did. The bears are sleeping now.
🚃 For Example

For example, change "Because I missed the train" into "Thanks to that, I found a nice cafe by the station."

What happened is the same, but when you change the words, you feel lighter when you remember it.

☀️ Try This

Today, when a bad memory comes back, try adding one "thanks to that."

🐧 In Short

You cannot change the past, but you can change what you call it.
