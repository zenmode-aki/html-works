Standing on your own feet is like a diet: it slips back easily ⚖️

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💡 Tips
🛋️ Sound Familiar

Do you sometimes just follow others, or let someone else decide things for you?

🐾 The Point

"Standing on your own feet" means thinking and deciding by yourself, and it does not end after you do it once.

⚖️ The Reason

This is because, like a diet, it soon goes back if you relax.

Leaning on the easy way is natural for everyone.

🌱 Start Small

So you do not need to change a lot, and it is OK to start with small things.

⚡ TRY IT
The 7-day challenge
Each day, pick 1 small step. Watch the meter.

📅 In Short

Even if you go back a little on some days, that is normal.

Doing a little every day works best.
