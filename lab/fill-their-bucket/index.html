When you fill someone's heart bucket, your bucket fills too 🪣

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😊 Happiness
🍪 Sound Familiar

After you are kind to someone, do you ever feel good yourself, too?

🪣 Heart Bucket

There is an idea that everyone has an invisible "bucket" in their heart.

🐧 🐧
💧 Pour Some Water

Compliments, "thank you," and small kindness pour water into the other person's bucket.

When the bucket is full, that person gets energy and can be kind to others, too.

↩️ It Comes Back

Then that person pours water back into your bucket.

On the other hand, a mean word takes water out of both their bucket and yours.

🏪 For Example

For example, just saying "thank you" to a convenience store clerk pours water, too.

Today, try to say a small "thank you" to one person.

🌊 Both Full

The person who pours and the person who gets it both become full.
