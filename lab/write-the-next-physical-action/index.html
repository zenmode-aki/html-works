On your to-do list, write the next action your hands can do 📞

📖 Books✅ Productivity💡 Tips
☁️ Sound Familiar

Do you ever write "Clean my room" on your to-do list, but cannot start?

That is because it is not clear what to do first.

📞 The Point

On your list, write "the next action your hands can do," not a cloudy goal.

⚡ TRY IT
The action machine
Tap a cloudy task. The machine turns it into something your flippers can do.
⚙️🔧⚙️
🐧 Flipper-ready: 0/4
Cloudy tasks float. It is hard to grab them.
See? Now you know exactly what to do first.
🎉 All 4 are things your flippers can do right now!
🔧 For Example

For example, write "Call the mechanic," not "Fix the car."

For "Clean my room," write "Pick up the socks on the floor."

🚀 The Reason

With these, you can move your body right now without thinking too much.

Once you start 1 thing, the next thing becomes easy to see.

✏️ Try This

Next time you write a list, try to use action words like "call," "pick up," or "write."

On your list, write things your hands can do.
