When someone teases you, just say "Yes, it is!" and play along 🍉

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🫧 Feelings
😤 Sound Familiar

Have you ever got upset when a friend teased you a little?

In fact, the more upset you get, the more fun your reaction is, so they tease you again.

😎 The Point

For light teasing, it is best to play along and say "Yes, it is!"

😤Upset
😎Play along
🦜 The Reason

This is because the person only wants to see your upset face.

If you laugh and play along, they lose interest and stop teasing you.

🍉 For Example

For example, if someone says, "Your shirt looks like a watermelon," you say, "Yes! Didn't you know I love watermelon?"

🛟 But Remember

But if the words really hurt, or it is bullying, you do not need to laugh it off.

In that case, please talk to someone you trust.

🍃 Willow Style

Let light teasing pass softly, like a willow lets the wind pass.
