Your real life has already started, even with chores left 🌷

📖 Books💭 Mindset😊 Happiness
🧺 Sound Familiar

Do you ever think, "When all these chores are done, I will start what I really want to do"?

But laundry and dishes come back again and again, however many times you finish them.

🗓️ The Point

The day when "everything is done and real life starts" never comes, however long you wait.

Even now, busy with chores, you are already in your real life.

⚡ TRY IT

🔁 The Reason

This is because new chores come every day, as long as you live.

For example, if there is a book you want to read, read just 5 pages today, even with laundry left.

🌷 Try This

Even if some chores are left, try doing a little of what you love first.
