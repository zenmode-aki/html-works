Watch difficult people like animals at a zoo, without judging them 🔭

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💭 Mindset
🦁 Loud Lion

Even if a lion is loud, you don't get angry, right?

🦍 Quiet Gorilla

Even if a gorilla ignores you, you just think, "That's how this animal is."

🔭 Just Watch

With people too, if you watch without deciding good or bad, you can see how to deal with them.

⚡ TRY IT
Put on your zoo glasses
Tap the animals. Then switch the glasses and tap again.

りかい
rikai = understanding 🤝ikari = anger 💢
🔄 Read It Backward

Read "rikai" (understanding) backward, and you get "ikari" (anger).

Maybe we get angry because we don't understand.
