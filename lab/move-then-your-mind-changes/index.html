Moving first is easier than changing your mind first 🐾

📖 Books🫧 Feelings💭 Mindset
😟 Sound Familiar

Do you ever want to start something, but feel too worried to move?

If you wait until your worry is gone, you can never move.

🐾 The Point

Moving first and then changing your mind is easier than changing your mind first.

📈 The Reason

This is because just thinking in your head does not give you more proof that "I can do it."

Each time you take 1 small step, you get 1 more piece of proof: "It worked out."

⚡ TRY IT
Think first, or move first?

💻 For Example

For example, if you want to start English lessons, just opening the school's website is fine at first.

⏱️ Try This

Today, try doing the thing on your mind for just 5 minutes.

It is OK if the worry is still there.

🚩 In Short

If you move first, your feelings will follow later.
