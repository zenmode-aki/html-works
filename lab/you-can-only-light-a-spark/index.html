You cannot change someone's heart, you can only light a small spark ✨

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😊 Happiness
☁️ Sound Familiar

When a friend or family member is feeling down, you want to cheer them up somehow.

But even if you say, "Be more positive," they do not change easily.

🚪 The Reason

This is because you cannot change another person's heart from the outside.

A heart moves only when that person thinks, "I want to try it," by themselves.

✨ The Point

All we can do is make that small spark.

⚡ TRY IT
Push, or enjoy?
Your friend is under a gray cloud. What will you do?
🐧
You
☁️
I am fine like this. Stop pushing me… 😣 …? What is that? You look happy. What is so fun? Can I try it too?
🐧
Friend
➡️ Pushes: 0 · ☀️ Happy moments: 0
Pushing does not move a heart. Try the other button.
✨ Not by pushing: your friend came by themselves. That is a spark.
🍳 For Example

For example, when you see someone cooking happily, you want to cook too, right?

🐧 You First

So first, enjoy your own days, and try to make yourself happy.

🦁 Needs Courage

Doing what you like without worrying about other people's eyes may take a little courage.

But your happy face can be a spark for someone.
