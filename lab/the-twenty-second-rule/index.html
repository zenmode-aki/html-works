Put bad habits 20 seconds farther and good habits 20 seconds closer ⏱️

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity💡 Tips📖 Books
🍪 Sound Familiar

Do you want to stop eating snacks, but your hand still reaches for them?

🏠 The Point

When you want to change a habit, changing where you put things works better than willpower.

⏱️ The Reason

This is because people lose a lot of motivation when starting takes just a little effort.

It is said that about 20 seconds of effort is enough.

↔️ How To

For things you want to stop, add 20 seconds of effort before you start.

For things you want to keep doing, cut 20 seconds of effort before you start.

📖 For Example

For example, put snacks in a drawer in a far room, and put the book you want to read on the table.

🍪+20 sec: far drawer
📖−20 sec: on the table
