Your part ends when the bowling ball leaves your hand 🎳

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💡 Tips
🎳 My Area

In bowling, "my area" is only until I throw the ball.

After it leaves my hand, praying does not change where it goes.

✋Before the throw: my area
🙏After the throw: not my area

Switching to a bus is much easier than getting angry at the station staff.
