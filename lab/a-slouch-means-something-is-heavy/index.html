A round back can be a sign that your heart feels heavy 🎒

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💭 Mindset
🎒 Sound Familiar

Do you ever notice that your back is round?

When you carry a heavy backpack, your body naturally bends forward.

🪨 The Point

A round back may be a sign that your heart is carrying something heavy.

💭 The Reason

This is because worries make your feelings heavy, and it shows in how you stand.

The heavy thing is not a bag, but your feelings.

🪶 For Example

For example, when you feel worried, say light words out loud, like "It's OK, it's OK."

"Maybe I'm thinking too much" or "It will be fine" is OK too.
