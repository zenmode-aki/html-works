Instead of blaming yourself, make a small plan for next time ⏰

🧠 Psychology😅 Mistakes💭 Mindset
🔁 Sound Familiar

When you oversleep and are late, do you ever blame yourself: "Late again. I am no good"?

But blaming yourself does not change the next morning.

🧭 The Point

The important thing is to think "What will I do next?" instead of "I am no good."

🩹 The Reason

This is because each time you blame yourself, your heart gets a small scratch, and you slowly lose confidence.

When you lose confidence, it gets harder to try new things.

Oh no, 9:05. Late again! What will you do? "I am no good… I am always like this." 😣 The next morning… 🌅 A new scratch on your heart… and the same morning again. 🔁 3 scratches, and still late. Blaming does not move the clock. ⏰ Good! Pick 1 small plan for tomorrow. 8:55. On time! ✨ A small plan changed tomorrow.
⏰ For Example

For example, if you were late, decide to "set the alarm 10 minutes earlier."

A small plan you can do right away, like "lay out your clothes the night before," is also fine.

📝 Try This

🌅 Next Step

Deciding your next step changes tomorrow more than blaming does.
