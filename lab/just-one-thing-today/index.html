You cannot do everything, so just do one thing today ✅

📖 Books💭 Mindset😊 Happiness
📚 Sound Familiar

Do you have so many things to do that you do not know where to start?

🎛️ The Root

Much of that heavy feeling comes from wanting to control everything well.

But your time and energy have limits, so you cannot do everything.

🪶 The Point

When you accept "I cannot do it all," strangely, you feel lighter and can start to move.

This is because when you stop aiming for perfect, you spend less time wondering where to start.
