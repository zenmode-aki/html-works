When you cannot change, you may be protecting a hidden goal 🛡️

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset🫧 Feelings
🌀 Sound Familiar

Do you ever think, "I want to get up early," but cannot change?

🛡️ The Point

If you cannot change, it is not always because your will is weak.

Often, somewhere in your heart, there is a "hidden goal" that you protect by not changing.

🚦 The Reason

This is because changing feels like losing that important thing, so your heart puts on the brakes.

🧺 For Example

For example, some people want to ask others for help, but they do everything alone.

Their hidden goal may be "I want to show that I am useful."

They feel that asking for help breaks that goal, so they keep trying hard alone.

When you find the hidden goal, you blame yourself a little less.
