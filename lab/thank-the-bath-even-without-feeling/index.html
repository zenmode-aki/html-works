Say thank you even without feeling it, and it slowly becomes real 🛁

🧠 Psychology😊 Happiness💡 Tips
💭 Sound Familiar

When people say, "Be thankful," are there days when you cannot really feel thanks?

💬 The Point

On days like that, it is OK to just say "thank you," even if your heart is not in it.

🌱 The Reason

This is because it is said that when you repeat words, your feelings catch up later.

At first, it is fine if it is just a habit of words.

⚡ TRY IT
The thank-you button
Tap things and say thanks. You do not need to feel it.

🛁 For Example

For example, when you take a bath, say quietly, "Thank you, bath."

You can say it to the toilet, to your phone, and to your own feet that walked all day.

🌙 Try This

Tonight, try saying "thank you" 3 times to things around you.

💖 In Short

As you keep saying it, it slowly changes into real thanks.
