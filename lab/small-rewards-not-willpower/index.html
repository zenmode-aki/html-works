Small rewards keep a habit going better than willpower does ☕

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity💭 Mindset
😤 Sound Familiar

Have you ever decided, "I will try hard every day from today," but stopped after about 3 days?

🎁 The Point

Habits last better with "a small reward right after you do it" than with willpower.

🔥 The Reason

Willpower runs out in about 3 days, and if you push too hard, you burn out.

On the other hand, when a reward comes right away, your brain learns, "This feels good."

You naturally want to do things that feel good again.

Instead of pushing hard, make a way to keep going that feels good.
