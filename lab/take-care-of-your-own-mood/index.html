Take care of your own mood, and you can be kind to others 🔋

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings🛋️ Rest
🍙 Sound Familiar

Have you ever said something harsh when you were hungry or sleepy?

😊 The Point

If you want to be kind to people, first take care of your own mood by yourself.

💢 The Reason

This is because when people are in a good mood, they can be kind to others naturally.

On the other hand, in a bad mood, you have no room in your heart, and you tend to be sharp with people.
