When you get nervous, narrate it and hop lightly 🎤

🧠 Psychology💡 Tips🫧 Feelings
💓 Sound Familiar

Before you speak in front of people, does your heart beat fast and do your hands get cold?

🚫 The Wrong Way

It is said that telling yourself "Calm down" at that time makes you more nervous.

This is because the more you think "I must calm down," the more you look at your nervous self.

🎙️ Narrate It

Instead, try narrating in your head, like "My hands are getting cold."

When you look at yourself from the outside, you calm down little by little.

🐧 Hop Hop

Then, try hopping lightly on the spot.

It is said that when you are nervous, your shoulders get tight and your body's center goes up.

When you hop lightly, your center goes down, and your body feels easier.

🎤 In Short

When you get nervous, narrate it and hop lightly.
