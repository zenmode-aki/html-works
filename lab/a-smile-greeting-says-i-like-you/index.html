The way you say hello quietly tells people how you feel about them 💌

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
🌅 Sound Familiar

When you meet a neighbor or a friend in the morning, how do you say "Good morning"?

💌 The Point

The way you say hello is a message by itself: "I like you" or "Not really."

😄 For Example

For example, if you say it first with a smile, it means "I'm glad to see you."

But if you just say it back with a straight face, it can sound like "I'm not interested."
