People naturally cheer for someone who keeps trying hard 📣

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💪 Fitness
🏁 Sound Familiar

At a sports day, have you seen people clap a lot for a child who falls but runs to the end?

Sometimes the clapping is bigger than for the child who came first.

📣 The Point

People naturally cheer for someone who tries hard with all their heart.

⚾ The Reason

This is because people imagine how hard that person worked and feel it as their own.

We also cheer for high school baseball because everyone knows the players practiced a lot.

💧 Not For Pity

But if you show weakness to get pity, the cheering does not last long.

People want to cheer for someone who does not give up, not someone who looks sad.

🐢 Try This

Even on days that do not go well, keep going at your own pace.

🔥 In Short

Keeping on trying will surely move someone's heart.
