Being serious and calm earns respect, but being desperate does not 🔥

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset
🤔 Sound Familiar

Do you ever try hard, but for some reason, people around you do not take you seriously?

It may be because you look "desperate," not "serious."

🔥 The Point

"Desperate" and "serious" look alike, but they are different.

Desperate people are in a hurry and have no room, but serious people are calm.

🧠 The Reason

When you are in a hurry, people worry, "Is this person OK?" and they cannot leave things to you.

Calm people have a quiet power that says, "I will not make a mistake," and people trust them.
