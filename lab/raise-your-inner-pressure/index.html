When stress pushes you, push back from the inside 🎈

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset🫧 Feelings
🛞 Sound Familiar

When you are tired, even a small word can make you feel very down.

It is like a tire with little air that goes flat even on a small bump.

🎈 The Point

Your heart also has "pressure inside," like the air in a balloon.

When stress pushes you, push back from the inside with the same strength.

🌊 The Reason

Deep in the sea, the water pushes hard and crushes a balloon.

But if the air inside is just as strong, the balloon can stay round.

⚡ TRY IT
Keep the balloon round
Dive deeper, and the sea pushes harder. Push back from the inside with strong words.

📣 For Example

For example, on a worried night, say "I'm excited to see what happens!"

Just changing your words makes your feelings face forward a little.

😰"I'm so worried I can't sleep."
😄"I'm excited to see what happens!"
🐧 Try This

Next time you feel worried, say one strong word out loud.

It is OK even if you are only acting, and your legs are shaking.
