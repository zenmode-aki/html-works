Before you scold someone, say the reason first 🔔

🧠 Psychology👪 Family🫧 Feelings💡 Tips
💥 Sound Familiar

Have you ever jumped because someone suddenly got angry at you in a loud voice?

If it happens again and again, you feel nervous all the time just being near that person.

🐭 Old Experiment

It seems there was an old experiment with mice.

The mice that got a shock with no warning were always nervous.

The mice that heard a bell before the shock could stay calm while there was no bell.

😰No warning: always nervous
😌Bell first: calm in between
🔔 The Point

People are the same: if they know what is coming, they can feel safe the rest of the time.

💬 Try This

So when you scold someone, do not yell suddenly, and say the reason first.

For example, say, "Bugs come when dishes pile up, so please wash them today."

🍀 In Short

When they know the reason, they can listen without being nervous.
