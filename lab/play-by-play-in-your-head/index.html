Commentate in your head, and words come out easily 🎙️

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
💬 Sound Familiar

In small talk, do you ever not know what to say?

🎙️ The Point

At such times, I recommend commentating in your head like an announcer.

🧠 The Reason

This is because being bad at talking is often just a lack of practice putting what you see into words.

When you commentate in your head, you get a habit of quickly putting what you see into words.

⚡ TRY IT
🎙️ Live commentary practice

📺 For Example

For example, it is like, "It is a clear autumn day today!" "A person on a bicycle is in a hurry!"

You do not need to say it out loud, so you can do it on the train or at a cafe.

🚃 Try This

First, today, try to commentate on 3 things you see, in your head.

⏳ In Short

If you keep it up for about 2 weeks, words come out more easily in small talk too.
