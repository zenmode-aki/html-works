When a friend shares a worry, listen with empathy first, then advice 💗

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🫧 Feelings
🤔 Sound Familiar

When a friend tells you about a worry, do you ever want to give an answer right away?

But it seems people who talk about a worry usually have the answer already.

💗 The Point

When you listen to a worry, I recommend this order: "empathy → OK → advice."

🫧 The Reason

This is because what they really want is to let out their feelings and feel better.

When their feelings are accepted first, people feel safe and can find the answer by themselves.

💬 For Example

For example, try saying "That was hard," not "That happens a lot."

Next, say "It is OK to feel that way," and give just a little advice at the end.
