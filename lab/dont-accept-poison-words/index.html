You do not have to accept poison words from other people 🛡️

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💭 Mindset
🌧️ Sound Familiar

Has someone ever said something mean to you, and you worried about it for days?

🍲 The Point

If a meal has poison in it, you go home without eating it, right?

Words are the same, and you do not have to accept poison words.

😵Take it
😌Don't take it
🛡️ The Reason

This is because you can choose whether to accept the words or not.

If you do not accept them, the words stay with the person who said them.

⚡ TRY IT

Here they come… Get ready! You are so slow! That is a weird idea. Your bag is not cute. Nobody asked you. You can't do it. Boring! Are you serious? What a silly plan. …I give up. You don't take anything!
