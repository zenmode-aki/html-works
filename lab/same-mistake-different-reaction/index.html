People who chat every day get forgiven for the same mistake ⏰

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😅 Mistakes
⚖️ Same Mistake

It seems people react differently to the same mistake, depending on whether you usually chat.

🥺 They Worry

For example, when you are late, if you often talk, people worry: "Did something happen? Are you OK?"

😤 So Lazy

If you do not talk much, people think, "So lazy!"
