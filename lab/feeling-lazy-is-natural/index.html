You feel "what a bother" because your brain wants to save energy 🦁

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset🛋️ Rest
🧹 Sound Familiar

On a day off, do you ever think "I will clean," but your body does not move easily?

😮‍💨 The Point

Feeling "what a bother" is not because you are lazy; it is natural.

🧠 Brain Logic

This is because the brain is made to save as much energy as it can.

🔋 Save energy!→🛋️ Lazy = good
🦁 The Zoo Lion

It is said that lions at the zoo also lie down most of the time, except at feeding time.

When there is no reason to move, resting is a "win" for the body.

🧠 Your brain is the same. It wants to save energy first.
⚙️ Use Systems

So moving with "systems" works better than waiting for motivation.

For example, decide the order: "After breakfast, I wash the dishes right away."

When the order is decided, you do not need to think about it each time.

🐧 In Short

Do not blame yourself for feeling "what a bother," and leave it to your systems.
