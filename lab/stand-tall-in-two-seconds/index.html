Stand tall in 2 seconds: breathe in, pull in your chin, and smile 🐧

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💡 Tips
🚪 Sound Familiar

In a new place or a crowded place, does your back get round before you notice?

⏱️ The Point

You can make a confident pose in just 2 seconds.

🌬️ Three Steps

First, take a big breath in.

When you breathe in, your chest opens naturally, and your back gets straight.

Next, pull in your chin a little, and last, smile a little.

Without a smile, you look bossy, not confident, so this part is important.

😤No smile = bossy
😊Smile = confident
