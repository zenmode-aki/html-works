If you stop thinking things are normal, you say thank you more 💛

🧠 Psychology😊 Happiness💭 Mindset
🤔 Sound Familiar

Do you ever feel that you say "thank you" less these days?

🪤 The Point

We say "thank you" less because we think the things around us are "normal."

🔁 The Reason

People get used to things that happen every day, and stop noticing them.

But behind that "normal," there is always someone's effort.

🚃 For Example

For example, a train comes on time because the driver gets ready from early in the morning.

The driver must also have days when they are sleepy or do not feel well.

⚡ TRY IT
Flip the "normal" things
Tap a card to see the other side.
💛 Thank-yous found 0/6
Each "normal" thing has a person behind it.
🌏The "normal" world is full of hidden effort. Thank you, everyone!
🍙 Try This

When you buy a rice ball at a convenience store, try to imagine the people behind it a little.

Then you can naturally say "thank you."

💛 In Short

Even things that look normal are thanks to someone.
