Decide "if this, then that," and habits are easier to keep 🚦

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity💡 Tips
🗓️ Sound Familiar

Do you ever decide, "I will exercise every day," and then stop after 3 days?

📝 The Point

At times like that, decide, "If X happens, I do Y," and write it on paper.

People say this makes habits easier to keep.

🚦 Brain Rules

This is because the brain is good at moving with "if" rules, like "If the light is red, stop."

With a clear "trigger," you can act without stopping to think.

🔴 If red→🛑 stop
😤 For Example

For example, decide, "If I feel irritated, I take 3 deep breaths."

Small things like "If I wake up, I drink a glass of water right away" are fine too.
