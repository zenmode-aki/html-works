From the universe, you are tiny, and that makes you free 🌌

📖 Books💭 Mindset🫧 Feelings
😣 Sound Familiar

Do you ever remember a small mistake again and again?

🔭 The Point

At those times, try to imagine in your heart a view of yourself from very far away.

🏔️ The Reason

When you see a big view, like heavy snow or a wide sea, your stress can suddenly get lighter.

This is because you remember that you are a tiny dot in a big world.

Oh. It's fine. 🌌 From here, the worry is a tiny dot.
💬 For Example

For example, say you sent a message with a typo.

In your room it is a big problem, but if you zoom out to the city, the Earth, and the universe, you can almost not see it.

🕊️ Free To Try

If you are tiny, a mistake is not the end of the world.

When you can think so, you relax, and you can try things easily again.

🌌 In Short

Because you are tiny, you can be free.
