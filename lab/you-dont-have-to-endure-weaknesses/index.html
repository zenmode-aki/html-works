You do not have to endure your weak points; shine somewhere else 🎛️

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset🏫 School
🥵 Sound Familiar

Do you think, "Even if I am bad at it, I should endure it and try hard"?

🗑️ The Point

I think you do not have to force yourself to keep doing things you are bad at.

🔦 The Reason

If you keep enduring things you are bad at, your heart gets worn out.

Instead, using your strengths in a place where your weak points do not show is much better.

⚡ TRY IT
On stage or backstage?

🎛️ Go Backstage

🎭 School Play
