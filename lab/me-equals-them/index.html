Take care of yourself as much as you take care of others ⚖️

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🛋️ Rest
😮‍💨 Sound Familiar

Do you ever care about others too much and always feel tired?

You cannot say no to invitations, or you feel bad after a free taste and end up buying the food.

⚖️ The Point

You can take care of yourself as much as you take care of others.

The goal is "me = others," which means both have the same weight.

🔋 The Reason

This is because if you only do things for others, your energy runs out, and you have no power left to be kind.

A person with energy can be kinder to others than a tired person.

⚡ TRY IT
The "me = others" seesaw
🍜🛁🎧📚🍰☕
🐧 Me
🎁☂️💐📦🍱🧹
🐧🐧 Others
🔋My energy:50%
Add things to each side. Can you keep it balanced?
😵 Tilted! You give too much, and your energy is low.
🤔 Tilted the other way. Think of others, too!
⚖️ Almost! Just a little more.
🟢 Balanced! Me = others. Calm and steady.
🛁 Try This

For example, to an invitation you do not want, try answering, "I will pass this time. Thank you."

With the free time, take a warm bath or eat something you like.

💚 You Count Too

Taking care of yourself is not selfish.
