Argue back at your gloomy thought as if you were another person 📓

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💡 Tips
🌧️ Sound Familiar

After a small mistake, do you ever think, "I am always no good"?

If you leave a dark thought alone, it gets bigger and bigger.

📓 The Point

At times like that, write the dark thought in a notebook, and argue back at it as if you were another person.

💡 The Reason

This is because you are often strict with yourself, but you can talk back kindly for a friend.

It is said that if you practice arguing back again and again, you get better at positive thinking.

✍️ Three Steps

First, write what happened and the thought that came to you then.

Next, pretend to be a friend, and argue back at that thought with all your power.

Last, write how your mood changed.

⚡ TRY IT
The 3-step notebook
