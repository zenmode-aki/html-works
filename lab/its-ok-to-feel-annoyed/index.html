It is OK to feel annoyed, because we are only human 💛

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💭 Mindset
🚫 Don't Feel It

The more you think "Getting angry is bad," the weaker your heart gets.

📱 No Reply

If your message is read with no reply and you think "Don't worry," you worry more.

🌙 Sleep Now

It is like how "I must sleep now!" makes you more awake.
