When you are not sure, choose by asking "Is it cool?" 😎

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset😊 Happiness
📏 Sound Familiar

When we choose something, we use different "rulers."

They are "gain or loss," "right or wrong," and "cool or not."

💰Gain?⚖️Right?😎Cool?
😤 Gain And Rules

When you think by gain or loss, you get angry easily: "But I'm the one paying!"

When you think by right or wrong, you get stressed easily: "They should…!"

😎 The Point

It is said that choosing by "Is it cool?" keeps you in the best mood.

This is because you can decide cool actions by yourself, no matter what others do.

🥫 For Example

For example, letting others go first, or picking up an empty can on the street.

💭 Try This

Next time you are not sure, ask yourself, "Which one is cool?"

🐧 In Short

When you choose small cool things, your mood gets lighter too.
