If someone dislikes you, it is often just a matter of their taste 🫑

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset🫧 Feelings
🌧️ Sound Familiar

Have you ever worried all day because you thought someone disliked you?

🎮 The Point

But being liked by everyone is impossible from the start.

🎨 The Reason

This is because liking or disliking often depends on the other person's taste more than on whether you are good or bad.

If you are quiet, some people say "too quiet," and if you are bright, some people say "too loud."

⚡ TRY IT
The green pepper's tasting day
5 tasters are coming. The pepper stays a pepper.
🍽️
Who will taste first? "Too bitter!" 😖 "I like carrots more." 🥕 "I don't know why, but… no." "Can you be sweeter, please?" 🍬 "I love the bitter part!!" 💚
🫑💚
🫑 "I'm a pepper. Nice to meet you." 🫑 "That's OK. I'm still a pepper." 🫑 "Sorry, I can't be candy. I'm a pepper." 🫑 "Thank you! I'm happy I stayed a pepper."
🐱🐰🐻🐶🐧
0🫑 Times it changed itself
0💚 Fans
🎉 It stayed itself, and a fan came! The 4 "no"s were just their taste.
🫑 For Example

For example, even if a child does not like green peppers, the green pepper is not bad.

There are also people who say, "I love this bitter taste," about the same green pepper.

💭 Try This

So when you feel someone dislikes you, try thinking, "Our tastes did not match."

You do not have to change yourself by force for that.

💚 In Short

Someone who likes you just as you are will surely come.
