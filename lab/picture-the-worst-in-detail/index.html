Imagine the worst in detail, and the worry gets smaller 🔦

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💭 Mindset
😱 Sound Familiar

Do you ever have a worry like "What if I fail the exam?" that does not leave your head?

🔍 The Point

At such times, please try to imagine the worst thing in detail, on purpose.

☁️ The Reason

This is because a worry looks bigger when it is blurry.

When you think in detail, you see, "Even the worst is only this much," and you feel a little safer.

☁️Blurry = big
🔍Clear = small
⚡ TRY IT
Shrink the fog monster
😱 What if I fail the exam?!
☁️ Fog: 100%
  • 📅 Next month. I can try again.
  • 🐧 A friend who passed it, and a good book.
  • 😂 "I failed once!" It is a funny story now.
Tap the questions. Look at the worst in detail.
😌 Oh… even the worst is only this much.
📝 For Example

For example, for an exam, you think in order: "When is the next exam?" "Who can I ask?" "How will it be in 3 years?"

Each time 1 answer comes, the worry gets much smaller.

✏️ Try This

When you feel worried, please try writing 3 "things to do in the worst case" on paper.

😌 In Short

A worry you can see clearly is not so scary anymore.
