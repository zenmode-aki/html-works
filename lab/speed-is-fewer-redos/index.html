Speed comes from fewer redos, not from fast hands 🏁

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity💡 Tips
👟 The Habit

When I want to finish fast, I try to move my hands fast.

🔑 The Real Key

But it seems that speed is decided by "fewer redos."

They say fast hands help only about 10%.

✋ Fast hands10%
↺ Fewer redos90%
🐌 Why So Slow

It gets slow because remaking, checking, and fixing mistakes pile up.
