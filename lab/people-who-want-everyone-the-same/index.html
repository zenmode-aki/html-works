Slowly step away from people who want everyone to be the same 🌈

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💭 Mindset
😮‍💨 Sound Familiar

With friends, has someone said "This is normal, right?" and made it hard to breathe?

🧱 Same As Me

Some people have a very strong feeling: "I want everyone to think the same as me."

When they see a different idea, they get worried and often say strong words.

🌈 Big Heart

But a person with a calm heart hears a different opinion and says, "That is one way to think, too."

For example, friends who say "That is nice, too" about different weekend plans are easy to be with.

🚪 Step Away

If you feel bad with such a person, you do not need to force yourself to match them.

You do not need to fight; you can see them a little less and slowly step away.

🐧 In Short

Please take care of the places where you can breathe easily.
