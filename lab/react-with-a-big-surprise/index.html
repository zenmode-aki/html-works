A talk gets exciting when you react with surprise 🎉

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
🍃 Sound Familiar

When a friend tells you something happily, do you ever only answer "Oh"?

If you only say "Oh" and "I see" in a flat voice, the speaker feels a little lonely.

😲 The Point

The trick to make a talk exciting is to show clear surprise.

An easy way to remember it is "a-i-u-e-o."

"Ah!" "Nice!" "Hmmm, I get it!" "Eh?! Wow!" "Oh, good for you!"

あAh! いNice! うHmmm! えEh?! おOh!
🔥 The Reason

This is because when you are surprised, the speaker is happy that they told the story.

When they feel happy, they want to talk more, and the talk goes on naturally.

⚡ TRY IT
Surprise combo
Your friend tells a story. React with a-i-u-e-o and keep the combo going!
🐧💭
Press start. I have a story for you! "I went to the aquarium yesterday." "A penguin walked right up to me!" "It looked at my ice cream…" "…and it took 1 lick!" "Then the staff gave me a new one." "And it was a double scoop!" "…Well, anyway. Never mind." "Thanks for listening! That was so fun to tell!"
🔥 Talk heat
0COMBO
Tip: try not to use the gray "I see." button.
Keep reacting! Your friend is getting excited.
🍃 The talk stopped. A flat "I see." made it cold.
🔥 6 COMBO! Your friend told you the whole story.
🏅 You used all of a-i-u-e-o!
🧳 For Example

For example, when a friend tells you about a trip, answer "Eh?! That happened?"

🐧 Try This

When you talk with someone today, try saying one of "a-i-u-e-o" a little louder.

Your surprise alone gives the speaker energy.
