Life never gets fully sorted, and that is OK 🌻

📖 Books💭 Mindset😊 Happiness
🚪 Sound Familiar

Do you ever think your real life will start "when my room is clean" or "when I pass the test"?

🌈 The Point

But the day when everything is sorted never comes.

And that is OK.

🔁 The Reason

This is because when you finish one thing, the next task or worry soon comes.

Then it is better to enjoy each day now, even if things are not sorted.
