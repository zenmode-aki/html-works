When you stop trying to look nice, you can enjoy the people around you 😆

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🫧 Feelings
🪞 Sound Familiar

At a get-together with friends, when you want them to think you are nice, don't you feel a little nervous?

"Is my hair OK?" "I must say something funny!" — your head gets very busy.

😆 The Point

If you just think, "Laugh if you want," you can enjoy the time more.

💭 The Reason

This is because when you want people to like you, your mind looks only at yourself.

When you only look at yourself, you miss the faces of the people in front of you.

☕ For Example

For example, listen to your friend's story and just say, "Oh, and then?"

🌿 Try This

Try to look at the other person's story more than at how you look.

Then your shoulders relax, and the talk goes on naturally.

🎉 In Short

When you take your eyes off yourself, you start to see the fun around you.
