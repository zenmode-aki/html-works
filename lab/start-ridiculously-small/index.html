Start so small that your brain does not notice 🤏

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity🚶 Walks
🔥 Sound Familiar

Have you ever decided, "I will run 1 hour every day from tomorrow," but stopped after 3 days?

Hard workers often make their first goal too big.

🐜 The Point

The trick is to start a new habit so small that your brain does not notice.

🧠 The Reason

It is said that the brain feels a big change is "dangerous" and tries to go back.

If the change is small, your brain does not care much, so it is easier to keep going.

👟 For Example

For example, for exercise, start by walking just 5 minutes every day.

On hard days, you can just put on your shoes and go outside.

📅 Try This

After 2 weeks, try adding a little more time.

🌱 In Short

A step that feels too small keeps going the longest.
