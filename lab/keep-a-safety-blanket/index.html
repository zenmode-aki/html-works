Keep 1 safety blanket in your heart, and hard days get a little easier 🧸

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💡 Tips
🌧️ Sound Familiar

When you feel down, do you ever find that nothing makes you feel better?

At times like that, little kids hold their favorite blanket tight.

🧸 The Point

It is good for adults to keep 1 "safety blanket" in their heart, too.

💛 The Reason

This is because if something calms you just by thinking of it, your heart has a place to go back to.

Even on a stormy day, when you remember it, your heart gets a little warmer.

🔑 For Example

For example, a dear person's smile, an old pet, your hometown view, or your usual food.
