Keep many small ways to make yourself happy 📒

🧠 Psychology😊 Happiness🛋️ Rest💡 Tips
🔋 Sound Familiar

On a low day, do you ever forget, "What can I do to feel better again?"

🎁 The Point

I recommend collecting many ways to make yourself happy on your good days.

This is because when you feel down, your head does not work well, and good ideas do not come easily.

If you have only 1 way, you get stuck on days when you cannot do it.

🔢 How Many

There is also an idea to have at least 15, 25 if you can, and someday 100.

15at least
25best
100final goal
🕯️ For Example

For example, a bath with candles, time to look out of a train window, or a sunset.
