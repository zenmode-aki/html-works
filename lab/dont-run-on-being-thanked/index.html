If being thanked is your only fuel, you will run out ⛽

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset😊 Happiness
💐 Sound Familiar

Have you ever tried hard for someone, but felt sad because nobody said "thank you"?

Of course, it is nice to hear "thank you."

⛽ The Point

But if it is your only fuel for energy, it soon runs out.

🎢 The Reason

This is because whether people thank you depends on their mood and how busy they are.

When days with no words go on, even your energy disappears.

⚡ TRY IT
5 days with 2 fuel tanks
Tap the days in order, Monday to Friday. Some days, nobody says thank you.
⛽ Fuel: being thanked
🐧
Running!🪫 Out of energy…
🌱 Fuel: doing what I believe
🐧
It fills up again by itself.
🐧 Thanks is a nice bonus. But your own fuel keeps you going every day.
🧽 For Example

For example, when you clean a shared kitchen, you think, "I do it because I like a clean kitchen."

When you do it for a reason you believe in, you can try again the next day, even if nobody notices.

🌱 Try This

Today, think about "Why do I do this?" in your own words.

🐧 In Short

"Thank you" is a nice bonus, and the fuel is inside you.
