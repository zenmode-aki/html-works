Pay first, and you will not quit before it gets fun 🎟️

🧠 Psychology💡 Tips📚 Learning
🎸 Sound Familiar

Have you ever started a new hobby and quit after a few days?

This is because at first, you are not good yet, and it is not much fun.

⏳ Fun Comes Later

But many things finally become fun after you keep going for a while.

🎟️ The Point

So paying for a class or tools first is one good way.

The feeling "I paid, so quitting is a waste" gives you the power to keep going.

⚡ TRY IT

⭐ Day 7: it got fun! "What a waste!" kept the penguin going.
🗓️ For Example

For example, you pay for 1 month of ukulele class first.

Even on days you do not want to go, you can think, "It is a waste, so I will go."

👛 Not Too Much

But please pay only an amount that is OK to lose.

🐧 In Short

Let money help you a little in the first days, until it gets fun.
