Step back from people with minus words, and get close to plus words ☀️

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😊 Happiness
😩 Sound Familiar

Is there someone who somehow makes you tired when you are with them?

Near someone who always says "What a pain" or "It is no use," your mood slowly gets dark.

🌧️ The Reason

This is because words also pass to the mood of the people who hear them.

☀️ The Point

So step back just a little from people with many minus words.

And get close to people who often say "I am happy," "This is fun," and "Thank you."

⚡ TRY IT
Catch only the plus words!
☀️ ☁️ ☁️ ☁️ 🐧
☀️ 0 ☁️ 0
Tap only the plus words. Let the minus words fall away.
☀️ Sunny heart! Only plus words came close.
⛅ Nice! Mostly plus words.
🌧 Cloudy… Minus words came too close.
🍜 For Example

For example, cup noodles with a good friend can taste better than an expensive dinner.

Who you spend time with changes how fun the same time is.

🍽️😑Fancy dinner
🍜😆Cup noodles
🎁 But Remember

But please do not cut ties with people who helped you when you were in trouble.

🐧 In Short

Let us fill the space around us with plus words.
