Say "yes" in 0.1 seconds: speed matters more than a loud voice ⚡

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
⏱️ The Rule

It seems you should say "yes" within 0.1 seconds when someone asks you something.

😶 The Pause

If there is a strange pause like "...uh, yes," people may think, "Does this person really want to do it?"

🏎️ Speed Wins

Speed is more important than how loud your voice is.
