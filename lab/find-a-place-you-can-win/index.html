Stop the fight you cannot win, and find your own sea 🌊

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset😊 Happiness
😮‍💨 Sound Familiar

Do you ever try hard at the same thing as everyone, but cannot win and get tired?

🏳️ Stop Fighting

At those times, stopping a fight you cannot win is one good way.

This is because when you stop, you begin to see places where you can make the rules or win.

🐆 On Land

On land, a penguin cannot beat a cheetah.

But in the sea, a penguin swims surprisingly fast.

🌊 Your Own Sea

Finding a place you are good at is much more fun than trying hard in a place you are bad at.

✏️ For Example

For example, if speaking in front of many people is hard for you, you can tell things in writing.

Write down 3 things that are a little easier for you than for others.

🐧 More Fun

When you find your own sea, life becomes more fun.
