Stop at your set time, even when it is going well 🪑

📖 Books✅ Productivity📚 Learning
🌙 Sound Familiar

Do you ever enjoy a diary or drawing so much that you go on late at night?

But the next day, you are tired and take a day off.

⏱️ The Point

Stop at your set time, even when it is going well.

🔥 The Reason

This is because "I want more" is sometimes just a hurry to finish fast.

If you do too much in one day, you have no energy the next day.

📅 Big days made skip days. Stopping on time lets you come back.
⏰ For Example

For example, at first decide on 10 to 15 minutes, and stand up when the timer rings.

If you stop a little early, you will want to do it again the next day.

🌱 Patience Muscle

Then you grow a "patience muscle" to come back every day.

🐧 In Short

To keep going for a long time, stop on time, especially on good days.
