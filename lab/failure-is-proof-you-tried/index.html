A failure is proof that you tried without running away ⛸️

🧠 Psychology😅 Mistakes💭 Mindset
😣 Sound Familiar

When you try something and fail, do you ever feel embarrassed and sad?

🏅 The Point

But a failure is proof that you tried without running away.

So I think you can be a little proud of it.

😂 The Reason

This is because if you do not try, you do not fail, but you also learn nothing.

A failure from real effort becomes a funny story later, and it leaves no regret.

⚖️ Balance
Tap "Try!" It is OK to fall.
💥 You fell! 🏅 +1 "I tried" medal.
💥 Fell again, but a little farther! Learned: bend your knees.
💥 Learned: look ahead, not down.
💥 Learned: arms out for balance.
🏆 You glide! Every fall taught you something.
🙈 No falls. But no medals, and nothing learned.
⛸️ For Example

For example, when you just start skating, you fall many times.

But each time you fall, you slowly learn tips, like bending your knees and looking ahead.

📝 Try This

On a day you fail, think about just 1 thing: "What did I learn today?"

🏆 In Short

The number of times you fell is the number of times you tried.
