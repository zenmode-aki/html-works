Successful givers are kind, but not too kind to people who only take 🎁

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💭 Mindset
😮‍💨 Sound Familiar

Do you often feel tired because you can't say no to people?

The more you want to be a "nice person," the more your energy goes down.

🎁 The Point

Successful "givers" are not kind to everyone.

They are not too kind to people who only take from them.

🔋 The Reason

This is because the energy you can use for others is limited.

If you use it on takers, nothing is left for the people who really matter.

💸 For Example

For example, to someone who always borrows money and never pays back, you can say, "Not this time."

Then you can use that time and money for a friend who always helps you.

💛 Try This

When someone asks you for something, think once, "Does this person care about me, too?"

You can save your kindness for the people who matter.
