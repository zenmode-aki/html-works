3 or 4 hours of real focus a day is enough ⏳

📖 Books✅ Productivity🛋️ Rest
🌙 Sound Familiar

On a day off, have you ever decided, "I will work hard all day today"?

But by evening, your head is foggy, and often you have not done much.

⏳ The Point

It is said that we can really focus for about 3 to 4 hours a day.

🔋 The Reason

This is because focus goes down when you use it, like a phone battery.

After the battery runs out, you often just read the same page again and again.

⚡ TRY IT
Protect just 4 hours
Tap your best time of the day.
🏯 Focus time🌀 Free time
🐧 When are you full of energy? The rest is free? Fine! I will focus all 12 hours!! …my head stopped working.
🏯 4 hours protected. The rest can be free.
😵 After 4 hours, the focus ran out anyway.
🏯 That Is Enough

So if you use those 3 to 4 hours well, that is enough for one day.

☀️ Try This

For example, make 3 clear-headed morning hours your "focus time."

In the rest of the time, you can do the laundry or just relax.

🐧 In Short

Protecting a short time moves you forward more than trying hard for a long time.
