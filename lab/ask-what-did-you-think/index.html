Even for a story you know, ask "And what did you think?" 💭

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🫧 Feelings
😔 Sound Familiar

When a friend starts a story you heard before, do you ever say, "I know that story"?

When you say that, they feel a little lonely.

💭 The Point

Even for a story you know, try asking, "And what did you think?"

🔦 The Reason

This is because you know what happened, but you have not heard how they felt yet.

Feelings come out new each time people talk.

⚾ For Example

For example, if a friend tells again about a baseball game in Korea, ask, "How did you feel then?"

Then a story you did not know comes out, like "The cheering gave me goosebumps."

🎁 Try This

Even the same story is always new when you ask about feelings.
