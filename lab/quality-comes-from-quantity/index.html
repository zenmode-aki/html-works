If you stop judging and make a lot, good ideas come out 💡

📖 Books✅ Productivity💡 Tips
📓 Sound Familiar

When you try to write something in a notebook, do you ever stop because you think, "I have to write something good"?

💡 The Point

If you want to make good things, first try to make a lot without judging.

🧐 The Reason

This is because if you think "Is this good?" about each one, your hand stops, and you make almost nothing.

When you make a lot, sometimes a good one is mixed in.

📏 For Example

For example, you decide only "I will write 3 pages in a notebook every morning," and write without caring about the content.
