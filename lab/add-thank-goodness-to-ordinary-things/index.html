Add "thank goodness" to ordinary things, and you see what you have 💛

🧠 Psychology😊 Happiness💭 Mindset
🔍 Sound Familiar

Do you ever see a friend's trip photos on social media and somehow feel down?

When you compare yourself with others, you only look at what you do not have.

💛 The Point

At times like that, try adding "thank goodness" to the "normal" things around you.

💡 The Reason

This is because when you feel thankful, you look at what you already have, not what you don't have.

Even on the same days, when you look at different things, your feelings change too.

🚰 For Example

For example, it is a "thank goodness" thing that clean water comes out when you turn on the tap.

A convenience store open at midnight and a train on time are the same.
