Ask for small favors, and people come to like you more 📕

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends📖 Books
🤔 Sound Familiar

Do you ever feel bad about asking people for something, because it seems like a bother?

But a small favor can bring you closer to the other person.

🌱 The Point

It is said that people care about those who make them feel "I was useful."

💡 The Reason

This is because when you help someone, the feeling "I did this for them" can easily turn into liking them.

And when they get a polite thank-you, they feel happy: "I'm glad I was useful."

🔌 For Example

For example, ask, "Can I borrow your charger?" and give it back with "Thanks, that helped!"

A small favor like that is enough.
