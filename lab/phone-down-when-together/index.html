When you are with a friend, put your phone face down 📱

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
☕ Sound Familiar

Do you ever look at your phone when you are at a cafe with a friend?

🙃 The Point

When you are together, I recommend putting your phone face down.

🤫 The Reason

It is said that if you look at your phone while they are talking, they feel like "someone is whispering secrets next to me."

If this goes on, little by little they feel, "Maybe this person is not interested in my story."

⚡ TRY IT

🍰 For Example

For example, when your friend is happily talking about a cake, do not look at your phone right away, even if it buzzes.

If it is not urgent, you can look at it later.

📱 Try This

Next time you meet a friend, first put your phone face down when you sit.

💞 In Short

Just that tells them, "I am listening to you."
