The real skill is to stop and rest, even when work is not finished 🔔

📖 Books🛋️ Rest💭 Mindset
🧺 Sound Familiar

When your tasks are not finished, do you feel that resting is bad?

🛑 The Point

The important thing is not the power to try harder.

It is the power to stop at a set time and rest, even when you are not finished.

♾️ The Reason

This is because tasks never go away, however hard you try.

⛪ For Example

It is said that at one monastery long ago, work time was only 3 hours a day.

When someone asked a monk, "What if the work is not finished?", he answered, "Never mind."

🔔 Try This

Today, when the time you set comes, try to stop, even in the middle.

The rest of the tasks will still be there tomorrow.

🍵 In Short

Being able to stop and rest is also an important power.
