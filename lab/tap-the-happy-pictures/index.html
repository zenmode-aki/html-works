The habit of seeing bad things first can change with practice 🎈

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset😊 Happiness
🌧️ Sound Familiar

When you look back on your day, do you remember only the bad things?

🎯 The Point

The habit of seeing bad things first can change if you practice looking for good things.

🔍 The Reason

This is because our eyes naturally find the things we care about.

If you look only at bad things, the world starts to look more dangerous than it really is.

On the other hand, when you get used to looking for good things, the same day looks brighter.
