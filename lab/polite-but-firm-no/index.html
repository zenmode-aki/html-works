Be friendly, but say no politely to things you cannot do 🍜

🧠 Psychology💡 Tips💭 Mindset
🍜 Nice Shop

A friendly ramen shop gets many regular customers.

📢 Hard Customers

But difficult customers also come: "The music is too loud!" "Bring it to my house!"

📈 It Grows

If you say yes to everything, the requests get bigger and bigger.

⚡ TRY IT
Run a friendly ramen shop!
🍜♨️
🐧
Welcome! What can I get you? Some water, please. Extra green onions, please! Can I have a spoon? Less salty, please. Bring it to my house! Turn the music off. Now! Make it free for me! Open at 3 a.m. just for me!
📈 Even bigger!
😋 Happy customer! 👏 Polite and firm! 📈 Now they want even more… 💔 Oh no, that was a normal request.
⭐ 0 / 8
📈 Demands
Say yes to normal requests. Say no politely to impossible ones.
🏅 Friendly and firm! Your regulars love this shop.
🌀 Chaos! The requests got bigger and bigger.
🙂 Not bad! Be friendly, and say no to the impossible.
✋ Polite No

So be friendly, and say no politely to things you cannot do: "I'm sorry, but I can't do that."

🧰 A Tool

Good manners are a tool for dealing with difficult people.
