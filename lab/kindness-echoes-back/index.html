What you do for others comes back to you like an echo ⛰️

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😊 Happiness
❄️ Sound Familiar

When someone is cold to you, you sometimes want to be cold back, right?

On the other hand, when someone is kind to you, you naturally want to be kind back.

✨ The Point

So I recommend doing for others first what you want them to do for you.

⛰️ The Reason

This is because people want to give back the same thing they got.

It is like when you say "Yoo-hoo" to a mountain and "Yoo-hoo" comes back.

😊 For Example

For example, people who say hello with a smile often get a smile back.

People who talk to others get talked to, and people who help get helped.

🎁 Try This

Today, try doing just 1 thing for someone that you want others to do for you.

💛 In Short

What you do for others comes back to you like an echo.
