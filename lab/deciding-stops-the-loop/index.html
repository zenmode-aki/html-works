When you decide, the endless meeting in your head stops 💡

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💡 Tips
🧠 Sound Familiar

When you cannot decide, do you ever feel a meeting goes on and on in your head?

"What should I do?" "But…" "Maybe…" and it never ends.

🤔 What should I do?😟 But…🙄 Maybe…
✅ The Point

At those times, the best thing is to just decide.

🔁 The Reason

This is because until you decide, your head keeps thinking about the same problem.

When you decide, you get a reason to stop thinking, and your head becomes quiet.
