Things with a name are treated with more care 🏷️

🧠 Psychology💡 Tips🏫 School
🪴 Sound Familiar

Do you ever forget to water the plant in your room?

🏷️ Names Matter

It is said that when you give a thing a name, it becomes easier to take care of it.

This is because with a name, it feels like a friend, not just a "thing."

🏫 The Schoolyard

For example, there is a story about a ball left out in a schoolyard.

When someone named the ball "Pyonta," everyone started to put it away.
