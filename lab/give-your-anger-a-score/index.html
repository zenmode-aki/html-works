Give your anger a score, and it stops growing bigger 🌡️

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💡 Tips
💢 Sound Familiar

Do you ever get annoyed by a small thing, and then find your anger has grown big?

🌡️ Score It

At those times, try giving that anger a score.

Make your angriest time in life 10 points, and think how many points this is.

🏷️ Decide First

For example, if a friend is 10 minutes late, decide first: "This is 3 points."

📈 It Grows

If you do not decide, 3 points becomes 5, then 6, and keeps going up.

This is because you play the same scene again and again in your head.

🧊 It Stops

If you decide the score first, it does not go up easily.

While you think about the score, you can become a little calm.

🐧 Try This

Next time you get annoyed, first ask yourself, "How many points is this?"
