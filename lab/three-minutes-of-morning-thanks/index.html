Spend 3 minutes every morning writing your thanks in detail 🌅

🧠 Psychology😊 Happiness💡 Tips
📓 Sound Familiar

You hear "Writing your thanks is good" and start, but do you find it hard to keep going?

⏰ 3 Minutes

The trick to keep going is to write for just 3 minutes at the same time every morning.

This is because when you fix the time, your hand moves without thinking, like brushing your teeth.

🔍 In Detail

One more point is to write in detail.

🍵 For Example

For example, "Someone made me tea on a cold morning" shows the good thing more clearly than "Thanks to my family."

When you write in detail, you can remember even the warm feeling of that moment again.
