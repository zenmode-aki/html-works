When you are not sure, choose the way that lets you like yourself 💛

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset🫧 Feelings
🤔 The Question

When you are not sure, it seems good to choose by asking, "Which one lets me like myself?"

🍟 Dropped Fry

For example, when you drop a French fry on the floor.

Rather than acting like you did not see it, picking it up yourself lets you like yourself a little more.
