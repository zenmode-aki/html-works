Irritation spreads, so step away from it a little 🌀

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings🤝 Friends
🚪 Sound Familiar

When the person next to you sighs or shuts a door hard, do you start to feel uneasy too?

🌀 The Point

It is said that irritation spreads to the people around you, like a cold.

🪞 The Reason

This is because people copy the faces and voices of people near them without noticing.

When someone at home is irritated, you can also rest in another room for a while.
