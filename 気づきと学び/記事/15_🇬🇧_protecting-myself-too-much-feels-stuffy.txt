When I try too hard to protect myself, it feels stuffy 🐧

Somehow, when I try too hard to protect myself, I feel more stifled instead.

For example, even at work, when I slack off, I feel a bit guilty, and I get into an unpleasant mood. Compared to that, I get busier, and yes I think I get tired, but going and picking up work myself is, in the end, more fun.

Maybe keeping on thinking like this, always, about what I can do now for the people around me, is the trick to living happily and with energy.

Maybe the more energy and stamina a person has, the more that person is always trying hard to be useful to someone.
