Relationships with people 🐧

When someone gets angry at you, do not focus on yourself, focus on the other person.

Maybe they are not feeling well.

Maybe something is not going well at home, you think.

Because if you think things like "someone like me," or "why did I make a mistake like this," you get more and more down.

You must never blame yourself.

You must not reflect on yourself.

Because if you blame yourself you get down, and after that you cannot take constructive action, so it creates even more of a negative loop.
