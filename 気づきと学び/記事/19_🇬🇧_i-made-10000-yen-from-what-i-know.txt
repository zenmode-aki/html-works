For the first time in my life, I got a reward of 10,000 yen for my own knowledge 🐧

My mother's friend runs a business and said they were interested in AI, so we decided to meet at Starbucks.

There, for about 2 hours, I showed how I usually use AI at work.

I did nothing difficult.

I showed Claude, explained what "the cloud" is, and showed that AI can work with Gmail, Google Drive, Forms, and Spreadsheets.

For me, it was something I already knew. But explaining it was honestly fun.

They even bought me coffee and dinner, so that alone made me grateful enough.

After dinner, that person suddenly gave me 10,000 yen. I was surprised.

It was the first time in my life that I got money not because I worked a shift, but because what I know was valuable to someone.

10,000 yen will not change my life. But this experience might.
