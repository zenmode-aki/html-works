Today, I made 10,000 yen in a way I had never experienced before 🐧

My mother's friend runs his own business, and he was curious about AI. So we met at Starbucks, and I spent about two hours showing him how I use AI in everyday work.

Nothing complicated.

I showed him Claude, explained what "the cloud" is, and showed him how AI can work with Gmail, Google Drive, Forms, and Spreadsheets.

To me, this was just stuff I already knew. I genuinely enjoyed explaining it.

He even paid for my coffee and dinner, so I already felt grateful.

Then, after dinner, he suddenly gave me 10,000 yen.

I was shocked.

It was the first time in my life that I had made money purely from my own knowledge — not because I showed up for a shift, but because something I knew was valuable to someone else.

10,000 yen isn't going to change my life.

But the experience might.
