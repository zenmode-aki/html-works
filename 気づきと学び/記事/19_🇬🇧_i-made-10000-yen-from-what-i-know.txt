For the first time in my life, I got a reward of 10,000 yen for my own knowledge 🐧

My mother's friend runs a business and said they were interested in AI, so we decided to meet at Starbucks.

There, for about 2 hours, I showed how I usually use AI at work.

I did nothing difficult.

With Claude, I showed a simple way to use it, just the part where AI connects with Gmail, Google Drive, Forms, and Spreadsheets, and can even send emails and edit spreadsheets from the AI chat screen.

For me, it was something I already knew. But explaining it was honestly fun.

They even bought me coffee and dinner, so that alone made me grateful enough.

After dinner, that person suddenly gave me 10,000 yen in an envelope. I was surprised.

It was the first time in my life that I got money because what I know was valuable to someone.

Somehow, I feel this experience might change my life.
