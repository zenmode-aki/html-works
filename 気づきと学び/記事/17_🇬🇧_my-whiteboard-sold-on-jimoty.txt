My whiteboard sold on Jimoty for 4,000 yen 🐧

Number 2 on my ranking of the purchases I regret the most in 2026 is the whiteboard.

It is 120 cm wide. It is not really for a house, it is for an office, and it is quite big.

The reason I bought it at first was that I thought, if I have this whiteboard, I can handle what I set up as a goal.

Imagine if I always write down my goals and tasks and look at them every day. Of course I can do everything. That is what I thought, and I bought it with a lot of expectations.

But reality is not like that. I do not update the contents on the whiteboard. And gradually it just got in the way when I went to the toilet.

So I decided to sell it on Jimoty. Jimoty is a Japanese free market app especially for local people. I mean, it is not selling to the whole country, it is selling locally. For example, I live in Nagoya, so I sell things to people in Nagoya.

And once I posted, like, does anybody want to buy it for 4,000 yen, then after about 2 days a person bought it.

So I gave it to him.
