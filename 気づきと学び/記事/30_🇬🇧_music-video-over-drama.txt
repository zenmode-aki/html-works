When I feel down, one music video works better than one drama

Rather than watching one drama on Netflix, I feel my worries fly away faster when I watch one music video on YouTube or Spotify.
I wonder why, but the energy I get from music lets me take action in real life right after.
But when I try to heal myself or cheer myself up with videos like Netflix, I usually use too much time.
My eyes get tired, and somehow I feel I cannot really change my mood.
I noticed that recently.
Maybe the best way to refresh is to watch a music video.
Also, following the lyrics while playing music is fun.
Anyway, especially when I feel down, maybe it is better to listen to music, not Netflix or short videos.
