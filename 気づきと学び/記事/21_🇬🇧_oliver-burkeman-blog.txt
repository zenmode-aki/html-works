There is a blog that I always want to read once a month. It is Oliver Burkeman's blog.

Actually, I love his books very much.

How much do I love them? 4 books on Kindle (8,000 yen), and 3 books on Audible (9,000 yen).

He is probably the only writer I have paid 17,000 yen to. That is how much I like his way of thinking.

The one I reread the most is "imperfections." In 2025, I seriously read it about once a week. I have probably read it more than 52 times.

And he also writes his own blog. Of course, I love that too!

I am a born perfectionist and a productivity geek, so his posts have almost all the answers to my life.

If you also worry about being a perfectionist, I really recommend his blog.
