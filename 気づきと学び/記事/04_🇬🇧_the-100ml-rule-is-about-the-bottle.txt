What I learned today 🐧

The 100ml rule on planes was about the container 😳!

It is not about what is inside.

For example, even if there is only 50ml inside, if the container can hold more than 100ml, it is not allowed.

I did not know that at all 😳

Because of that, a staff member stopped me at Kansai Airport.

But luckily, Kansai Airport has a drugstore on the second floor, and they sell empty containers of 100ml or less, so you can just move your liquid there and it is OK.
