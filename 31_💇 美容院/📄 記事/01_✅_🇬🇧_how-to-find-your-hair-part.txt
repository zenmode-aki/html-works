How to find the natural part in your hair, from my hairdresser

Today, I went to the hair salon.
There, my hairdresser taught me how to find the part in my hair.
It seems the part is often right above the inner end of your right eyebrow, on the side near your eye.
After wetting your hair, dry it with a hair dryer while pushing your hair back, almost like an all-back style.
Then your hair stands up softly.
It seems that by looking at how it stands up, you can find your own hair part.
