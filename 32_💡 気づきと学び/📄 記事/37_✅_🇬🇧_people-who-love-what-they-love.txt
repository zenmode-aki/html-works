At Tsuruma Park, I saw people who really show they like what they like

At Tsuruma Park, I saw people cheering for idols and putting their hearts into cosplay.

They seemed to have a clear sense of what they loved and to be grounded in it.

In a good way, they seemed guided less by appearances and more by the feeling, “I love this, so I want to do it.” I thought the way they threw themselves into it was really cool.
