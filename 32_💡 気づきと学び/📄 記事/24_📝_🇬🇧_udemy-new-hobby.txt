I might have found a new hobby. It is Udemy.

What I like about Udemy courses is how the videos are divided.
On YouTube, there are many long videos, like "complete explanation" videos that are about 10 hours long.
On YouTube, there are also videos made into a series, like "Video 1" and "Video 2."
But ads come in the middle, and related videos get in the way.
On Udemy, the videos are divided into small pieces, and I feel a sense of achievement each time I finish one.
I thought that was very good.

Also, just looking around Udemy makes me curious, and that is a good point too.

Opening Udemy might be one way to get my motivation up.
