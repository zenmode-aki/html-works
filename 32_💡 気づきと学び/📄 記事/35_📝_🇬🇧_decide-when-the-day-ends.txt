I decide when my day ends. Good night.

For a perfectionist like me, "having no end" is very painful.

■ Endless is the worst

When someone tells me I can do it until any time, I can never finish.
And I blame myself for not being able to finish.

But if I decide the end, like "I will do it until this time," I can think, "Let me make the time until then meaningful."

For people, maybe nothing is as painful as having no end.

■ Sharing online is 30 minutes a day

So I decided.
I think about sharing things online for only 30 minutes a day.

I also decide the end of the day.
When it is 9 p.m., that day is over.
I stop right there.
