I think it is OK to pay for 2 music apps!

According to Darwin's theory of evolution, things that are not needed slowly disappear through evolution.
But music has never gone away, from long ago until now.
Maybe that is because music is very important for people.
I have heard a story like that.
In fact, when you listen to music, you feel the lyrics, and you can think that you are not alone.
It also helps you relax, right?
For a long time, I paid only for Spotify.
But recently, I started paying for YouTube Music too.
This is because Spotify does not have many remixes or live songs.
On YouTube Music, you can hear the same song with a different sound.
That feels really good.
So now I pay for 2 apps.
It has only been about 2 weeks since I started paying for 2, but I am happy now.
I do not know if this will still continue in half a year.
But I want to use my own body to test what happens when you pay for 2 music apps.
