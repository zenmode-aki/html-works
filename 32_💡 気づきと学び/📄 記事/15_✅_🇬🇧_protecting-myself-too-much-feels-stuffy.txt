When I try too hard to protect myself, it becomes hard to breathe 🐧

Somehow, when I try too hard to protect myself, I feel it is harder to breathe instead.

Rather than that, having poison inside myself, even though I think I will get tired and fail a lot, and always keeping on thinking about what I can do now for the people around me, may be the trick to living happily and with energy.

Maybe the people with more energy and stamina are the people who are always trying hard to be useful to someone.
