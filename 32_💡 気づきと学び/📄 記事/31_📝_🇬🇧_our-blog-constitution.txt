I made a "constitution" for this blog.
It is the rule that comes before every other rule.
Article 1: I will never write anything that has a bad effect on the company I work for, or the company I worked for, or on the work.
I will not cause trouble for the company, the people I work with, or the customers.
I will not break a promise to keep secrets, and I will not write things that could lead to paying damages.
And I will not make careless mistakes.
I will not write about the company, or about what I actually do at work.
I will not write things that only people inside the company know.
I will only write about the industry in a general way, because I do not want to cause trouble for the people at the company.
Article 2: I will not hurt people.
Article 3: With all of that, I will share fun things.
I asked the AI that makes this blog with me to remember this constitution as the top rule too.
Because I can write without worry, I think I can also write fun things properly.
