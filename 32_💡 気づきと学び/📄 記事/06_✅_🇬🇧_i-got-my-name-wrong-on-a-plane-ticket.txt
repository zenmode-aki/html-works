A failure story with a plane ticket 🐧

In the past, I made a big mistake with a flight booking.

When I went from Nagoya to Manila in the Philippines, it seems I had booked the ticket with my family name plus my family name (for example, if your name is Gojo Satoru, it was like Gojo Gojo), and in the end I paid 40,000 yen at the counter.

When you book a plane ticket, check that your name is written properly!

Because even if the name is only a little bit wrong, you can really lose a huge amount of money 😢
