A note for myself. How to make a draft 🐧

When I take notes of things I want to post on my blog, taking notes is fun, but when I try to turn those notes into a blog post and give a big pile of them to AI all at once, I usually end up checking all the sentences later. And that is a pain, and I usually get bored in the middle. I get tired of checking.

But today I realized.

Copying and pasting to AI one by one is better than pasting them all together.

Because if I don't split it small and check the output, my own judgment does not last.

Maybe the surprisingly annoying thing in the AI era is that when AI gives a lot of output, a human has to check all of it.

Maybe checking is harder than making
