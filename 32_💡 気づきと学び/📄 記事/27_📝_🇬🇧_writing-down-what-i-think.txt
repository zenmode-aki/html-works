I think writing down what I think is the best.

When we ask people something or try to help someone, whether we notice it or not, we need to write things down objectively.

And once we write objectively, we can think from a wider point of view.

So from now on, I will write what I am thinking now on this blog. Because when I think I will write on this blog, I need to write sentences objectively, and thanks to that, my view becomes wider.
