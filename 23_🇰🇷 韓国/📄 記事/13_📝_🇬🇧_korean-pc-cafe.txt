I came to a Korean internet cafe, like the ones you often see in Korean dramas!

The seats were softer than in Japanese internet cafes, and they were like the seat a CEO would sit in.

The gadgets like the keyboards and headphones were cool too.

It is just my feeling, but there are about 3 PC cafes within a 10-minute walk.

There are that many.

If you want to kill some time in Korea, I recommend a PC cafe.
