At Suzuka, I saw the pit garages I knew from the F1 documentary

When I went all the way through the paddock tunnel, I could see the pit garages.
I thought, "Oh, this is the place I saw in the F1 documentary."
I got excited, thinking this is where the engineers build and fix many things.
But I did not know if I was allowed to cross the paddock tunnel and come to see the pit garages.
I did not know if people who are not staff could come this far.
So, a little scared, I took photos from quite far away.
I thought, "Oh, so this is their view."
Going around here got me excited.
