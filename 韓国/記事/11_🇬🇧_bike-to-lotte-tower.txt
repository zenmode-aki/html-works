I rode a rental bike to the foot of Lotte Tower in Seoul

I rented a bike in Seoul and went to the foot of Lotte Tower.
It is said that Lotte Tower is the tallest tower in Korea.
Indeed, I could not see all the way to the top.
It went up that far, and it was very powerful.
The area around Lotte Tower was very well kept.
It is a big city, but it felt calm.
If I force a comparison with Japan, it felt like the area around Tokyo Station.
Tokyo Station in Japan is a very big-city place.
But because it is so big-city, it is well kept.
The roads are wide, and people are not crowded.
So, the other way around, you can take it easy even though it is a big city, or you can relax.
The area around Lotte Tower felt like that too.
All the buildings are big, but my impression is that most of them are well kept.
There is nature, and you can walk slowly.
It was the kind of big city that I like.
Riding the bike was a lot of fun.
I was able to spend my time feeling very relaxed.
There is a park and a lake around Lotte Tower and Lotte World.
It was almost 11 at night, but everyone was running there.
My image is that there are no fat people at all in Korea.
My image is that everyone has a nice figure and is in good shape.
I wondered if it is because they exercise even this late at night.
Everything felt like a Korean drama, and it was quite interesting.
