I thought Korea meant Subway

On YouTube or something, I heard "There are so many Subways in Seoul! Subway is very popular!" and I had an image that Korea means Subway.
So I went.
The taste was not very different from Japan.
But just eating it in Korea made me a little excited.
Later, when I asked AI, Korea was number 9 in the world for the number of Subway shops.
It was more normal than I thought.
Here is one piece of Subway trivia I heard from AI.
About 10 years ago, Japan had about 480 Subway shops, and Korea had about 100.
Now Korea has over 600, and Japan has about 230.
Before anyone noticed, they had switched places.
