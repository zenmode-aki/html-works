I thought Korea meant Subway

Somehow, I had an image that "Korea means Subway."
Probably from a YouTube video or something, I heard that "there are so many Subway shops in Seoul, Korea."
Since I came all the way to Korea, I went to a Korean Subway.
As an experience, it was not very different from Subway in Japan.
But still, I thought, "Korea means Subway, right?"
I knew that Subway is an American company.
But I thought Korea had Subway on a scale like number 2 in the world.
I was somehow curious, so I asked AI about the number of Subway shops in each country.
Korea was number 9 in the world.
Even so, Japan seems to have only 234 shops for now, so Korea has almost 3 times as many shops as Japan.
"I had an image that Korea has so many Subways, but it was not really like that after all!" That was the punchline.
But the Subway in Korea served me politely, and it was delicious.
