About the Japanese version 🐧

The Japanese version of this blog is made by having AI translate the posts I first wrote in English.

So if you thought, "Hey, you are Japanese, so translate it properly yourself," you are sharp.

But Japanese is my native language, so the moment I start writing, my worst personality trait, perfectionism, turns on.

So, exactly because it is Japanese, I do not fuss over it. I leave the Japanese version to Pengesso.

So the Japanese sentences may sometimes be a mess.

If they are a mess, please send an email to Pengesso. To pengesso@gmail.com. Then I will fix it.

Thank you to everyone who is reading.
