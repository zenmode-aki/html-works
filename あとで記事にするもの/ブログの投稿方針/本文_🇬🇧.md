The rules of this blog 🐧

About work, I write in a fun and positive way. I do not write negative things, or complaints, or dissatisfaction.

About life, it is the opposite. I write about my failures, the things I gave up on, and negative things. I do not post to show off my happiness. About my private life, I think the more negative it is, and the more it is a failure, the better.

About love, I do not post hints. Pengesso is a character who does not do romance.

Hints. Showing off happiness. Showing off that I have friends. I do not write these.

About money, I also do not show off that I have money.

On the other hand, I want to share the things I am bad at. Like, this is scary, or, I am bad at this kind of thing. I will write those, more and more.
