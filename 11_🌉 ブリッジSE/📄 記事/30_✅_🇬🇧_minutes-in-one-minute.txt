People were surprised when I made the meeting minutes in 1 minute 🐧

When I worked as a bridge SE,
I was allowed to use whatever AI I liked on my own computer.

It wasn't some huge company. It was more of a startup,
and the president was the type to say "let's use AI."

So I paid for it, and every week I made something at work more efficient.
That was genuinely fun.

The one I liked most was making the meeting minutes.

At the start, writing up the minutes of a meeting
used to take me 1 hour, sometimes 2 hours.

With Gemini, it took 1 minute.

The good thing about Gemini is that it handles multimedia,
so it's strong with images and audio, not just text.

So when a meeting ended, I handed the minutes over to Gemini,
typed a prompt, and generated it in about 1 minute.

And people around me would go "whoa, that was fast." That made me happy.
