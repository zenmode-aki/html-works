When I was about to start working in the Philippines, the training had a story that shocked me 🐧

In the Philippines, there is apparently a strong culture,
or rather a taboo, against scolding someone in front of other people.

Hurting the other person's pride
is apparently a seriously rude thing to do.

Learning that as a culture, as a Japanese person,
I thought it was a really good culture to have.

And it's true, walking around in the Philippines,
I hardly ever saw two people shouting at each other.

I never saw anyone blowing up at a shop over a complaint,
and at work I never saw a Filipino person getting seriously angry either.

In Japan, younger people don't seem to get that angry,
but you do sometimes see an older man losing it in a restaurant.

Staying calm with the person in front of you, never blowing up,
and getting through each day with peaceful, constructive relationships.

I thought that was a really good thing.
