This is about the inside of the room I lived in 🐧

It was a studio. Just the one room.

The rent was about 25,000 pesos, which is around 400 US dollars today.

For size, maybe about as much floor as 4 single beds.
It was a fairly small room.

The furniture came with it from the start.

And apparently I was the first person to move in,
so on the day I arrived they got me a washing machine,
kitchen things, all sorts.

I'm really grateful to the owner for that.

The place I spent the longest inside that room
was in front of my standing desk.

I often connected the TV to YouTube and watched it,
eating my meals standing up while it played.

From the window, you could see the open space on the 7th floor.

"Oh, someone's walking a dog." "Someone's doing yoga."
I'd peer down at them a bit from above.

By the way, I filmed this video right before I moved out.

I'd lived in Cebu, and I wanted to keep that as a memory,
so I just sort of made a video of it.

I never showed it to anyone, and I didn't really post it either.
