- Hair salon
- Paying for Claude (AI)

Since I started spending money on the hair salon, my confidence lasts for a whole month.
When my hair and eyebrows are neat, I look in the mirror and think, "I am not bad today either."
Then I want to go out, and talking with people became more fun than before.
I especially want people who think, like I used to, "I do not need to spend 5,000 yen on my hair," to try it once.
