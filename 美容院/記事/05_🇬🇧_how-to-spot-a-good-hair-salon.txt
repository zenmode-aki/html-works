How to spot a good hair salon, from my hairdresser: check the shampoo chairs

Choosing a hair salon is pretty hard, right?
The usual way is to choose a hairdresser with many followers on social media.
If you look at the salon itself, it seems good to check the shampoo chairs.
The price range of shampoo chairs is very wide, and it seems the expensive ones are very expensive.
She said a salon that spends money there is a little more trustworthy.
On the other hand, be careful of salons that finish by roughly thinning your hair with thinning scissors at the end.
According to my hairdresser, that can sometimes be a sign they are hiding a poor finish.
