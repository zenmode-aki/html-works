When you get tired in Tokyo, go to Thermae-yu in Shinjuku

This is my recommended place for when you get tired on a Tokyo trip.
I came to Tokyo thinking I would ride a bike around Tokyo a little.
But this was in summer.
Tokyo in summer is really hot.
I lost the energy to ride a bike outside and travel around.
I just wanted to relax.
At that time, the place AI recommended to me was "Thermae-yu" in Shinjuku.
This place was good.
Really, you can take a bath, and you can relax a lot.
It is a place like a hotel.
Inside, there are many chairs, sofas, and reclining chairs.
You can charge your phone and take many naps, and I relaxed a lot.
There are also terrace seats on the roof.
At that time, the 2026 World Cup in North and Central America was on.
On the roof, World Cup soccer matches were shown on a projector.
I took a break while watching the matches.
Also, right in front of Thermae-yu, there is a Yoshimoto office.
That was also kind of fun.
Yoshimoto is a Japanese comedy company.
When you turn on the TV in Japan, you can think that about 1 in 5 people on TV belong to Yoshimoto.
Many famous comedians in Japan belong to Yoshimoto.
With that Yoshimoto office right in front of me, I got a little excited that I might spot someone from TV.
And the Yoshimoto office was a building that looked a lot like a school.
That also surprised me a little.
Thermae-yu is really a place where you can kill time for as long as you like.
It seems it is open 24 hours.
I stayed about 6 hours, and it was still about 3,000 yen.
I could take a bath and sleep a lot, and it was a very good place.
I relaxed so much that I took a bath 2 times here.
I took a bath, slept, ate, and took a bath again.
If you get tired in Tokyo, a bath place like this may be a good choice.
