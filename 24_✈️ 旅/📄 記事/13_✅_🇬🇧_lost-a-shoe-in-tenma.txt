I drank sake in Tenma, Osaka, fell asleep on the street, and lost 1 shoe

The place where I failed big with alcohol was "Tenma" in Osaka.
Tenma is an area with so many izakaya bars.
And there are many cheap ones too.
There, I once went out to eat with a friend who lives in Osaka.
That day, I got really drunk.
And I fell asleep on the street in Tenma.
In the morning, when I woke up, my shoe was gone.
I lost only 1 of my shoes.
I was that drunk, and I slept somewhere I did not even know.
From that day, I became scared of alcohol.
But my friend was a really good guy.
It seems I fell asleep on the street, but my friend took my bag and other valuables home for me.
So my important things were not stolen.
Also, I thought Japan really is safe.
My smartphone was in my pocket, and it was not stolen at all.
When I woke up, I still had my smartphone, so I somehow got home.
I am really glad I passed out drunk in Osaka, or I mean, in Japan.
I was so thankful for how safe Japan is.
If you find 1 black shoe in Tenma,
that is probably mine.
I am sorry.
