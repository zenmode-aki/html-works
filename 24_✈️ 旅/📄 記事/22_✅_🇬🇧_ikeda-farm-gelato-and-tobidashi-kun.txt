Gelato at Ikeda Farm, and the "Tobidashi-kun" signs all over Shiga

Last, I went to Ikeda Farm in Shiga Prefecture.
The gelato was very delicious.
I do not know if it is the Kansai area or only Shiga,
but when you go to Shiga, you see so many signs of a kid about to run out into the road.
My sister taught me, "This is called Tobidashi-kun."
When you see it many times on the road, you slowly start to feel attached to this Tobidashi-kun.
