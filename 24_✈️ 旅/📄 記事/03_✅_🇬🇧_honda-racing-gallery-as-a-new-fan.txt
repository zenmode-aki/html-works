As a new F1 fan, I visited the Honda RACING Gallery but could not get excited yet

Inside Suzuka Circuit, there is a place called the Honda RACING Gallery.
Inside, cars such as F1 cars were on display.
For F1 fans, I think this Honda RACING Gallery is an incredibly exciting place.
But for me, and it is really frustrating, I did not get very excited.
I started to like F1 around March or April 2026.
I went there when only 2 or 3 months had passed since I became an F1 fan.
I only know recent F1.
So the displays were a little hard for me.
I regret that if I had learned a bit more F1 history before going, I could have enjoyed it more.
Because I am a newcomer who started liking F1 this year, in 2026.
