I went to Eigenji, a Japan Heritage temple in Shiga, and the walk through nature was the best part

I went to a place called Eigenji in Shiga Prefecture.
It is a temple that is a Japan Heritage site.
Eigenji is in a place full of nature.
You walk quite a lot from the parking lot to the temple entrance.
That path felt really good.
Surrounded by nature.
You climb many steps, and that was fun too.
Indeed, the forest and everything were very well kept, enough to be called Japan Heritage.
Inside Eigenji, you had to pay, so I did not go in.
But the nature on the way to the entrance was very beautiful.
It was a lot of fun.
