In Malaysia, there is a shopping mall that looks like an Egyptian pyramid, and I found Jollibee inside

In Sunway, Malaysia, there is a pyramid-like shopping mall called "Sunway Pyramid."
When I passed by it in a taxi, I went, "What is this? What is this building?"
Then, when I had some time, I stopped by.
There was a sphinx and more, and it looked flashy too.
The inside is flashy too.
It is a shopping mall, but there is an ice skating rink inside.
Anyway, this pyramid mall is really big.
It was so big that walking from one end to the other made me think, "Is this an airport?"
Inside the pyramid mall, I found a shop called Jollibee.
The reason I took a photo is that it made me nostalgic.
When I lived in the Philippines, this shop called Jollibee was very popular in the Philippines.
How popular? The Philippines is probably the only country where this fast food shop, Jollibee, beats McDonald's.
In the Philippines, you see Jollibee more than McDonald's.
In Japan, you do not see Jollibee. You see McDonald's, though.
I saw Jollibee, which you do not see in Japan, thought "Wow, so nostalgic," and took a photo without thinking.
