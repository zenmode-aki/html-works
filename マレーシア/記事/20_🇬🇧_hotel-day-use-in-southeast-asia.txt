When you get tired in Southeast Asia, book a hotel for day use

■ Too Hot
This is my recommended way to spend the daytime when you are tired in Southeast Asia.
In Southeast Asia, the daytime is hot, right?
If you try to keep traveling from about 8 in the morning until night, it is hard.

■ 5 AM Flight
On this trip, because of a flight trouble, our flight to Kansai Airport changed to 5 the next morning.
Because of that, in a good way, I could use the whole daytime.
I could stay in Kuala Lumpur city until about 1 at night.
I thought, "What, I have this much time left?"

■ Tired But Eager
I am in Kuala Lumpur, so I want to enjoy it.
But I am really tired.
Killing time at a cafe until midnight felt like a waste somehow.
I want to go sightseeing with energy at night.
So I want to recover a little.
But the flight is at 5 the next morning, so it is not worth staying the night at a hotel.

■ Day Use
That is when I finally found "day use."
On Agoda and other sites, you can book a hotel for day use.
For example, it is a booking where you can be at the hotel for only 4 hours, from 14:30 to 18:30.
I did not know there was a way of booking like this.
When you want to rest a little during a trip abroad, I really recommend day use.

■ 52nd Floor Pool
The hotel I chose had an infinity pool very high up, on the 52nd floor.

■ Burger And Nap
I went back to the room, ate a hamburger by the window, had a drink, and took a nap for about 3 hours.

■ Under 3,000 Yen
Thanks to that, I could enjoy Kuala Lumpur more at night, when it was cool.
And it was about 2,800 yen for 4 hours.
There was an infinity pool, and the room was very clean, but it was under 3,000 yen.
It may be a common thing on trips abroad, but if you try to enjoy the whole day, you get tired instead.
Rest a good amount.
For those times to rest, Agoda's day use helped me a lot.
