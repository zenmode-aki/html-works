When you get tired in Southeast Asia, book a hotel for day use

This is my recommended way to spend the daytime when you are tired on a trip in Southeast Asia or somewhere like that.
In Southeast Asia, the daytime is hot, right?
If you try to keep traveling from about 8 in the morning until night, it is hard.
On this trip, because of a flight trouble, our flight from Kuala Lumpur airport to Kansai Airport was going to leave at 5 the next morning.
The time moved.
Because the flight was at 5 the next morning, in a good way, I could use most of the daytime.
I could stay in Kuala Lumpur city until about 1 at night.
I thought, "What, I have this much time left?"
I am in Kuala Lumpur, so I want to enjoy it.
But I am really tired.
Killing time at a cafe until midnight felt like a waste somehow.
I do not want to get tired.
I want to go sightseeing with energy at night.
So I want to recover a little.
But the flight leaves at 5 the next morning, so it is not worth booking a hotel, checking in, and staying the night.
That is when I finally found "day use."
On Agoda and other sites, you can book a hotel for day use.
For example, it is a way of booking where you can stay at the hotel for only 4 hours, from 14:30 to 18:30.
I did not know there was a way of booking like this.
With Agoda, you can book hotels, and you can also book day use.
You want to relax a little during a trip abroad.
At times like that, I really recommend this day use.
I rested at the day use hotel for about 3 and a half hours.
That hotel had an infinity pool very high up, on the 52nd floor.
I went back to the room, ate a hamburger by the window, had a drink, and took a nap for about 3 hours.
Thanks to that, my trip in Kuala Lumpur at night, when it was cool, became even more fun.
And it was about 2,800 yen for about 4 hours.
