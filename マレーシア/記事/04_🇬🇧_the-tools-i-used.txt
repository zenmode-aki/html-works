These are the three tools I used in this project, to send my sister to a university abroad 🐧

GitHub

It is the place where I put all the data. My sister's high school grades, the documents about going on to university that she got from her high school, the information about each of the eight candidate universities, the latest chat history, anything.

Claude Code

I used the information in GitHub to talk things over. I organized the information inside GitHub, and fixed it. I also had it check whether any emails or WhatsApp messages about my sister had come in.

Claude in Chrome

It is for everything I did with Chrome. This was very useful. When I needed to do a lot of browser work, like the universities' inquiry forms, or booking hotels and flights, I left almost all of that clicking and typing work to this AI.

Among these, the one I relied on the most was Claude in Chrome.

It is a thing where Claude does the browser work for you, and having all that clicking on web forms taken over was a big help.
