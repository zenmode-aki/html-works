On the first day in Malaysia, I could not use ChatGPT and Claude 🐧

Both ChatGPT and Claude said something like "your region is not supported."

Oh my gosh. I panicked. That is because my travel plan was already in the project folder of those AI apps.

The cause was maybe the SIM card. The SIM card for Malaysia that I bought on Amazon before the trip seems to be related to a Chinese internet company, so maybe my IP address looked like a Chinese one. I am not sure.

So I thought, if I cannot use American AI, then let's use Chinese AI. I installed DeepSeek and asked it how to use Claude and ChatGPT in this area. It told me many things, like first check the VPN and the proxy.

Thanks to DeepSeek, I could use Claude and ChatGPT. It is kind of funny.

Actually, with the Kuala Lumpur airport Wi-Fi, Claude and ChatGPT worked normally. So it was not a Malaysia problem, but a problem only with my SIM card. Malaysia is a country where you can use Claude and ChatGPT.

In the end, I stopped using that SIM card, and I used Rakuten Mobile, the one I use in Japan, as it is with international roaming.
If you want to use American AI like Claude or ChatGPT abroad, international roaming from a Japanese carrier is perfect.

To be honest, maybe because of the media, I somehow felt uneasy about AI from China, and I had not used it much.
But DeepSeek was really, really good. It was a good AI.
