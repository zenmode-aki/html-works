On the first day in Malaysia, I could not use ChatGPT and Claude 🐧

Maybe the reason was my IP address. Maybe it looked like a Chinese one. I am not sure.

I bought a SIM card for Malaysia on Amazon before the trip. That was what I brought. But somehow that SIM card seems to be related to a Chinese internet company. I am not sure.

Anyway, I could not use them. ChatGPT and Claude said "your region is not supported," something like that.

Oh my gosh. I panicked. My travel plan was already saved in the project folder of those AI apps.

But when I used the Kuala Lumpur airport wifi, Claude and ChatGPT worked. So maybe this problem was only my SIM card. It was not a Malaysia problem. Malaysia is a country where using Claude and ChatGPT is allowed. It is not restricted.

So I changed. I had already bought that SIM card on Amazon in Japan, but I did not use it. Instead I used my Japanese carrier, the one I use in Japan. Rakuten Mobile. International roaming is better.

This is a tip only for Japanese people. But if you love to use Claude or ChatGPT — American AI, anyway — and you want to use it in a foreign country, then a Japanese carrier's international roaming is better. It is the perfect way.

I am not sure, but maybe the SIM I bought has restricted areas. Malaysia is near China. Maybe that is why a Chinese company is involved. I am not sure.

So, when American AI could not be used, I thought: then let's use Chinese AI. I installed DeepSeek. I asked DeepSeek how to use Claude and ChatGPT in this area. DeepSeek advised me. First, check the VPN and the proxy, and so on.

Thanks to DeepSeek, I could use Claude and ChatGPT.

It is kind of funny.

DeepSeek was really, really good. It was a good AI.

But somehow I am already stuck. I already love Claude so much. I even love that Claude icon.

So I cannot cheat on my AI anymore. Kind of. It's a joke.
