In the countryside, the village radio rings at 12:00 🔔

🏡 Grandma's House

This is a story from when I went to my grandmother's house in Gifu.

What felt new to me in the countryside is that radio is still used.

🔔 Noon Chime

When it turns 12:00, the village radio automatically rings "kin-kon-kan-kon."

That felt new.

🏙️ In The City

When you live in a city, there is nothing like "at this time, something always rings."

So, I thought it was new.
