🧩 PERFECTIONISM · PART 2 OF 3

Preparation Never
Becomes Perfect

The first rule is simple, even if it is not exciting
↩️

This is the next part. I wrote that perfectionists hate finishing, so they keep starting new things. So what should I do? The first answer is not exciting, but I should just do it.

🌀

Perfectionists try to start after they make their preparation perfect. But preparation never becomes perfect. You may think, “If I do only this, I can finally start.” But this “only this” comes out forever. A preparation to-do list is not limited. For a perfectionist, it is really endless. There is no end.

ONE MORE THING
THEN I START
📝

Also, making the list is quite fun. This is not good. While I am preparing, I have not failed at anything yet.

🏃

Just do it. Finish what you started.

📌

It is not exciting, but this is the rule. “When I can do XX” will not come. I should remember only that. Next, I will write another way.
