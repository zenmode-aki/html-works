The first answer is not exciting, but I should just do it.

🌀 Never Ready

Perfectionists try to start after they make their preparation perfect.
But preparation never becomes perfect.
You may think, “If I do only this, I can finally start.”
But this “only this” comes out forever.
A preparation to-do list is not limited.
For a perfectionist, it is really endless.
There is no end.

ONE MORE THING

📝 Lists Are Fun

Also, making the list is quite fun.
This is not good.
While I am preparing, I have not failed at anything yet.

🏃 Just Do It

Just do it.
Finish what you started.

📌 The Boring Rule

It is not exciting, but this is the rule.
“When I can do XX” will not come.
I should remember only that.
