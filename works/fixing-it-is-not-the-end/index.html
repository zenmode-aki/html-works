🔧 Not Finished

With a fault, fixing it is not where it ends.

⏱️ Every Second

For a customer whose line is down, every single minute and second is heavy, so first of all it is about speed.

📝 After Recovery

But after the recovery, the job of leaving a record is waiting.

🕐 Four Things

What happened, at what hour and minute. How far the impact reached. At what hour and minute, what response was made. When it recovered.

📂 Accurately Kept

All of that has to be kept accurately.

🏛️ Going Outside

When a fault gets large, it is not only an internal report. Sometimes documents are needed for the other carriers, for related organisations, and in some cases for public authorities.

✍️ That Far

Not just fixing it, but accurately writing down what happened, how we responded, and how it recovered. I realised the job goes that far.
