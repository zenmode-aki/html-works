🏎 A DAY AT SUZUKA CIRCUIT

I got hooked on Drive to Survive, so I went to Suzuka Circuit 🏎

🏁 We Arrived

We arrived at Suzuka Circuit.

📺 Hooked On F1

Lately, I am really hooked on "Drive to Survive," an F1 documentary on Netflix.

I have already watched about 5 seasons, and I got really hooked.

So, since I had the chance, I came to Suzuka Circuit.

📅 A Quiet Friday

That day was a Friday.

It seems races at Suzuka Circuit are usually held on weekends.

Because it was a Friday, there were very few people.

But I was very excited.

🎢 Also A Park

Actually, Suzuka Circuit is a race circuit, but it is also an amusement park.

It is a race circuit and an amusement park too.

It is that kind of place.
