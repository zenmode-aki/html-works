🏎 A DAY AT SUZUKA CIRCUIT

Suzuka Circuit is an amusement park, but the staff feel like engineers 🔧

🎢 An Amusement Park

Suzuka Circuit is also an amusement park.

So there are many amusement park rides too.

👷 Good Work Vibes

But is it totally an amusement park? It is a little different.

What I found interesting was the staff.

The staff feel like workers.

They feel like engineers.

🏰 Theme Park Staff

For example, Universal Studios Japan or Tokyo Disneyland.

There, when you pass the staff, they welcome you in the world of Disney or Universal, right?

🔧 Car People

Suzuka Circuit is also an amusement park.

But it is a car-related place, so even when you pass the staff, it is like "Good work, man."

That slightly stiff and awkward feeling was interesting.

It is an amusement park, but it felt like, "Well, that makes sense."

Somehow, I remember that it was interesting.
