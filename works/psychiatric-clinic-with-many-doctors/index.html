When choosing a mental health clinic, one with many doctors may be better 🌙

🌙 Once A Month

Now, I make sure to go to a mental health clinic once a month.

It is because working a job with night shifts makes it hard for me to sleep.

It is not that my mind is very sick.

I only go to get sleeping pills.

😴 They Really Work

The sleeping pills from the clinic are really amazing.

If I take them, I can sleep.

On nights when I cannot sleep, if I take the medicine from the mental clinic, I can sleep very deeply.

Also, just the feeling of safety that "I have that medicine" helps me sleep, and that makes me happy too.

🩺 How To Choose

From here on, this is a total amateur's opinion.

I think a clinic with more doctors might be better for mental health clinics.

Clinics in Japan usually cost quite a lot for the first visit.

If you change the clinic itself, you have to pay for a first visit again.

But if you can change your doctor inside the same clinic, you save a little money.

👨‍⚕️ Many Doctors

The clinic I go to is like that.

In a good way, it is very systematic, and it is always very smooth until I see the doctor.

Also, there are many doctors.

I think some mental health doctors suit you and some do not.

When you feel "Maybe I do not get along with this doctor," you can change doctors from the next visit.
