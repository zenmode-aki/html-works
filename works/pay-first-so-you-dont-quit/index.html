Pay first so you do not quit before the fun begins 🎟️

🧠 Psychology💡 Tips📚 Learning
🌱 Easy to Quit

With new things, it is easy to quit before you find the fun. At first, you are bad at it, and it is boring.

🎟️ Pay First

So paying for a class or tools first, so you cannot easily quit, is a good idea too.

😤 What a Waste

The feeling "What a waste!" pushes you until it gets fun.
