Willpower is a battery that runs low during the day 🔋

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity🛋️ Rest
🔋 The Battery

It seems willpower is like the battery of a smartphone.

🚃 Small Drains

It goes down a little each time you hurry, ride a crowded train, or worry about other people.

🍩 Evening Trap

When the battery runs out in the evening, you lose to snacks and your phone easily.
