This is the next part of the last story. I thought about why too much input makes me unable to move.

👂 → 🔎 → 🧍

Maybe the order is like this. I listen to many people’s opinions. Then I start to feel that there is a correct answer in this world. But I do not know it yet. So I listen to more. I have to know more, and more, and in the end I do not move from that place.

Each time I learned something, I thought I was getting closer to the correct answer. But in fact, only one idea was growing: “There is a correct answer.”

💡

I remembered a quote. It seems to be words from a psychologist named William James.

“Wisdom is knowing what to overlook.”
