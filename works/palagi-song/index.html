The one Filipino song I still keep listening to: "Palagi" 🎧

🇵🇭 Song Tips

When I lived in the Philippines, my coworkers and friends often recommended Filipino songs to me.

But to be honest, most of the songs did not really touch my heart.

🎵 Just One

But there is just one song that is still in my favorite playlist, and I keep listening to it.

It is "Palagi." It means "always."

😌 It Calms Me

I like the melody, and when I listen to it, my overthinking stops and I can relax.
