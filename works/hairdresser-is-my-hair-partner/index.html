💇 WHAT MY HAIRDRESSER TAUGHT ME

A good hairdresser is a partner for your hairstyle, and even says no when needed 🤝

🎯 My Hair Goal

At my salon now, the hairdresser remembers what hairstyle I am aiming for.

My hairstyle now is kind of like Himmel from "Frieren: Beyond Journey's End."

But it is a little too straight, so I want the side hair to curve softly and flow.

🤝 Built Together

We are building that goal little by little, together with my hairdresser.

Hair grows by itself even if I do nothing, so it is fun to feel, "Today I got a little closer to my ideal."

🙅 They Say No

Also, even when I say, "Please cut here," they give their honest opinion, like "If I cut there, it will grow out strangely."

They do not just go along with everything, like "Yes, I will do it." When they need to say no, they say no.

That is what I like most.
