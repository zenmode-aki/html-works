University visit, day 1: Taylor's University 🏞️

🗓️ Day 1 · Morning🏞️ Big lake🤖 Tour by a robotics student☕ Coffee
🎓 Day One

University visit, day 1! 🐧

I went to Taylor's University (Lakeside Campus, Subang Jaya).

🏞️ Big Lake

What stood out was that there was a big lake, and both the nature and the size of the school were overwhelming.

It seems it is one of the top private universities in Malaysia.

🤖 Campus Tour

Here, an Indian student majoring in robotics (robot engineering) gave us a campus tour.

His English was very fluent, he was friendly, and he was very cool.

The campus tour took almost 1 hour.

The buildings were that complex, and there were many unique classrooms.

☕ Great Welcome

They even served us coffee during the school explanation, the hospitality was really good, and it became one of the universities that both my sister and I liked a lot.
