Faults usually happen when the machines come back up after lightning ⚡

⚡ After A Blackout

After a power cut, errors happen easily.

🌩 Power All Drops

When lightning causes a power cut, the power at the site drops all at once, and the power to the routers, the home gateways and the VPN equipment goes off too.

🔌 Coming Back Up

And then, at the moment when the equipment starts up again, a fault tends to happen.

🌩 Lightning→🔌 Power drops→💡 Machines restart→🚨 Fault appears
☎️ Customers Call

So after lightning or a power cut, we often get phone calls from customers.
