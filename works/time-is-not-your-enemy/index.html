The real point of Pomodoro is to stop fighting time 🍅

📖 Books✅ Productivity💭 Mindset
🍅 The Pomodoro

Pomodoro is a way to work for 25 minutes and rest for 5 minutes, 4 times, and then take a longer rest.

🎮 Not Control

It seems the real point is not to control time.

🤝 Accept It

It is to accept that your time is limited.
