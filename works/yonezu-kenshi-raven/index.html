A song by Yonezu Kenshi made me think about what adults quietly hide 🐧

🎧 A Song Played

This is a song that Spotify happened to play for me on an ordinary way home.

☀️ That Summer Feeling

The moment I heard the intro, for some reason I remembered a summer from when I was a kid. A view like the blue sky of Japan, the kind I used to look up at right beside a park.

🎤 Two Lyrics

There are 2 lyrics that hit me.

⭐ The First One

星ほしの名前なまえを知しるたび
僕ぼくらは大人おとなになった
誰だれにも渡わたせない秘密ひみつが１つずつ増ふえてった。Every time we learned the name of a star,
we became adults.
One more secret that we could never hand to anyone
kept piling up, one at a time.— Yonezu Kenshi, "Raven"

😮 I Get It

I get it...!! The older you get, the more things pile up that you can not tell anyone.

🙊 Things Unsaid

Why did I do that back then, things like that. The faces of people I never repaid, or someone I ended up betraying.

📖 My Own Story

A version of my own story that I can not really call clean quietly keeps piling up. From now on, I really want to keep living honestly, for good.

🎼 One More Lyric

There is one more lyric that struck me.

💔 The Second One

人ひとが嫌きらいなあの子こに
気きづけば子供こどもができた
同おなじ夢ゆめを見みてたあいつは
心こころを壊こわしていたTo that kid who hated people,
before I knew it, she had a child.
That guy who had the same dream as I did
had broken down inside.— Yonezu Kenshi, "Raven"

😳 About Myself

I thought, "wait, is this about me?"

💭 A Few Times

I really have had a few times when my heart almost broke down.

🙂 Always Fine

My friends from elementary and middle school probably think I am the kind of person who is always fine, for life.

🤐 They Don't Post

People whose hearts have broken down do not really post about it.

🌫 Just Invisible

So maybe it is just invisible, and there are actually quite a lot of us.

⚽ World Cup Theme

By the way, apparently this song was made as the soccer theme for a Japanese TV station's coverage of the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
