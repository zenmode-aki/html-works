🛠 NETWORK OPERATIONS⚡ 15 SEC

Even the veterans work with the manual open 🐧

🤔 Thought Easy

Honestly, at first I thought it might be easy, because it is a temp job.

👹 Very Wrong

But I was very wrong. There is a monstrous amount to remember.

📋 The List

Equipment model numbers, types of service and network, telling alarms apart, isolating faults, arranging field engineers, working with other departments, checking with other carriers, reporting after recovery.

🫠 Paper Fails

If you tried to manage all of that with just a paper notebook, you would definitely break.

📖 Veterans Too

Actually, even the senior people who have done this for years basically work while looking at the manual.

🧠 Too Much

It is not an amount you can memorise one by one.

🔗 Shortcut Lists

So the more veteran someone is, the more neatly they keep a list of shortcuts that gets them to the manual in the fewest steps.

🎯 Real Skill

Rather than remembering everything, keeping the information you need within reach. I think that is the more important skill on this floor.
