📞 Calling The Police

When I handle a fault, sometimes I have to call a police station.

⚡ Dangerous Hanging Cable

If the cause of the fault is a broken cable on a utility pole, the hanging cable is dangerous for people walking and for cars, so we need to get permission to use the road for the emergency repair.

📠 Fax And Call

While the field worker is heading to the site, I make the papers and send them by fax, and I also call the traffic section of the police.

😳 I Call Them
