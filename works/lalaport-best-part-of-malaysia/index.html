The best part of my Malaysia trip was LaLaport, a Japanese shopping mall 🚪

🥇 The Best Part

In Malaysia, I saw the twin towers and the food street.

But honestly, the best thing in Kuala Lumpur was "LaLaport."

📏 Bigger Than Japan

LaLaport is a shopping mall made by Mitsui Fudosan in Japan, and it is familiar in my hometown.

LaLaport in Kuala Lumpur opened in 2022.

It has about 400 shops.

When it opened, it was the biggest of all the LaLaports, including the ones in Japan.

Surprisingly, it is bigger than the original.

🪶 Not Heavy

Shopping malls abroad somehow feel like metal, and the buildings feel heavy.

The mall under the twin towers was like that too.

But LaLaport is bright and light.

Even the toilets are shiny like in Japan.

The inside looks just like the LaLaport in my hometown.

My sister and I felt relieved the moment we went in.

🚪 Like Anywhere Door

Only inside LaLaport, it feels like I went through the Anywhere Door.

I almost really thought, "Huh, did I come back to Japan?"

LaLaport is trying so hard abroad🥺

As a Japanese person, I somehow felt proud and happy.
