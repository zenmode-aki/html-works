💇 WHAT MY HAIRDRESSER TAUGHT ME

The difference between a 1,000-yen haircut and a haircut over 5,000 yen ✂️

✂️ Used To Think

For a long time, I thought a 1,000-yen haircut was enough for my hair.

But now, I go to a salon that costs almost 7,000 yen without a discount.

💬 The Talk First

The biggest difference is the talk before cutting.

When I talk about my job now and how I want to be in the future, the hairdresser thinks about it with me.

"Then, maybe this kind of hairstyle would be good." They talk with me properly for about 10 minutes, and then start cutting.

Each visit takes about 1 hour, and eyebrow trimming and shampoo are included.

🌱 It Grows Well

Right after the cut, to be honest, I could not see much difference.

But after 1 week, then 1 month, I am surprised to see, "It is growing out really nicely."
