🍔 MY McDONALD'S PART-TIME JOB

McDonald's has company-run stores and franchise stores, and they feel different 🏪

🏪 2 Kinds

McDonald's in Japan actually has 2 kinds of stores.

"Company-run stores" run by the McDonald's company itself, and "franchise stores" run by owners.

🔍 How To Tell

There is no clear rule to tell them apart, but I have a rough image.

Company-run stores are often inside city buildings or inside stations.

I feel franchise stores are often in the suburbs, as a building with only McDonald's.

👔 Strict Store

The store I worked at was a company-run store.

Looking back, my impression is that it was strict in many ways.

Especially, the training on how to speak was serious.

Once, a customer saw a manager training a crew member, and wrote this in a survey.

"I think the store and the staff's service were very good. But I am not here to eat fancy French food, so I do not think you need to train them that strictly."

It seems the area manager was strict too, and the store manager and managers always seemed desperate.

😌 Relaxed Franchise

On the other hand, when I went to help at a franchise store, the atmosphere was quite different.

The way of speaking was not so strict, the rules for hair and nails were looser, and you could chat a little during work.

Overall, it had a looseness and relaxed feeling that the company-run store did not have.

If you want a relaxed part-time job, maybe a franchise is more fun😅

🎮 Like A Game

But company-run stores have good points too.

They are strict about numbers and goals, like sales, serving speed, survey satisfaction, and the number of complaints, so I could work like it was a game.

When we reached a goal, the reward and sense of achievement were big, and it was easy to get into the zone while working.
