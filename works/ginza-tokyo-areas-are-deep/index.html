🗼 MY SUMMER TRIP TO TOKYO

Walking in Ginza, I felt that Tokyo's areas go much deeper than Nagoya's 💎

🗼 Tokyo Is Amazing

As someone from Nagoya, I went to Tokyo and found something that made me think, "Tokyo really is amazing."

I went to Ginza.

📺 From TV

I had heard on TV and other places that Ginza is an area where fancy shops gather.

I am not from Tokyo at all, but I heard Ginza is that kind of area.

📏 1 Km Long

What I think is amazing about Tokyo areas is that the area really goes on for about 1 kilometer.

Even 1 or 2 roads over, there are still fancy shops lined up.

🏯 Nagoya Is Thin

Nagoya is also called a big city area.

But there is a big difference between Nagoya and Tokyo.

For example, Nagoya has an area called Nishiki.

People say it is an area with many bars.

But even so, once you go 1 street over, it can become a place that is not that special.

Osu is also really only about 1 block, or the area is small.

So if you go about 2 roads over, it is already a totally normal area.

💪 Tokyo Is Real

The places in Nagoya people call "this kind of place" are, you could say, very weak.

But when Tokyo says "this is this kind of area," it really is that kind of area.

It is a little hard to put into words.

The scale is totally different.

I walked around really thinking, "Tokyo is amazing."
