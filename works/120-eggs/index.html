🇵🇭 LIVING IN THE PHILIPPINES⚡ 15 SEC

When I lived in Cebu, my fridge was always full of eggs 🐧

🥚 Full Of Eggs

When I lived in Cebu, my fridge was full of eggs.

💪 Protein Matters

I had this idea in my head that getting enough protein matters.

🍖 The Meat Smell

But if you want protein in Cebu, the first thing is meat, and both the chicken and the pork have a bit of a smell to them compared to the meat you can buy in a supermarket back home.

🦴 Lots Of Bones

There are also quite a lot of bones in it, which made it hard to eat.

🍳 Not Cooking

So I never really felt like cooking the meat myself.

🐧 Eggs Instead

Which is how I ended up eating eggs all the time in Cebu.

🍳 Fried Eggs Only

The eggs, I did cook myself. Mostly, I just made fried eggs.

📦 Trays Of 30

I always bought eggs in trays of 30.

🏬 Ayala And Metro

There was a fairly well known shopping mall nearby called Ayala, and inside Ayala Center Cebu there's a store called Metro.

🛒 Buying Them Up

I was basically buying up their eggs.

🎯 Packed With Eggs

Before I knew it, the fridge was packed with them.

🎮 Eggs As Lives

Back then, I genuinely believed the number of eggs in my fridge was the same as my number of lives in real life.

⚠️ Like Mario

Like in a Mario game, I felt this tension that if the number of eggs ever hit zero, I might actually die in the real world, so I always kept my fridge stocked with eggs.

🥚 A Superfood

Because eggs are a superfood. As long as I have eggs, life will somehow work out.
