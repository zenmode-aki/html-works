Say good feelings out loud to really taste them 🍤

🧠 Psychology😊 Happiness💡 Tips
💬 Into Words

It seems you can enjoy good feelings more when you put them into words.

🍵 Its Name

This is called "savoring."

"Tea, nice smell, so warm." "Fried shrimp, so plump!" "Futon, feels so good."

⚡ TRY IT
Fill the happy jar
Tap the good things. Does the jar fill?
What shall I enjoy? … (munch munch) 💨 Tea, nice smell! So warm… Fried shrimp, so plump! Futon, feels so good… Sunshine, so cozy!
🐧
🍯 Happy0%
Hmm, the jar is still empty… Try 💬 Say it!
🍯 Full jar! Same things, more happiness.
🤐 We Stay Quiet

People say bad things out loud, but they don't say good things much.

📅 2 Weeks

Why not try saying good things out loud for 2 weeks?
