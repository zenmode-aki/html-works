I cannot take a single page of work material home with me 🚪

1🔒 Nothing leaves the office
📄 Not One Page

I cannot take home even 1 page of the work material.

💻 All On Screens

The computer they give us is not a notebook either, it is all monitors. I cannot take photos, and I cannot review it at home.

📧 Blocked Email

At the beginning, there was so much to remember that I tried to send notes from the manuals to my own private email address to study, but that was managed in one place too, and I could not send it.

2🧠 Learning at work
🏢 Learn At Work

If I want to remember something, the only way is to remember it while I am at the company.

📓 Only The Meaning

So for the words I did not understand, I write only the meaning in my notebook on the spot, and leave it there.

🌗 A Clean Line

At first I was sad that I could not take things home, but now it is the opposite, it gives me a clear line between work and private life, and that helps.
