This job made me grateful to NTT 🐧

1📡 The big three carriers
🙏 Grateful Now

After working in operations, I became grateful to NTT.

📶 The Big Three

In Japan, the big telecom carriers are NTT, KDDI and SoftBank.

🏛️ Used Government

KDDI and SoftBank started out as private companies. But NTT is the one that used to be run by the government.

2🌾 Even the deep countryside
📜 An Obligation

So apparently there is a law saying that no matter how rural the land is, they have to provide phone lines there.

🏙️ Where Crowds

Usually, a company puts the network where the city centres are, where people gather.

🌾 Deep Country

But NTT is the one that has put it in even for the really rural parts.

💚 My Reading

The way I read it, if you can use the internet out in the Japanese countryside, that is thanks to NTT in a big way.
