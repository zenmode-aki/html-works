In the Philippines, scolding someone in front of others is a taboo 🐧

🎓 The Training Story

When I was about to start working in the Philippines, the training had a story that shocked me.

🤫 A Strong Taboo

In the Philippines, there is apparently a strong culture, or rather a taboo, against scolding someone in front of other people.

💗 Someone's Pride

Hurting the other person's pride is apparently a seriously rude thing to do.

🌏 A Good Culture

Learning that as a culture, as a Japanese person, I thought it was a really good culture to have.

🛣 On The Street

And it's true, walking around in the Philippines, I hardly ever saw two people shouting at each other.

🏪 Shops And Work

I never saw anyone blowing up at a shop over a complaint, and at work I never saw a Filipino person getting seriously angry either.

🍽 Back In Japan

In Japan, younger people don't seem to get that angry, but you do sometimes see an older man losing it in a restaurant.

🕊 Staying Calm

Staying calm with the person in front of you, never blowing up, and getting through each day with peaceful, constructive relationships.

🎯 A Good Thing

I thought that was a really good thing.
