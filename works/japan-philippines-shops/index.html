Inside the Store

Many people outside. How many workers inside?
🇯🇵 Back In Japan

I used to live in the Philippines.
After I came back to Japan, I often thought that Japan had few workers.
Of course, at Tokyo, Osaka, or Nagoya Station, there are so many people that I think, “How many people are here?”
But I feel it most when I enter a store.

🇵🇭 Three At Once

When I lived in Cebu, about three people stood at one register counter.
One scanned barcodes, one handled payment, and one put things in bags.
Sometimes there were three or four people, including people who did not seem related to the work.
They also chatted with each other while they worked.

🍽️ One Does All

Japan is not like that.
Usually, one person does every job.
Restaurants are the same.
In the Philippines, workers talked or looked a little free, so I could catch one quickly.
But calling a worker in a Japanese restaurant is quite hard.
I think, “They look busy now.”

🙇 Customer Worries Too

There are no workers who look free.
The customer worries about the worker.
I think this is a strange scene.
