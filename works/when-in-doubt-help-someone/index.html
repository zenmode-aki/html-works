When I do not know what to do, I just do something that helps someone 🎁

1🌱 A simple principle
📏 Simple Principle

A simple principle 🐧

😊 To Be Happy

If you want to become happy, the only way is to take actions where you feel you are contributing.

🤲 Help Others

In other words, when you are not sure what to do, you should take an action that contributes to other people.

2🤝 In my own life
🔁 Looking Back

Looking back on my own life too, when I am only thinking about myself, in the end my thinking often becomes negative. Compared to that, when I am working, or when I am doing something for someone, I can feel happier.

👉 When Unsure

So when I am not sure what to do, I think I should just do something that looks useful to someone.

🎁 Making People Happy

I have heard the phrase "life is a contest of making each other happy" from an influencer.
