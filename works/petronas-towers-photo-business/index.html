At the Petronas Twin Towers, people do a small business of taking your photo 🏙

🏙 The Classic Spot

It is very standard, but I came to the Petronas Twin Towers.

🏆 World's Tallest

It seems these Petronas Twin Towers were the tallest buildings in the world until 2004.

📸 Photo, Photo

There was something I found interesting at the Petronas Twin Towers.

When you go to the bottom of the towers, there are so many local people.

I thought they might be beggars.

But that was not it.

They are saying things like "Photo, photo."

There are many people who say they will take your photo, and they show photos, like "If I take it, the photo comes out this beautiful."

💡 A Small Business

I thought, "Oh, there is that kind of small business."

Indeed, there were people nearby making small money by taking good photos, and I thought, "I see."

I did not have them take my photo, and I ignored them, though.

If you go to the bottom of the twin towers, people will talk to you like that.

⛲ KLCC Park

Under the Petronas Twin Towers, there is a shopping mall.

If you go straight through that shopping mall, on the other side, there is a park called KLCC.

KLCC means Kuala Lumpur City Centre.

🏁 Finally Here

The KLCC park was also calm and very nice.

Honestly, when I was looking things up before my Malaysia trip, I saw pictures of these Petronas Twin Towers again and again.

So I felt like I finally came to the place I saw on the internet, and I felt a little sense of achievement.
