The best shift I had was during the baseball tournament 🐧

1🎉 A festival day
⚾ The Best Day

If you ask which day was the most fun, it was the day of the World Baseball Classic.

🇯🇵 As Japanese

As a Japanese person, working the shift on the day of the baseball WBC was fun.

🎉 Like Festival

On that day the whole country turns into something like a festival. Japanese people really love baseball.

🗣️ All Talking

Talking about baseball with everyone, keeping an eye on the live scores, but also watching the alarms, all of us chatting away while we worked.

2📈 The internet traffic
📈 The Traffic

The traffic was interesting too.

📺 Everyone Streaming

The 2026 WBC was streamed online, and from around 7pm to 10pm, everyone connects to the internet to watch that stream.

🔴 Red And Yellow

When that happens, you can see the internet usage in red and yellow.

👀 Watching It

You could tell across the board that usage was high in that time window, and think: almost everyone is probably watching the WBC right now. Monitoring that was fun too.

🛡️ Special Setup

Because so many people across the country watch the stream, a slightly special setup was put in place so the network would not go down. That made it interesting.

3🍕 Pizza together
🍕 Pizza Together

And that day they ordered pizza and delivery for us, and we all ate it together while working. It is a good memory.
