DDP in Seoul felt like a pure white planet 🛸

🇰🇷 South Korea🏙️ Seoul✈️ Travel🚶 Walks🎬 Video
🛸 Famous Building

I went to DDP, a famous design place in Korea.

When you think of DDP, first, the shape of the building is interesting, right?

🎨 No Art Sense

But I do not have enough art sense to be charmed by the art of the DDP building.

Even from an amateur's eyes, it just feels like an interesting building.

My art sense🎨
🏃 Game Of Tag

So, why did I want to go to DDP...

There is a YouTuber group called Comdot that is popular in Japan, and they did a game of tag at DDP.

I watched that video, and I wanted to see the real DDP, so I went.

🏃🏃‍♂️
🤍 White Planet

You could go inside DDP for free, too.

🆓 Free🤫 Quiet🌌 Lots of space

It was very quiet, it used the space in a luxurious way, and it was artistic.

It felt like I came to a planet other than Earth, or it was a pure white world.

🙂 Still Fun

If I knew more about art, maybe I could have enjoyed it more.

But even as an amateur, it was fun.
