The work went overseas because it cost less, so I sat in the middle 🐧

🏗 Asked A Lot

"Why not just build it in Japan?" People often asked me that.

💴 The Simple Reason

The reason we used an overseas team was simple: it costs less. A team in Southeast Asia can be cheaper than hiring a Japanese engineer.

🧱 The Gap Stays

But the language and culture gap stays. When you work closely together every week, that part gets worrying.

🌉 In The Middle

Someone like me sat in the middle to take that worry away.

🎯 One Bridge

Put one bridge in place, and suddenly you can build things together with a team overseas. Looking at it that way, it was a pretty good role to have.
