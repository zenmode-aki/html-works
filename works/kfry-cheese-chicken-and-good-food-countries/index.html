Cheese chicken at K Fry, and how to tell if a country's food is good 🧀

🍽 Dinner Plan

At the pyramid mall, I wondered which shop to eat dinner at.

I asked AI and looked up popular shops.

It said that "K Fry," a Korean fried chicken shop in the mall, is popular.

🧀 Cheese Chicken

When I went in, they said something in Korean, probably like "Welcome."

I ordered Korean cheese chicken.

You eat fried chicken coated with cheese.

First, they grill and melt the cheese in front of you.

Then the staff mixes the chicken and cheese together in front of you.

They did it very well, and it was really delicious.

🌍 How To Tell

Talking about this reminded me of something.

It seems there is a way to judge whether a country's food is delicious or not.

You can tell if a country's food is delicious or not by whether there are restaurants of that country's food.

🍣 Found Everywhere

For example, Japanese, French, Italian, Chinese, and Korean food is everywhere in the world, right?

It seems that means that country's food is delicious.

On the other hand, if you do not see that country's food much in the world, that country's food is not thought to be very delicious.

But maybe people all over the world just have not noticed its charm yet.

That is what it seems to be.

📺 From YouTube

I think it was something a Japanese YouTuber living abroad said.

When you move abroad and worry, "Is the food there good?" or "What if the food does not suit me?"

You can tell if it is good by whether you see that country's food in Japan.

I remember it as a small piece of trivia.

After all, Korean food is all over the world, so Korean food is delicious.
