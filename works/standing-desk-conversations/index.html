🙋 I Like It

I like standing desks quite a lot, and I am the type who likes working on my feet. But teachers, and students walking past, always asked me why I was standing.

💬 So Much Chatting

🪑 Easier To Approach

When someone is sitting and working normally it is hard to talk to them, but when someone is working standing up, it is a bit unusual, and you want to say something, right?

🗣 It Worked

I really did want to communicate with the students and the staff around me, so from then on I kept working standing up. And after that, people talked to me a lot more.
