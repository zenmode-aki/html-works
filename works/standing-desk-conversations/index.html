I worked standing up, and people started talking to me 🐧

1🧍 My standing desk
🧍 Standing Desk

When I was a manager at a language school in Clark, I really wanted a standing desk, so there was a period when I put my laptop on the counter and worked standing up.

❓ Why Standing

When I did that, everyone always asked me, "Why are you working standing up over there? Why don't you sit down?"

🙋 I Like It

I like standing desks quite a lot, and I am the type who likes working on my feet, but the teachers and the students passing by always asked me why I was standing.

2💬 So much small talk
💬 So Much Chatting

Because of that I could have a lot of small talk, so standing up actually got many people to speak to me, and I thought that was good. That made me happy.

🪑 Easier To Approach

When someone is just sitting and working it is hard to talk to them, but if there is someone working standing up, it is a bit unusual and you want to talk to them, right?

🗣 It Worked

I also did want to communicate with the students and the staff around me, so after that I decided to work standing up. And then people started talking to me a lot.
