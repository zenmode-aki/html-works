💇 WHAT MY HAIRDRESSER TAUGHT ME

Korean idols push their bangs up but leave a few strands on the sides to look smaller in the face 💫

💡 Salon Trivia

I want to share a little trivia I heard from my hairdresser at the salon.

⬆️ Up, With Strands

Korean idols these days push their bangs up, right?

But they leave really just 3 or 4, or about 10 strands of side hair falling on purpose.

🤔 Why Not Up?

Why do they do this? It seems that by leaving about 3 strands falling at the sides, the face looks smaller.

It seems your face looks smaller, a bit slimmer, and you make a better impression.

If I feel like it, maybe next time I will try leaving a few strands at the sides of my bangs.
