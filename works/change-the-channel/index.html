Too much dark news grows worry, so change the channel 📺

🧠 Psychology🫧 Feelings💡 Tips
📰 Too Much News

It is said a big cause of worrying too much is taking in too much dark news.

✈️ Same Flight

People who watch news about a plane accident feel worried about their trip. People who watch a show about airplane food look forward to their trip.
