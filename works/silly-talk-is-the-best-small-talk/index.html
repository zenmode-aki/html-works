Small talk does not need smart topics; silly talk is the best 🍙

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😊 Happiness
😓 Trying Too Hard

If you think, "I must talk about something smart," small talk makes you tired.

🫠 Loose Is Fine

Small talk is for enjoying loose, silly chatting.

Something like "Which rice ball flavor is the strongest?" is fine.
