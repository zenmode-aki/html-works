🏫 The Job

There was a time when I worked as the Japanese manager at a language school in Clark, in the Philippines.

🤝 Day One

On my first day, the owner told me: "Aki, your job is to become friends with the study abroad agents." That was the whole explanation.

📋 More Than Classes

Part of my job was managing lessons, like telling a teacher to give a certain student a certain kind of class. But it was not only that. Dealing with the cleaning staff and the maintenance staff, and with the real estate company that managed the dorm, was part of the job too.

📞 The Biggest Job

The biggest part of the job was answering the study abroad agents in Japan when they asked me to introduce the school to them.

🙏 Full Of Gratitude

It was so much fun, and it was a time I am full of gratitude for.
