There are 3 kinds of people I deal with 🐧

👥 Three Kinds

In this job, there are broadly 3 kinds of people I deal with.

🏢 The Customers

The first is the customers. Local governments, company IT departments, hospitals, factories, shops.

📞 Their Reports

We get reports like: the network is down, only one site cannot connect, the connection is unstable.

🔧 Field Engineers

The second is the field engineers who go to the site. We have them measure the light level, or check the lamps on the equipment, while I look at the documents and tell them: next, please check here.

🌐 Other Carriers

The third is the other carriers. When a customer's sites are spread across the whole country, we are not providing every section ourselves.

🤝 Working Together

So we ask them, could you check the state of your section, and we isolate the fault together with the other company.

🕸️ So Many

Networks really are held up by a lot of companies and equipment connected together where you cannot see them. I feel that a lot while working.
