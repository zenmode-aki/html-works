The Working Side

The same shift, with three fewer people
🏢

This continues from last time. This time is about the difference when I became a worker. When I worked overseas, honestly, just going to the office felt like work. Even talking with co-workers was valued to some degree, and the mood said that was enough.

⏱️

My work had some room. I did not keep working at the limit. After I came back to Japan, I thought every place lacked workers. People handle many kinds of work, and overtime is normal.

🍺

I heard an example somewhere. Around the 1980s bubble economy, imagine a Japanese bar that needed eight people for one shift. Today, because the population fell, it feels like five people run it. Losing three people hurts a lot. This is happening now in many places in Japan.
