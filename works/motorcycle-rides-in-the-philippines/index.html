🚕 Cheaper Than Taxis

Taxis are cheap in the Philippines, but there is a way to get around even cheaper than a taxi.

It is the motorcycle.

With a motorcycle, you can get around for about 1/3 of the taxi fare.

When I was in the Philippines, I used these motorcycles a lot.
