📢 Also My Job

When a fault happens, writing “network outage in progress” on our website is also part of my job.

network outage in progress
😰 Never Gets Easy

No matter how used to this I get, it is difficult, and on top of that I have to share it quickly inside the company too, including the directors and the management.

🌙 First 30 Minutes

The night shift starts at 4:30pm, and for the first 30 minutes the whole team does a simulation that we call “training time”.

16:30 → 17:00
🎭 Right Before Sending

We imagine a fault that is not real, and we practise preparing the text to put on the website and the internal message, right up to the point just before actually sending it.

✋ Hands Remember

While repeating this, it started to feel less like thinking with my head, and more like my hands already remember it.
