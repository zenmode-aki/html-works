🇵🇭 LIVING IN THE PHILIPPINES⚡ 15 SEC

I filmed the inside of my studio room right before I moved out 🐧

🚪 Inside The Room

This is about the inside of the room I lived in.

🛏 One Room Only

It was a studio. Just the one room.

💵 The Rent

The rent was about 25,000 pesos, which is around 400 US dollars today.

📐 4 Single Beds

For size, maybe about as much floor as 4 single beds. It was a fairly small room.

🪑 Furniture Included

The furniture came with it from the start.

🧺 The First Tenant

And apparently I was the first person to move in, so on the day I arrived they got me a washing machine, kitchen things, all sorts.

🙏 Very Grateful

I'm really grateful to the owner for that.

🧍 My Standing Desk

The place I spent the longest inside that room was in front of my standing desk.

📺 TV And Meals

I often connected the TV to YouTube and watched it, eating my meals standing up while it played.

🪟 Out The Window

From the window, you could see the open space on the 7th floor.

👀 Watching From Above

"Oh, someone's walking a dog." "Someone's doing yoga." I'd peer down at them a bit from above.

🎬 Right Before Leaving

By the way, I filmed this video right before I moved out.

💾 Keeping A Memory

I'd lived in Cebu, and I wanted to keep that as a memory, so I just sort of made a video of it.

🎯 Never Showed Anyone

I never showed it to anyone, and I didn't really post it either.
