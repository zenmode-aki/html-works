🇵🇭 LIVING IN THE PHILIPPINES⚡ 15 SEC

There was a concierge on the ground floor of my building in Cebu 🐧

🏨 The Ground Floor

This is about the ground floor of the condominium.

🛎 The Big Desk

First, there's a big concierge desk, right there in the middle.

👮 3 Staff

There was a receptionist and a security guard too, so there were always about 3 staff on the ground floor. One concierge, one guard, and one other staff member.

👋 Saying Hello

Whenever you go out, you have to pass through there, so you say hello to the concierge and the security person before you step outside.

🇯🇵 Not Back Home

That turned out to be a habit we don't really have back home.

💰 Very Expensive Here

A building with a concierge in Japan is, I think, probably very expensive.

🌱 A Good Habit

Saying hello to someone every single day before going out. Looking back, I think that habit was really good for me.

🌳 The Park Outside

Step out of the building and there was a park-like space right in front, and sometimes a football school was running there.

⚽ Kids Playing Football

Watching the kids playing football was fun too.

🚪 People Come Through

Also, when you're on the ground floor, all sorts of people come through.

👂 Hearing Japanese

Sometimes I'd hear Japanese and realise, oh, there were Japanese people living in this building too.

🌏 More Western People

In Cebu, my impression was that there were more Western people.

🗺 Before That Island

Before that I lived on Luzon, the island Manila is on, and there it was more people from China, Taiwan and Korea. I didn't hear much Japanese there.

🏝 More Japanese Here

Cebu felt like it had more Japanese people.

🏊 The Pool Too

You can also get to the pool from the ground floor.

📦 No Amazon

The Philippines doesn't have Amazon. You shop with apps called Lazada and Shopee, and the concierge receives the parcels for you.

💧 Water By Gallon

For water, you didn't have to go out to a convenience store either. You could buy it for about 50 pesos a gallon.

🎯 Really Convenient

Living with a concierge downstairs was, I kept thinking, really convenient.
