Expecting is like leaning on a wall that can move at any time 🧱

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset🫧 Feelings
🧱 What It Is

To "expect" something is to lean on the result or on other people's hearts.

🛋️ Easy At First

When you lean, if things go wrong, you can be "poor me, pushed around," so it is a little easy.

⚡ TRY IT
The wind and the wall
The penguin is leaning on an "I expect…" wall. Let the wind blow.
Leaning feels so easy… 😌 Oops! The wall moved. 💫 I am standing on my own feet. The wall moved, but I am OK!
🐧
They will reply soon. I will surely win. They will praise me.
💫 Falls: 0 · 🐾 Steady: 0 / 3
🎉 The wall still moves. But now you do not fall.
🌬️ Then It Hurts

But every time the wall moves, you wobble, and little by little it gets hard.

🐾 Own Feet

If you stand on your own feet, you do not fall when the wall moves.
