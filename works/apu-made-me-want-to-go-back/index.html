After seeing APU, I wanted to go to university too 🎓

✈️ The Trip

On the Malaysia trip...

I booked appointments at the universities my sister is thinking about, took her there, and listened to their explanations... and in the end, I found myself thinking: I want to go too!!!

💻 APU Campus

Especially APU (Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation). It moved me a lot.

But I feel I am already too old to go back to university now...
