🐧 EVERYDAY LIFE⚡ 15 SEC

I realized that when I get anxious, I am not running away, I am just freezing 🐧

🧊 Not Even Escaping

I might not even have been good at running away.

🍫 Chocolate And Videos

When I get anxious, I eat chocolate. 🍫 And then I end up watching a lot of videos.

🌫 Called It Escaping

I wanted to get even a little distance from reality, so I thought that doing those inertia-driven things counted as "running away."

🤔 Maybe Not

But maybe that was not quite right.

🚶 Escaping Is Moving

I think running away is, in a way, about "moving somewhere."

📍 Going Nowhere

Even if I eat chocolate, even if I keep watching videos, I am not going anywhere. I am just sitting there, watching time melt away.

🧊 Just Freezing

Maybe I am not running away. Maybe I am just freezing.

⚔️ Fight Or Flight

There is this idea that when people feel fear or anxiety, they either "fight or flee." For me, it might look something more like this.

🧭 The Three States

Fight ⚔️ → Freeze 🧊 → Flee 🏃

🧊 Usually Freeze

And I usually end up choosing to "freeze."

📦 The Move

When I think about moving next month and get anxious, I freeze.

😶 Stuck In Place

Even when I start thinking about something negative, I cannot even manage to run away properly, and I just stay frozen in place.

❓ A New Question

So from now on, when I get anxious, I want to try asking myself: "Am I fighting this right now?" "Am I running away?" "Or am I freezing?"

🎯 A Small Win

I gained one more option to ask myself. Just that alone felt like a small win. 🙂‍↕️
