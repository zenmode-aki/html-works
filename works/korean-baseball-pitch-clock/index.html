Korean pro baseball has a pitch clock ⏱️

⏱️ A Pitch Clock

Korean pro baseball had a pitch clock.

⚾ What It Is

Baseball is a sport where games are just long.

Maybe because of that, Major League Baseball has a rule: “The pitcher must throw the next ball within about 15 to 20 seconds.”

This is the pitch clock.

20
PITCH CLOCKThrow before it hits 0
👀 Korea Has It

In the photo, the “13” shining next to the scoreboard is the time left.

I was surprised that Korean pro baseball has this too.

Actually, Japanese pro baseball does not have a pitch clock.

🇺🇸 MLB⏱️ Yes
🇰🇷 Korea⏱️ Yes
🇯🇵 Japan✖️ No
🧠 Mind Games

So games in Japan are a little longer.

The pitcher stands still for about 20 seconds without throwing, and plays a mind game with the batter.

That may also be part of what makes Japanese baseball Japanese.

🌏 The WBC

But because of that, it seems Japan has a hard time at international tournaments.

There is a tournament called the WBC (World Baseball Classic), like a World Cup for baseball.

Japan is a country with very strong pitchers.

Back when there was no pitch clock, Japanese pitchers were hard to hit, and Japan has won the tournament.

📰 Hit More Now

But after the pitch clock came in, Japanese pitchers could not keep up with that tempo and started getting hit.

I have read a news article like that.
