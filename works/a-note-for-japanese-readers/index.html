A message from the owner to people reading in Japanese 🐧

🌐 Translated By AI

The Japanese version of this blog is made by having AI translate into Japanese the posts I first wrote in English.

🎯 You Are Sharp

“Hey, you are Japanese, so translate it properly yourself.”

If you thought that, you are sharp.

🧠 My Bad Habit

But Japanese is my native language.

That is exactly why, the moment I start writing it myself, my bad habit, “perfectionism,” turns on.

🌀 Going In Circles

“This wording feels a bit off.”

“No, this expression is better…”

⏳ Never Finished

Once I start thinking about things like that, the post is never finished.

🐧 Left To Pengesso

So I leave the Japanese version to the AI, “Pengesso.”

📧 Tell Pengesso

Because of that, the Japanese may sometimes be a mess.

If you find a spot where you think “Pengesso really messed up here,” please send an email to pengesso@gmail.com.

🤫 Quietly Fixed

As soon as I see it, I will quietly fix it.
