🇵🇭 LIVING IN THE PHILIPPINES⚡ 15 SEC

Picking students up at the airport was a tug of war with the heat 🐧

✈️ Airport Pickup

The thing I was always afraid of getting wrong, in my job as a language school manager in Clark, was picking students up at the airport.

🕐 Never Late

I often went to pick students up at Clark International Airport, and I never wanted to be late for a pickup.

☀️ Too Hot Outside

But arriving too early was hard too, because it is quite hot outside the airport. At airports in Japan, like Kansai or Narita, ordinary people can just walk inside. But in the Philippines, you cannot get into the airport without a passport or a boarding pass. People with no ticket cannot wander in, so they have to wait outside in the heat.

⚖️ Tug Of War

That was the tug of war. I did not want to be late, but waiting outside for a long time was hard, so I was always checking which flight the student was coming in on.

🇰🇷 Flights From Korea

There were so many flights from Korea, so many routes connecting Clark and Korea, that telling one flight from another was quite hard. It is funny to think about now.

🗣 Hard To Pronounce

Also, as a Japanese person, telling Korean and Chinese names apart was very hard, and I struggled a lot at first. I never knew how to pronounce "Are you Mr. so and so?" Japanese names are easy, but that part was difficult.

😊 Actually Fun

But airport pickups were basically fun. While waiting I met all kinds of interesting people, and I also met managers from other language schools during pickups, and became friends and talked with them, so I have fairly fun memories of that time.
