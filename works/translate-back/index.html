🔁 Back Again

After translating, I would translate it back again.

✍️ The Easy Part

Turning an engineer's English report into Japanese, making it sound natural in Japanese, that part I could do.

⚠️ The Risk

But if it sounded natural while the technical meaning had shifted, that would be a problem.

↩️ Back To English

So I'd take the finished Japanese, turn it back into English, and have the person who wrote it check, "is this what you meant?"

🧘 Double The Work

It was completely double the work, but on the days I did it, I could hand it to the client feeling settled.

🎯 Still Good

Even now, I think that way of checking wasn't a bad one.
