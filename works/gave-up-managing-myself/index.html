I gave up trying to manage myself perfectly 🗓

🧊 The Realisation

I noticed that managing myself completely is impossible.

🗓 Fifteen Minutes

I used to cut my whole day into 15-minute pieces in Notion and Google Calendar, so that I would not waste a single minute.

⏱ 15 min × 48
🏳️ So I Stopped

In the end it did not work. So I gave up managing myself perfectly.

☁️ Instead, Now

Now, instead, every day I think lightly and abstractly about what I kept in mind that day.
