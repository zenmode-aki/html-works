The steps to go to a university in Malaysia from Japan 🏫

🤔 Going Abroad

How do you go to a university abroad? 🐧

Right. You have no idea at all, right? When I was a high school student, I did not know anything, so much that I could not even have the choice of going to a university abroad.

🤖 It Is 2026

But now it is already 2026! There is Claude Code 🙂‍↕️

In other words, it is a time when you can do anything, if it is the kind of research you do on a computer.

📝 What We Did

This time, I will roughly write what we did so that my sister can go to a university abroad.

First, this is what we did to choose a university.

🌏 Step 1

Choose the country

My family is an ordinary Japanese family, so we gave up on universities in countries with high tuition, like Britain, America and Australia.

When we looked into it, we found out that Malaysia seems to have a good reputation for education, and also that you can go there for about the same cost as going to a private university in Japan.

So we decided to aim for a university in Malaysia.

🏫 Step 2

Pick some universities

We ended up with 4 candidates.

Taylor's University — Lakeside, Subang Jaya

INTI International College/University — Subang

Sunway University — Bandar Sunway

APU (Asia Pacific University) — Bukit Jalil

✉️ Step 3
