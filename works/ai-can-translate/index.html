📜 Before And Now

It's true that in the past, documents had to be translated by people. But these days we have AI.

⚡ The Plain Fact

AI can translate.

🫧 My Own Question

So "what am I even doing here" was a question that stayed with me, honestly, the whole time I was working there.

🗣 Live Interpreting

For something like live interpreting in a meeting, there may still be some point in a person doing it.

📄 Just The Documents

But translating documents? A machine can seriously just do that.

👀 Noticing Early

Still, it was because I worked hard at it that I noticed early on: this is going to be replaced by AI.

💭 So I Thought

That got me thinking.

🛠 Which To Build

I thought it would be better to work on technical skill than on language skill.

🙊 Not About English

It's not so much that I can't speak English. In my case, anything I can't say in Japanese, I can't say in English either.

🇯🇵 My Own Language

If I can't say it in my own language, I can't say it in English.

🧱 Solid First

So I thought I should make the technical side solid first.

🌍 A Vague Feeling

I did have a vague feeling that I wanted to work globally, but a Japanese company would be completely fine too.

💗 Even A Little

Still, I like English, so it would be nice to work somewhere I can use English even a little. That's the vague direction I have.

🧩 Any Environment

And for that, if I don't properly understand the technology, I probably can't contribute much in any environment at all.

For the me of 5 years, 6 years, 7 years from now to do well overseas, or somewhere English is used, technical skill is what matters more. I felt that strongly.
