🇵🇭 LIVING IN THE PHILIPPINES⚡ 15 SEC

A run of holidays meant a Saturday shift, and a table of food we all ate with our hands 🐧

📅 Extra Saturday

Some contracts require you to work at least 18 days a month. But in the Philippines, holidays can line up back to back, so sometimes we still had to come in on a Saturday to make that number.

🤝 A Small Group

Most people wanted to work remotely on a Saturday, so very few of us actually came into the office. With fewer people around, we ended up eating lunch together.

✋ Eating By Hand

I already knew people ate like this, but eating with my own hands for the first time still felt strange, in a good way.

🍉 Kind Coworkers

The food itself was delicious. We ate a lot of watermelon and drank a lot too, and the people next to me kept giving me the best parts.

🥩 Mostly Pork

In Cebu, I noticed most people around me ate a lot of pork.

🍦 Ice Cream Box

And everyone loved ice cream. In Japan I would buy a single-serving cup, but the office freezer had big tubs of it, and everyone dug in during breaks. Finding tubs like that in Japan is hard, maybe only at Costco, so seeing it eaten so casually at work was a fun surprise.
