🛠 NETWORK OPERATIONS⚡ 15 SEC

I walked to work through a road that had turned into a river 🌊

1🌧 Record rain
🐧 Most Fun Commute

There is a day I would call the most interesting commute of my life.

🌧 Record Rain

On 8 September 2026, record-breaking heavy rain fell in Nagoya, and all the transport in the city stopped.

🌊 Like A River

The road in front of my workplace flooded to about below the knee, about 40cm, and it had completely become like a river.

about 40cm
2🚶 I just walked to work
🚶 I Just Walked

I lived within walking distance of my workplace, so although I got soaked, I just went to work as usual.

👏 Everyone Clapped

Just for coming to work, the whole office clapped for me.

🚇 Nothing Was Running

That is because the subway, the buses and the JR trains had all stopped, and almost none of the members of my team could get to work.

🏫 Students Slept There

As for how amazing the rain was, at my younger sister’s high school, students who could not get home ended up sleeping at the school.

3🚨 A very long shift
🚨 Alarms Everywhere

That day was a night shift, and because of the rain the alarms on the network equipment were incredible, so it was very busy, and I clocked out at about 12:30 the next afternoon.

16:30 → 12:30
⏳ Almost 24 Hours

A night shift is normally from 4:30pm until 9:30am the next day, with 2 hours of breaks in between, so about 15 hours of work. But on this day I worked until the point where 4 more hours would have made a full day, 24 hours.

4🌊 The fun part
😄 The Fun Part

The interesting part was that I really did walk to work through that road that had become like a river, soaking in the water.

💧 Water Was Gone

And the thing I quietly thought was amazing was that by the time I clocked out, that flooding had already completely drained away.

🏙 Really Impressive

I really think the drainage of Nagoya is amazing.

🤨 Then Again

(Although, if the drainage of Nagoya really were that good, maybe this flooding would not have happened in the first place. Ha.)
