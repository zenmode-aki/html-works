The 100ml rule on planes is about the size of the container, not what is inside 🧴

1🧴 It's the container
📚 Today I Learned

What I learned today 🐧

🧴 The Container

The 100ml rule on planes was about the container 😳! It is not about what is inside.

🔢 Only 50ml Inside

For example, even if there is only 50ml inside, if the container can hold more than 100ml, it is not allowed.

😳 Did Not Know

I did not know that at all 😳

2🛫 At Kansai Airport
🛑 Stopped At Airport

Because of that, a staff member stopped me at Kansai Airport.

💊 The Drugstore

But luckily, Kansai Airport has a drugstore on the 2nd floor, and they sell empty containers of 100ml or less, so you can just move your liquid there and it is OK.
