Decide "if this, then that," and habits are easier to keep 🚦

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity💡 Tips
📝 Write It

Decide "If I do X, then I do Y," and write it on paper.

🔬 Research Says

It seems research has shown that this makes habits easier to keep.

🚦 Brain Rules

This is because the brain moves with "if" rules, like "If the light is red, stop."

🔴 If red→🛑 stop
😤 For Example

For example, "If I feel irritated, I take 3 deep breaths."

⚡ TRY IT
The if-then slot machine
Spin! When "if" and "then" match, you get a good plan. Pin just 1.
