Saying thank you is rare, so people who say it really shine 💎

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends😊 Happiness
📉 Number One Reason

It seems the No. 1 reason people get disliked by accident is "too few thank-yous."

🗣️ Two Leaders

Leader A says, "It looks nice, thank you, it helps," and Leader Z says, "Put it there, and this too."

⭐"Thank you, it helps!"
🌵"Put it there."
🤝 Easy Choice

Of course, you want to be with Leader A.

💎 Rare Thing

Many people want thanks, but few people give thanks.

✨ You Shine

So just by saying "thank you," you can shine.
