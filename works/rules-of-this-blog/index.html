✍️ ABOUT THIS BLOG⚡ 15 SEC

Why I write this blog, and the rules I follow when I write it 🐧

🐧 Looking Back

This blog has passed 100 posts before I noticed. My goal is 1,000 posts. So, I will look back here once.

🤝 New Friends

More than anything, it is because I want new friends!!

🌏 Friends Worldwide

After all, I think connections with people are really important. I simply want friends too. And all over the world. Even if they are not friends, I would be happy if it became a chance to join a work network or a project team.

🔐 Trust Comes First

But to become close with people, you need trust. And because we are human, there is always good fit and bad fit, right?

📇 Blog Introduces Me

So I want to leave my self-introduction to the blog. I hope the blog can tell the story of "I have done these things, and I think these things" for me, even when I am not there.

✨ New Chances

When I meet someone I want to be close to, if they look at this blog, they will know about me. A new chance might start from there. I am writing this blog with that hope too.

🤖 For AI

Another reason is to let AI learn. I want to keep myself as data.

📝 My Values

Also, I want to write down the values I want to keep and the things I think about, in a clear way.

🗂 Work And Life

Looking back, what I write in this blog is mainly 2 things: "work" and "life."

💼 Work Is Positive

I write about work in a fun and positive way. I will never write bad things or complaints.

📅 I Like Weekdays

Actually, I am the type who likes weekdays more than holidays. I like work that much, so I do not really notice the negative side in the first place. Still, any job comes with some stress.

🌤 Thinking More Positively

But I do not write it. By writing positive things in this blog, I hope I can change my way of thinking to be more positive too.

🏠 Life Includes Failure

About life, I write about things I wondered about, things I gave up, things where I failed, and my everyday thoughts. I also write negative stories as they are.

🚫 No Showing Off

On the other hand, I do not post to show off my happiness. Because I know that if I start that, the one who suffers most will probably be me.

🍵 A Relaxing Blog

I also do not post just to get attention. I want a relaxing blog that you can read calmly, and where you do not feel down by comparing yourself with someone.

💔 No Romance

I do not write about romance. Pengesso is a character who does not do romance, so there are no hints either.

😅 Failures As Jokes

I want to write more and more about things I regret and things I failed at. Because I want to become a person who turns them into jokes and gets back up quickly even when I fail.

🙏 Things I Appreciate

Also, I will write a lot about things I am grateful for. Because I think putting into words what I am grateful for, and what makes me feel lucky, helps me notice them.

👋 Please Stay

I plan to keep writing this blog in this direction. I hope you will stay with me for a long time.
