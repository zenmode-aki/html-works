Why I write this blog, and my rules 🐧

🤝 New Friends

I write this blog because I want new friends!

I want to meet friends, and people I can work with, from all over the world. I would be happy if this blog introduces me, even when I am not there.

📝 My Rules
✅ I write
  • Work: only general talk about an industry
  • Life: failures and regrets too
  • Things I am grateful for
❌ I do not write
  • Showing off my happiness
  • Posts just to get attention
  • Romance (Pengesso does not do romance)
  • The company I work for, and what I do at work
👋 Please Stay

I will keep writing this way. I hope you will stay with me for a long time.
