🇵🇭 LIVING IN THE PHILIPPINES⚡ 15 SEC

Cabbage with black pepper was the best thing I cooked out there 🐧

🍛 Strong Flavours

Back then Filipino food was delicious, but the flavours were quite strong, and to a Japanese palate it did not feel like very healthy food.

😅 A Bit Worried

Even when I ate at the language school, everything was strongly flavoured, and as delicious as it was, I started to worry about my health.

🍳 My Own Kitchen

There was a kitchen inside my dorm room, so I would fry cabbage there and eat it.

🌶 Just Black Pepper

That alone was delicious. Cabbage and black pepper, and nothing else, was genuinely delicious.

🥬 Because Of Contrast

Exactly because I was usually eating strongly flavoured food at the school or eating out, this simple cabbage seasoned with only black pepper tasted amazing when I ate it once in a while.

🍽 Once A Week

I made this about once or twice a week. I would munch my way through the cabbage.
