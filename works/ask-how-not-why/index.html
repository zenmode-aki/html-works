Ask "How can I?" instead of "Why?" when things go wrong 💡

🧠 Psychology💭 Mindset💡 Tips
😮‍💨 Why Question

When you think "Why can't I lose weight?", only a sigh comes out.

💡 How Question

When you think "How can I lose weight?", ideas come out, like "I will make a lunch box" and "I will use the stairs."

😮‍💨Why…?
💡How can I?
⚡ TRY IT
💡 Push "Why?" or "How can I?"
