🌊 Yoro Falls

I went to Yoro Falls to heal.

Yoro Falls is a place in Gifu Prefecture, famous for its waterfall, about 1 hour from Nagoya.

🌿 So Relaxing

Around the waterfall, I felt a lot of negative ions.

When you are surrounded by nature this beautiful, your heart and body relax naturally.

😊 Smiling Alone

I went there alone.

Walking in the forest, I was probably smiling the whole time.

I was happy that nature was kept this beautifully.

💧 Fully Refreshed

I remember I sweated a good amount and felt very refreshed.
