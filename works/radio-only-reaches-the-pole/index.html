I used to think my phone talked to space 🐧

🙃 Pretty Clueless

Before I joined network operations, I was pretty clueless.

🛰️ Straight Up

I thought that when you use the internet on a phone or a computer, the phone shoots radio waves whoosh, all the way up into space, and that is how it communicates.

📡 Only The Pole

But it is nothing like that. The radio my phone sends only reaches something called a base station, on a utility pole nearby.

🔌 All Cable

From there on, it is all fiber. It is physically connected, with cable.

🌏 Across Oceans

Even from Japan to Brazil or America, it is laid out like a power grid, and one way or another it is all connected.

🕸️ Like Cobweb

The thing is, it is spread out messily like a cobweb, so you get lost in it like a maze.

🧭 Routers Help

What stops you getting lost is the router, and thanks to it you reach your destination properly.

💡 Much Easier

Networks are physically connected. Once you know just that, this job gets a lot easier to understand.
