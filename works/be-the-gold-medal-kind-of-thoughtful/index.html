Being kind is a silver medal, and noticing first is gold 🥇

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends💡 Tips
🥈 Silver Kind

Asking "Shall I buy you a coffee?" is kind.

🥇 Gold Thoughtful

Saying "You're doing your best, here you go" and just handing it over is thoughtful.

🏅 Two Medals

If kind is a silver medal, thoughtful is a gold medal.

🥈"Want a coffee?"
🥇"Here you go. ☕"
👀 Watch, Imagine

What you need is to watch well and to imagine.

⚡ TRY IT
Can you notice first?
Look at your friend. What do they need? Give it before they ask.
🌬️🐧❄️
Your friend sits right under the cold air conditioner.
🚪🐧🌨️
Your friend just came in from the snow.
🛍️🐧🛍️🚪
Your friend's flippers are full of bags, and there is a door.
🥇 Gold! You noticed before they asked.
🥈 Silver. Kind! But they had to think and answer.
😅 Hmm, not quite… Look again!
🏆
Gold medal champion! You watched and imagined.
🥈
So kind! Next time, try to notice before you ask.
🧤 Try This

If you notice, "The air conditioner seems to make them cold," quietly give them a hand warmer.

📚 I learned this from the Japanese blog "Panda no Ondo".
