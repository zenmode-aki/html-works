I Knew More.
I Moved Less.

A story about getting too full of information
🎧
🎧 📲 🌙

I am the kind of person who wears AirPods all day. It is because I want to put some kind of information into my head. While moving, doing housework, and before sleep, I keep Audible, Spotify podcasts, or YouTube playing. Even when I sleep, silence makes me feel that I have to learn something or do something, so listening to news has become my daily habit.

🧠✨ → 🪫

I felt like I was getting smarter. But I was not moving at all. Also, somehow, I was always tired.

Food becomes fat if you do not use it. Things you hear may be the same. If you do not use them, they stay inside as “maybe I will use this someday.” My body was not getting fat, but maybe my mind was getting fatter and fatter.
