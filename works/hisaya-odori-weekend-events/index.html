For sightseeing in Nagoya, go to the weekend events on Hisaya-odori 🎪

🗺 Where To Go?

Where should you go for sightseeing in Nagoya?

I want to talk about that.

🤷 Everything, Nothing

Even for me, living in Nagoya, Nagoya is a city that has everything but has nothing.

For living, it has everything.

But still, it has nothing.

If you ask for "the one place you must go in Nagoya," I think, "Hmm, where would that be?"

🎪 Weekend Events

But if you want to do something fun in Nagoya.

If you want to drop by an event.

I recommend the street in front of the TV tower called Hisaya-odori.

📅 Almost Every Week

Here, there is some kind of event on almost every Saturday and Sunday.

At least on about 1 weekend every 2 weeks, there is some event.

This time, there was a Thai event.

🙌 Just Come

When you want to kill some time in Nagoya, please come on a weekend.

There should be some event going on here.

I think it is nice to have a little fun at that event.
