🏆 C > A > BSurprise! A happy "thank you" is better than splitting.
🎁 Only Taking

If you only say, "Yay, thank you!", you can become someone who only takes.

✂️ Always Splitting

"No, no, let's split it" is very proper, but the connection can get a little thin.

🔁 Give and Take

The best answer is "Thank you! Next time is my treat."

You get, you give back, and the friendship keeps going.
