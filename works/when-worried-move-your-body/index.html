When you worry, your body stops, so move it on purpose 👟

🧠 Psychology🚶 Walks💭 Mindset
🧊 Frozen Body

When people worry, their bodies stop moving.

🔄 Do the Opposite

So it seems good to do the opposite and move your muscles on purpose.

While you move your body, your worries get smaller.

🧹 Any Move

Exercise, cleaning, a walk, or shopping is all OK.

⚡ TRY IT
Move to shrink the worry cloud
Pick a move. Then tap "Move!" again and again. If you stop, the cloud grows back.
