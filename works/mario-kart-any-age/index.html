🕹 Still Fun

Mario Kart is fun no matter how old you are. 🙂‍↕️

👶 Since Kindergarten

I have been playing Mario Kart on the Nintendo DS since I was in kindergarten.

🔤 Marikā For Short

By the way, Japanese people shorten Mario Kart to "Marikā." We also shorten Netflix to "Netofuri," by the way.

⚖️ Normal Weight

What I like in Mario Kart is, first, I like a character with a normal weight, about the same body type as Mario. Bowser is too heavy, and Baby Mario is too light, and both make the car hard to control.

🏍 Bike Over Car

And I like the bike more than the car. I feel like a bike with fat tires is easier to control.
