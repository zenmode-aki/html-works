🏢 Where I Lived

The place I lived in Cebu was a condominium.

🛗 Two Shared Floors

It had shared spaces on the 7th floor and the 25th floor. There are normal apartments on those floors too, but there are 2 places in the middle of the building that everyone living there can use.

🌿 The 7th Floor

The 7th floor had an area where you could walk a dog, and there was gardening done around it.

🎾 3 Tennis Courts

Maybe about the size of 3 tennis courts. A place where you can just walk slowly.

🏙 The 25th Floor

The 25th floor is where you can look out over the city from high up.

🪑 Chairs Up There

That one is about the size of 2 tennis courts, and there are chairs there that you can properly chill in.

🥤 Watching The City

You could sit down, have a drink at your own pace, and watch the city below.

🚶 My Floor

The one I actually used was the 7th floor.

🌙 Round And Round

When my thoughts wouldn't stop, or when I couldn't sleep at night, I'd go up to the 7th floor and just walk. Round and round.

📆 5 Or 6

I think I went up there 5 or 6 times a week.

🌊 Out To Sea

I didn't go to the 25th floor that often, but when I did, you can see all the way to the sea, so I'd stare out at the water in the distance.

📞 Friends And Family

Also, none of my friends lived in the same condominium, so I'd go up to the 7th or the 25th floor and call friends or family from time to time, to clear my head.

🎯 That Was Fun

That part was fun.
