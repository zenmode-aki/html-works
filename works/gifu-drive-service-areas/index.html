🐧 EVERYDAY LIFE⚡ 15 SEC

Driving my mother to Gifu, I stopped at expressway service areas that felt like theme parks 🐧

🚗 The Drive

I drove with my mother, who has panic disorder, to my grandmother's house (my mother's mother) in Gujo City, Gifu.

😳 If I Could

Well, to begin with, if I had a driver's license, this would all be solved! 😳 But even though I am 22, I still do not have a driver's license.

🩺 Why I Went

Still, my mother recently developed panic disorder, and since it is about 2 hours from Nagoya to Gujo City, Gifu, I wanted to go with her, so I decided to. She needed to recover somewhere in the countryside.

🛣 Expressway Story

Today I want to talk about the expressways in Japan that we passed on the way to Gifu.

🌊 The Kiso River

Once you leave Nagoya heading north, you see a river called the Kiso River. The Kiso River is a very famous river for people in Nagoya.

🎡 A Ferris Wheel

And there is an interchange called Kisogawa Park, which has a Ferris wheel. This is less like a highway service area and more like an amusement park.

🏊 Pool Toys

They were selling all kinds of pool toys for kids.

⚾ Doara Goods

And Gifu is also fan territory for the Chunichi Dragons, a pro baseball team from Nagoya, so the service area sells goods featuring Doara, the Chunichi Dragons mascot.

🅿️ Every 20km

Since my mother sometimes almost has a panic attack, and the expressways in Gifu have a service area roughly every 20km, we stopped at almost every single one.

🏡 Almost There

My grandmother's house is near a place called Bokka no Sato, so that is where we headed.
