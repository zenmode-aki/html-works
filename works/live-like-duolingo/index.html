🦉 I Love It

I love the design idea of Duolingo.

I want to live like Duolingo.

It focuses on the user experience.

And it moves around like a game.

🎯 This Site's Goal

Maybe the goal of this site is to give a user experience like Duolingo.

The Duolingo experience is really amazing, right?

It moves around, it is very fun as a game, you learn things, and it moves you.

Duolingo packs in so much user experience (UX), and I really respect it as a game.

🍎 Apple Too

When it comes to caring about UX (user experience), Apple products are also wonderful, right?

The iPhone and Apple Watch are easy to use.

To be honest, I am the kind of person who cannot understand difficult things very well.

So I think it is wonderful that they pack in the experience so you can understand it by feeling.

📚 UX Books

I want to learn design too.

✨ My Wish

I want to make this blog a place where you can have a fun experience, as fun as Duolingo or Apple products.
