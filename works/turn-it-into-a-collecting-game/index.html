Turn what you want to keep doing into a collecting game 🃏

🧠 Psychology✅ Productivity💡 Tips
🎮 Make It Game

It seems things you want to keep doing are easier to continue if you turn them into a game.

🃏 One Card Each

For example, you get 1 card when you clean your desk, and 1 more card when you do 10 squats.
