Repeat their words back, and their real feelings come out 🦜

🧠 Psychology🤝 Friends🫧 Feelings
🦜 Say It Back

It seems it is good to say the other person's words back when you listen.

😮‍💨 For Example

If someone says, "I'm tired," you say, "You're tired."

⚡ TRY IT
Be a kind parrot
🐧😮‍💨
"Today was so long. I am tired." "I looked at my phone all night. I feel worried." "I wanted to try the new class, but I got scared." "…Yes. Actually, I really want to try again! 🔥"
🦜 … 🦜 Hmm? That is not the feeling. 🦜 "You are tired." 🦜 "You feel worried." 🦜 "You got scared." 🦜 "You want to try again!"
🦜
🤍🤍🤍
Tap the feeling word. The parrot will say it back.
✨ Nice echo! Your friend keeps talking.
🤔 That is not a feeling word. Try another one.
💎 3 echoes, and the real feeling came out!
🛋️ Feel Safe

When their feelings are heard, people feel safe and talk more.

💎 Real Feelings

Then, after a while, their real feelings come out.

📚 I learned this from the Japanese blog "Panda no Ondo".
