How to catch a Grab at Terminal 2 of Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA2) 🚕

📍 Pickup Floor

At KLIA2, the Grab pickup area is on a lower floor (I think it was the basement).

⬇️ Go Down First

So, if you use Grab, when you arrive, first look for an escalator going down, and go down to the basement.

🛬Arrive
↓
⬇️Escalator down
↓
🚕Grab pickup (basement)
⚠️ Only There

If it is not there, Grab cannot come to pick you up.
