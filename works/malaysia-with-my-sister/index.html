I went to Malaysia for my sister's university visit 🧳

🇲🇾 Malaysia🌆 Kuala Lumpur👪 Family🎒 Study abroad✈️ Travel🏫 School
✈️ Off To Malaysia

I went to Malaysia with my sister 🐧 The purpose was my sister's university visit.

🎓 Not Ordinary

My sister does not have anything she especially wants to do in the future, but she somehow thinks "I don't want to be ordinary," so she decided to aim for a university abroad.

🧳 All On Me

So, in my family, I am the only one who has lived abroad and can speak English, so I was put in charge of planning this whole trip!

☺️ Parents Paid

But instead, my parents paid for the plane tickets and the hotel! ☺️ Basically, I could go abroad for free.

🗓 Five Days

This time, we went for 4 nights and 5 days!

☀️1🌙 ☀️2🌙 ☀️3🌙 ☀️4🌙 ☀️5

I had to plan how to fit everything into those 5 days. Especially when you don't know your way around Malaysia, visiting the universities you're hoping for is not easy.

📋 Lots To Do

This time, for me it was a trip abroad, and for my sister it was a part of choosing a university she might go to. When I tried to arrange all of it,

Book the flights and the hotel

(The hotel had to be in a spot worked out backwards from the university visits, the sightseeing spots, and the restaurants we wanted to try.)

Make appointments at all the universities my sister is considering

Take her there

Stay with her until the end of the campus tours

Look up the sightseeing spots there

and so on, there were a lot of things to do.
