Being a bridge SE also means adding to the engineers' workload 🐧

When a client says "please fix this," passing that on
means, in other words, adding one more task for the engineer.

So I was careful about timing.
I avoided the morning, when people are focused,
and sent things either first thing in the morning,
during the relaxed lunch hour, or I'd just go talk in person.

When pointing out a mistake, I used individual chat, not the group chat,
and tried to say it as gently as I could.

Some people worked remotely too,
so I was even more careful with them.

What I was really afraid of was people going quiet.
If they got scared of me and stopped telling me risky things,
the client would be the one who suffered most.
