There was one part of working in the Philippines I really liked 🐧

There's a strong feeling that you don't scold or yell at someone
in front of other people. It felt so strict I assumed it was a law,
though it turns out it isn't, it's just that deeply built into the culture.

I'm bad at being yelled at in Japan, so I found this really enviable.

Not wanting to be scolded, I guess that has no borders.
