"Could you give me permission for this?" I asked that constantly 🐧

An engineer would try to work, but couldn't open a file,
couldn't check an API, couldn't run a deploy.
Usually the cause was simple: no permission.

So I'd work out exactly who needed permission,
for what scope, for which task, before I asked the client.

Getting broad permission is easier, sure.
But when something goes wrong, the damage is broad too.
