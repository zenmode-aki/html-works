The moment I felt the most relief as a bridge SE 🐧

Right after a meeting ended.

I'm the type who gets pretty nervous explaining
what we did that week, and the deliverables, to the client.

Can I explain this well? Will it actually get through to them?

Before a meeting starts my stomach goes, honestly,
and I end up going to the toilet a lot.

But the moment it ends.
Saying "okay, that's over for now,"
and going outside to get some air. That felt the best.

There was usually at least one meeting every day,
and until it was done I stayed tense the whole day.

Once it ended, my shoulders finally dropped,
and from there I could work at my own pace.

I might have been working a little bit for that moment.
