People might assume it's the other way around 🐧

Japanese to English was, surprisingly, manageable.
Clients didn't talk about technical things that much.
They'd say things like, "we run this kind of business,
so please build it like this." If I understood the content,
I could translate it.

What was hard was the other direction, English to Japanese.

When I translated a technical sentence an engineer wrote,
I'd get stuck wondering, "can I even say it this way in Japanese?"
