Being able to translate wasn't enough 🐧

You pass on what the client said to the engineers.
Anyone can do that. Even a machine can do that.

But the part that was hard for me
was explaining what the engineers built, back to the client.

The person who explains it to the client is the Japanese person in the room. That's me.

If I didn't understand it myself, I couldn't explain it well.

I got assigned to all sorts of projects,
so each time, I had to go and understand the thing myself.

Understanding it, and getting to a state where I could actually explain it.
That was the hardest part.

So before a meeting, I always, without fail,
made something like an agenda or a table of contents first.

So I wouldn't get lost in the middle of the meeting.
So I wouldn't forget something, or go blank.
