The hardest word in the English engineers wrote was "it" 🐧

I translated reports from English into Japanese for the client,
and I often couldn't tell what "this" or "it" pointed to.

If I translated it as-is, the Japanese also became "which one?"

So every time, I walked over to the person who wrote it
and asked, "which feature does this 'it' mean?"

I went and asked several times a day,
so I'm pretty sure they thought I was annoying.

If I had understood the technical side better,
there were probably a lot of "it"s I wouldn't have needed to ask about.

Looking back now, I think I caused a lot of trouble.
