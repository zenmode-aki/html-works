After translating, I would translate it back again 🐧

Turning an engineer's English report into Japanese,
making it sound natural in Japanese, that part I could do.

But if it sounded natural while the technical meaning had shifted,
that would be a problem.

So I'd take the finished Japanese, turn it back into English,
and have the person who wrote it check, "is this what you meant?"

It was completely double the work,
but on the days I did it, I could hand it to the client feeling settled.

Even now, I think that way of checking wasn't a bad one.
