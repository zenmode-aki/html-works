"Why not just translate it with AI?" You would think that, right 🐧

It's true that in the past, documents had to be translated by people.
But these days we have AI.

AI can translate.

So "what am I even doing here" was a question
that stayed with me, honestly, the whole time I was working there.

For something like live interpreting in a meeting,
there may still be some point in a person doing it.

But translating documents? A machine can seriously just do that.

Looking back now, I really feel that
it wasn't an environment where translation alone was enough.

From here on, more than language skill,
I get the feeling that knowing the technology properly
is what will actually be useful to society.
