After talking something through, I'd type the same thing into chat 🐧

Both me and the Filipino engineers spoke English as a second language.
So if we only confirmed things by voice, something would usually slip.

I'd write, "just to confirm what we talked about, is this right?"
and send it, then wait for a stamp or a reply before moving on.

I thought it might be a bit much.
But we never once had a "he said, she said" problem afterward.

One stamp, and a whole future argument disappears.
Looking back, it was the cheapest insurance I ever bought.
