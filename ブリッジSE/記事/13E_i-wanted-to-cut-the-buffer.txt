Buffer time. I wanted to cut it, every time I explained a schedule 🐧

When telling a client "this is how far we'll get this month,"
I didn't want them thinking, "wait, that's all?"
So I wanted to say we could do a bit more.

But the PM never cut that buffer. Not once.

At first I thought they were being too cautious.
But bugs and extra questions really do show up every time,
so if we'd cut it, we'd have hit a wall every time.
