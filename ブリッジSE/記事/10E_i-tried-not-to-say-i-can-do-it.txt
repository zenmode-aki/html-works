I tried not to say "we can do it" too easily 🐧

I wanted to make the client happy,
so I always wanted to say, "yes, that works."

But saying it lightly raises expectations.
And those raised expectations land on the development team later.

So instead I'd say things like,
"with the current schedule, we can get this far,"
"beyond this point, it gets a bit ambitious,"
and just return the facts.

Saying that, I always felt a little cold, to be honest.
