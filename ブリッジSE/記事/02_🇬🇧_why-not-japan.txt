"Why not just build it in Japan?" People often asked me that 🐧

The reason we used an overseas team was simple: it costs less.
A team in Southeast Asia can be cheaper than hiring a Japanese engineer.

But the language and culture gap stays.
When you work closely together every week, that part gets worrying.

Someone like me sat in the middle to take that worry away.

Put one bridge in place, and suddenly you can build things
together with a team overseas. Looking at it that way,
it was a pretty good role to have.
