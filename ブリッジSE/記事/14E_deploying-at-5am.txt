Sometimes we started a deploy at 5 in the morning 🐧

Deploy means putting the code you built into the live environment.
Doing that during the client's business hours is scary.
If something goes wrong, their work stops right there.

So we'd deploy early, test for two hours,
and finish checking before the client came into the office.

I was sleepy, sure.
But being able to say "it's working" by 9am felt good.
