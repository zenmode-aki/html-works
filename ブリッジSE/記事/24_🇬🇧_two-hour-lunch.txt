At a Japanese company, lunch break is supposed to be an hour 🐧

But in practice, most people are back at their desk in about 50 minutes.
There's an unspoken feeling that going back early is just normal.

At the office I worked at in the Philippines,
a lot of people took a full two hours, from 11:30 to 1:30.
At first, it honestly surprised me.

Once I got used to it, I started to envy it, in a good way.
