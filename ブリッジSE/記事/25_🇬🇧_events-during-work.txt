Company events at Japanese companies often happen on days off 🐧

Something like, "there's a party on Saturday, please join."

At the office I worked at in the Philippines,
it was normal to hear, "we're having an event at 3pm today, get ready,"
and it would just start, right in the middle of work hours.

I'd never had a full-time job in Japan before this one,
so I didn't even realize, at the time, that this wasn't the norm everywhere.
