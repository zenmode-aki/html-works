When I lived in Cebu, my fridge was full of eggs 🐧

I had this idea in my head that getting enough protein matters.

But if you want protein in Cebu, the first thing is meat,
and both the chicken and the pork have a bit of a smell to them
compared to the meat you can buy in a supermarket back home.

There are also quite a lot of bones in it, which made it hard to eat.

So I never really felt like cooking it myself.

Which is how I ended up eating eggs all the time in Cebu.

I always bought eggs in trays of 30.

There was a fairly well known shopping mall nearby called Ayala,
and inside Ayala Center Cebu there's a store called Metro.

I was basically buying up their eggs.

Before I knew it, the fridge was packed with them.
