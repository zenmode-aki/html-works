This was the most surprising thing I learned 🐧

It's an English-speaking job, so I thought
language skill would matter the most.

But once I actually started working,
technical skill mattered far more than language skill.

Even if you understand the words,
if you don't understand the content, the translation stays thin.

If you don't understand the technical side,
you can't answer properly when the client asks a follow-up question.

I felt it pretty clearly, every single day:
there is no way around studying IT.

And then, simple people skills matter too.

To some extent you have to manage the team members,
so you give directions, and you hand work over to people.

And if that's the job, then being trusted as a person
is also important, I thought.

I was 20 years old at the time,
so whether people actually felt good working under my directions...

Honestly, back when I was doing it, I wasn't very confident about that.

I like technology, so I want to face the IT study
instead of running away from it.
