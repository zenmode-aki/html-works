The projects where I felt the most responsibility were like this 🐧

Projects where I was the only Japanese person on the team.

I'm the only one standing at the front,
so if I failed to pass on what the client said, that was on me.

And if I failed to explain what the engineers built, that was on me too.

Responsibility, or rather a duty to explain,
a duty to be the bridge. I felt that strongly, and I was pretty nervous.

So before a meeting, I prepared quite a lot.

Because when I talk, the story often drifts into something
only the insiders can follow.

So I took as many notes as I could, and built proper slides before going in.
