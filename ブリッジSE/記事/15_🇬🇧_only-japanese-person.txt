The projects I found the most rewarding were like this 🐧

Projects where I was the only Japanese person on the team.

I'm the only one standing at the front,
so passing on what the client said, and explaining what the engineers built,
both of those were entirely my job.

The duty to explain, the duty to be the bridge.
It felt like I was holding all of that myself.

Honestly, I was nervous.

So before a meeting, I prepared quite a lot.

When I talk, I end up getting lost in the middle of the meeting.
The talk tends to drift off in a strange direction.

So, as much as I could, I always made sure to take notes before the meeting,
decided firmly that "today's meeting will go in this order,"
and made slides before going in.

If I make slides, it is not only easier for the client to understand,
but I also do not get lost during the meeting, so I thought it was really good.

It was hard work, but the projects I found the most rewarding
were exactly these ones.
