Sometimes I was the only Japanese person on a project 🐧

Talking with the client, sharing things in English with the engineers,
preparing for meetings, writing the minutes, all of it fell on me alone.

Honestly, I was nervous every single time.

So before a meeting, I'd write down what I needed to ask
and what I needed to say, even the order I'd say it in.

I wasn't perfect at it.
But the days I didn't prepare were clearly harder.
