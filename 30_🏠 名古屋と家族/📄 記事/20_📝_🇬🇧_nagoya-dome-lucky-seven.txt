In Japanese baseball, the 7th inning is called the "Lucky Seven"

In Japanese baseball, the stadium gets the most excited in the 7th inning.
This is because there is a culture called "Lucky Seven."
Actually, it does not mean at all that you score a lot of runs because it is the Lucky Seven, but somehow, the number 7 brings good luck.

Usually, in the 7th inning, people sing the team's song, and cheerleaders and mascot characters come out and make the whole stadium excited.
