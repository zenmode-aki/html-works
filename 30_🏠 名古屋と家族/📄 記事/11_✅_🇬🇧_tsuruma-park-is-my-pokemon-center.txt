Tsuruma Park in Nagoya is my Pokémon Center, where my heart recovers

There is a park in Nagoya that I love to walk in.
It is a place called Tsuruma Park.
Right now, I live in a busy downtown area of Nagoya.
From there to Tsuruma Park, it takes about 30 minutes on foot.
But if I go to Tsuruma Park, my heart almost always feels at peace.
So in my heart, I call Tsuruma Park my "Pokémon Center."
That is how much my heart recovers when I walk in Tsuruma Park.
If you live around Nagoya or in Nagoya city, and you think, "I have not had enough nature lately" or "I want forest bathing,"
I really recommend walking in Tsuruma Park.
I think Tsuruma Park is very famous as a Pokémon GO spot.
For Pokémon GO users, it seems Tsuruma Park is a place where good Pokémon appear, or where you can get very strong.
When Pokémon GO was very popular, I still remember that many people gathered at Tsuruma Park.
