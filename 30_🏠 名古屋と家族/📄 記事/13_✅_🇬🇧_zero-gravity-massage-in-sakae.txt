To rest for 15 minutes in Sakae, try the 300-yen zero-gravity massage

You want to rest for just about 15 minutes in Sakae, Nagoya.
There is a zero-gravity massage place I recommend at times like that.
Underground in Sakae, Nagoya, there is a place with zero-gravity massage machines.
It is 300 yen for 12 minutes.
The massage feels so good that you really mistake yourself for being in zero gravity.
It is a place I recommend when you are a little tired and want to refresh just a little.
The inside of the massage machine is a capsule.
Inside the capsule, it is made so you can even take a short nap, and it was very interesting.
