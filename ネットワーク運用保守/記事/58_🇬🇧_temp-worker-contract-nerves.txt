As a temp worker, my heart races a little every 3 months

Temp workers often sign a contract every 3 months.
In my case, the staffing company I belonged to merged while I was working, so my contract was renewed a little irregularly, like 2 months → 3 months → 6 months.
When the first 2 months ended, and when the next 3 months ended, my heart beat a little fast.
Whether you can keep working there usually gets decided about 2 or 3 weeks before the contract ends.
I also get a survey message about 1 month before the renewal, asking, "Do you want to continue?"
So from about 1 month before the contract ends until the renewal is decided, I feel a little restless.
I feel like, "No way, they won't stop renewing only me, right? 🥺 No way, right?? 🥺"
Before the contract, I feel a little tense, and my feeling of "I have to work hard" gets stronger than usual.
Also, when you work as a temp worker, feedback on your work comes to you through your staffing company.
Things like "You are not doing ○○" or "You are doing ○○ well."
Getting that kind of evaluation through the staffing company also makes me nervous.
Full-time employees may hear it directly from their boss.
For temp workers, sometimes the employees tell you directly, and sometimes it comes to you indirectly through the staffing company like this.
In my case, I used to often forget to enter my timesheet every day.
About that, my boss told me directly, and my staffing company told me too.
After that, I fixed my habit of forgetting the timesheet for good.
But on the other hand, this is also a good point.
If I have complaints or hard things at work, I can tell my boss directly, and I can also say it through the staffing company.
Having 2 roads to get scolded means having 2 roads to speak up, too.
Well, in most cases, I think if you come to work every day without being late and say hello properly, they will not stop your renewal.
I am not sure. It also depends on the situation of the company.
It is like a small exam result day that comes every 3 months.
