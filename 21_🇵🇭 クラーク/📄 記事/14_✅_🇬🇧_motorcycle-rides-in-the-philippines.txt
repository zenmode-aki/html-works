In the Philippines, the motorcycle is the best way to get around

Taxis are cheap in the Philippines, but there is a way to get around even cheaper than a taxi.
It is the motorcycle.
With a motorcycle, you can get around for about 1/3 of the taxi fare.
When I was in the Philippines, I used these motorcycles a lot.
I really liked it.
First, it feels good.
Above all, the fare is 1/3 of a taxi.
Also, it does not get stuck in traffic jams, so it often arrives faster.
That is how convenient the motorcycle is for getting around in the Philippines.
It is cheap, and I liked it very much.
