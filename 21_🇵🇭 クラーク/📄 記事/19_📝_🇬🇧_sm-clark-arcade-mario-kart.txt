I found Mario Kart at the arcade in SM Clark

The SM mall in the Philippines had an arcade.
Compared to arcades in Japanese shopping malls, the whole arcade felt more like neon, and it had a purple lighting mood.
Also, inside the arcade, there were about 5 karaoke boxes, about the size for 2 people, and you could see right into them from outside.
Of course, there were also some Japanese games.
They had a game where you shoot basketballs, a Japanese fishing coin game, and a zombie game like Resident Evil.
And they also had Mario Kart!
