At McDonald's in Nagoya, Uber Eats orders were amazing

The McDonald's where I worked was near Osu Shopping Street in Nagoya.
Back then, I heard that stores around Sakae were in the national top 3 in the Uber Eats sales ranking.
It was surprising that it was Nagoya, not Tokyo or Osaka.
Actually, the line of Uber Eats and Demae-can drivers was longer than the line of customers in the store.
The line went outside the store, and it felt like there were about 2 times as many drivers as customers.
