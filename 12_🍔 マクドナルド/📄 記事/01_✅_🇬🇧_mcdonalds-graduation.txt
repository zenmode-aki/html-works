At McDonald's, quitting your job is called "graduating from the store"

I worked part-time at McDonald's for about 3 years.
I quit when I finished high school, and at that time, I got a message board and gift cards.
It seems at McDonald's, quitting the store is called "sotsuten," or graduating from the store.
It seems if you graduate from the store in March, you get cute gifts like these.
I felt they treated me well until the very end, and I thought, as expected of the world's biggest restaurant company.
