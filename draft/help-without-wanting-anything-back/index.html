Helping without wanting anything back keeps your heart light 🎁

🎁 Just Help

Helping someone without wanting anything back makes it easy to feel happy, it is said. This is because you do not start holding on to "I want something back."

☁️ Holding More

When you think, "I try so hard, so why don't they see it?", you may be holding on more.

⚡ TRY IT
Tag or no tag?
Give the same gift two times: once with the tag, once without. Watch the way home.
🎁 🏷 "You owe me one!"
☁️☀️
🐧
🏠
⏳ "Not yet…?" meter 0
Your friend is busy today and may not say thank you.
☁️ "Did they notice? Not yet…" The cloud follows you all the way home.
☀️ You gave it, and that is the end. You skip all the way home.
🐧 The same gift. But your heart feels so different.
🪶 Stay Light

Once you give it, it ends there. That way, your heart stays light.
