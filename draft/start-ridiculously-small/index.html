Start so small that your brain does not notice 🤏

🔥 Hard Workers

Strangely, hard workers sometimes do not get results.

🧠 Brain Pushback

This is because when you push with 10 power, your brain pushes back with 20.

💪 You: 10⚔️🧠 Brain: 20
🤏 Tiny Start

So start so small that your brain does not notice.

⚡ TRY IT
