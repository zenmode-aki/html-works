Do not keep anger inside; let it out in a small voice, alone 💨

🌋 Kept Inside

If you hold in your anger, it explodes later in a different place.

🏠 At Home

For example, you hold it in outside, then take it out on your family at home.

⚡ TRY IT

😝 Small Voice

So it seems good to stick out your tongue in your heart, or let it out in a small voice when you are alone.

💨 Little Puffs

A small "Come on!" can stop a big explosion.
