Push back strong stress with feelings that are just as strong 🎈

🛞 Flat Tire

A tire with little air goes flat even on a small bump.

🌊 Deep Sea

Deep in the sea, strong pressure crushes a balloon.

But if the air inside is just as strong, it does not get crushed.

⚡ TRY IT
Keep the balloon round
Dive deeper, and the sea pushes harder. Push back from the inside with strong words.

✅ Balanced! The balloon stays round. Now dive. 😣 Squish! Push back with a strong word. 🔥 Strong inside! You can dive deeper. 🏆 50 m deep, and still round!
📣 Strong Words

So answer stress with strong words, like "I'm looking forward to it!"

😰"I'm so worried I can't sleep."
😄"I'm excited to see what happens!"
🎈 Acting Is OK

It is OK even if you are only acting strong and your legs are shaking.
