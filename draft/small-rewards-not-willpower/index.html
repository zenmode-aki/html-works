Small rewards keep a habit going better than willpower does ☕

😤 Not Willpower

It seems habits do not last long with willpower and guts.

🔥 Three Days

Willpower runs out in about 3 days, and if you push too hard, you burn out.

⚡ TRY IT
The 7-day habit race
Two penguins start a walking habit. Who keeps going?
💥 Willpower🔥🔥🔥
🏁
🐧💥
"I will walk 10 km every day!" 🔥 Full power! Super fast! 😤 Still going… a little tired. 😮‍💨 So tired… my legs hurt. 💤 Burned out. I can't do it today…
☕ Small reward🔥
🏁

😮 Day 4: willpower stopped. The coffee penguin is still walking.
🏁 The small reward wins! Feeling good keeps you going.
🎁 Reward Right Away

Instead, it seems better to give yourself a small reward right after you do it.

😌 Feels Good

Things that feel good last.

☕ For Example

For example, a nice cup of coffee after a walk.
