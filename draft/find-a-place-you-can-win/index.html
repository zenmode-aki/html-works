Stop the fight you cannot win, and find your own sea 🌊

🏳️ Stop Fighting

When you stop a fight you cannot win, you begin to see new places.

📏 Your Rules

They are places where you can make the rules, or places where you can win.

🐆 On Land

On land, a penguin cannot beat a cheetah. But in the sea, a penguin swims surprisingly fast.
