My luck seems to go up from age 24 🔮

🔮 My Fortune

It seems my luck will go up a lot when I turn 24!

My older sister is a fortune teller, and the other day she told my fortune.

Then my sister told me this with a surprised face: "Hey, you! Things get amazing for you from 24!"

🌱 Hope Matters

Fortune telling or anything, when you can think "life will get better than now from here," somehow you suddenly get energy, right?

Maybe the reason I like technology is also that I can hope the world will keep getting better thanks to technology.

Hope really is important.

📈 Ten Good Years

By the way, it seems my luck will stay at a peak level of my life for about 10 years, from 24 to my early 30s. I am looking forward to it.

⏳ Check At 25

Someday when I am about 25, maybe I will look back at this post and check if the fortune was right.
