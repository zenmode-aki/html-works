Think about prices in hours of work, not in yen 🏷️

🏷️ Price Tag

When you buy something, think "How many hours do I need to work for it?" instead of the price.

✋ Only 20%

It seems the money you can freely use is about 20% of your income.

🍰 Times Five

So a 500 yen cake is about 2,500 yen of work.

🍰500 yen
⏰2,500 yen of work
