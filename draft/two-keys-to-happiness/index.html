2 keys to a happy life: hope and good people around you 🗝️

🗝️ 2 Things

It is said that there are 2 important things for a happy life.

🌅 Key 1: Hope

The 1st is hope: feeling "I can open up my own life, at any time."

🤝 Key 2: People

The 2nd is good relationships with people.
