Give your anger a score, and it stops growing bigger 🌡️

🌡️ Score It

When you feel angry, try giving that anger a score.

🔟 The Scale

Make your angriest time in life 10 points, and think how many points this is.

🏷️ Decide First

For example, if a friend is late, decide first: "This is 3 points."
