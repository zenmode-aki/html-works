🌫️ Losing Color

If "Anything is fine" is your habit, your own color slowly disappears.

🎭 Liked By All

It seems that if you try to be liked by everyone, you lose your own character.

🍜 Start Small

First, practice small wishes like "I want ramen more than a beef bowl" or "Saturday is better."

👌 Nobody Minds

If it is this small, nobody is troubled when you say it.
