There are 3 kinds of confidence, and you have value just by living 🧱

🧱 3 Kinds

It seems there are 3 kinds of confidence.

🎓💰✨ From outside 💪 I can do it 💖 I have value
🎓 1st And 2nd

The 1st comes from outside, like school, money, or looks.

The 2nd is "I can do it if I try."

💖 The 3rd

The 3rd is "I still have value," even with no reason.

⚡ TRY IT
Try to knock down the tower
Tap "Bad day!" 5 times. 🌪️ 📉 Your test score went down. ✂️ Your haircut went wrong. 👛 Your wallet is empty. ☔ It rained on your trip day. 🧦 A hole in your sock… again.
🎓💰✨ From outside
💪 I can do it
🌪️ Bad days:0/5
Which block will fall? 🎓 The block from outside fell off! 💪💖 It shakes… but the rest is still standing. 💖 It wobbles, but it never falls. 🐧💖 5 bad days, and "I have value" did not move.
🌱 Just By Living

People have value just by being alive.

🛟 The Proof

If someone is about to fall off a cliff, you try hard to help, whoever it is.
