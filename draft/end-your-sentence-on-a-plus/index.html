🐧 EVERYDAY LIFE⚡ 20 SEC

End your sentence with the good part, and you sound kinder ✨

🔀 Order Matters

Even if you say the same thing, the order changes how it feels.

🍜 Tasty Example

Rather than "It is tasty but expensive," say "It is expensive but tasty."

⚡ TRY IT
🔀 Swap it to end on a plus
🍜Ramen shop
😋tasty➕💸expensive➖
"It is tasty, but expensive."
"It is expensive, but tasty!"
🏠My room
🛋️cozy➕📏small➖
"It is cozy, but small."
"It is small, but cozy!"
☔Rainy trip
📸unforgettable➕🌧️rainy➖
"It was unforgettable, but rainy."
"It was rainy, but unforgettable!"
📚Hard test
💡I learned a lot➕😵hard➖
"I learned a lot, but it was hard."
"It was hard, but I learned a lot!"
😕🐧
The sentence ends with: ➖➕
A minus ending… The listener feels down. Swap it!
✨ A plus ending! It sounds kind.
🏆 4 for 4! Everyone leaves with a smile.
🧠 Last Words Stay

It is said that the last words stay in people's hearts more easily.

☔ Rainy Trip

If your trip had rain, say, "It was sad, but it became a memory we will never forget."

😊 End With Plus

End with a plus.
