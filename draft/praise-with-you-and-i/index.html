The best compliment is a set of "You…" and "I…" 💐

💐 Best Compliment

It seems the best compliment is a set of "You…" and "I…".

😊 The You Part

For example, "You have a nice smile!" is the "You" part.

💗 The I Part

Then add your "I" feeling: "I always feel calm when I see it."

You…+I…=💖💖
⚡ TRY IT
2 switches for a great compliment
Pick a scene. Turn on "You…" and "I…". Watch your friend's face.
🐧
You… You have a nice smile! You sing so well! What a cute bag!
I… I always feel calm when I see it. I was really moved. I want one too!
🐧💭
Turn on a switch! 👇 Oh, thank you! Huh? About what? Wow… that made my day!

💖 Add One Feeling

Just add 1 of your own feelings, and your compliment reaches their heart much better.
