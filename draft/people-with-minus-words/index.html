Step back from people with minus words, and get close to plus words ☀️

🍜 Who Matters

A fancy dinner with someone you dislike is less tasty than cup noodles with good friends.

🍽️😑Fancy dinner
🍜😆Cup noodles
☁️ Minus Words

With people who use many minus words, like complaints, bad talk, and "It's no use anyway," keep a little distance.

☀️ Plus Words

Get close to people who often say "I'm happy," "This is fun," and "Thank you."

⚡ TRY IT
Catch only the plus words!

☀️ 0 ☁️ 0

🤝 But Remember

But do not cut ties with people who helped you in hard times.
