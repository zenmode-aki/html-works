Trying to look better makes you tired, so be natural but polite 🏷️

✨ Looking Better

If you try to look better because you want people to like you, you get tired.

🏷️ Fake Sticker

It is like putting a "premium beef" sticker on normal beef.

You are always scared: "Will they find out?"

🥩PREMIUM 💦 Please do not look too closely…
⚡ TRY IT
The premium sticker test
Go out with a shiny sticker on. How long can you keep it?
🐧 ★ PREMIUM ★ 🙂 💦💦💦
🐧🔍

💦 Nervous

😵 So tiring! You can peel it off, you know.
Phew! No more sweat. Now say hello politely.
💛 Natural, but polite. That is the best.
🍃 Be Natural

So peel off the sticker and be natural.

🎀 Stay Polite

But be polite to people.
