Self-esteem can grow at any age through small wins of helping people 🧱

🌱 Any Age

It is said you can grow your self-esteem even as an adult.

💛 The Trick

The trick is to help people with a small idea, and pile up small "thank you" wins.

🗺️ For Example

For example, show a lost person the way on a map app, or teach a friend a phone trick.
