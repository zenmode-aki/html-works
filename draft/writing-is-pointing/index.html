Writing is just pointing and saying, "Look at that!" 👉

👉 Good Writing

It seems good writing is "pointing."

👀 Turn Their Eyes

You just turn the reader's eyes to an interesting thing you found.

🎩 No Showing Off

You do not need to show off how smart you are.

⚡ TRY IT
Show the reader the house
Across the valley, there is something fun. How will you show it?
🐐🏠
Paradigm Moreover Notwithstanding Synergy
🐧👉
🐧
🐧 "Hmm? What is over there?" 🐧 "😪 …I can not see anything." 🐧 "😊 Oh! A goat on the roof! I see it!"
Which button helps the reader?
🎩 Big words made fog. The house is gone.
✨ The reader sees it! That is writing.
🔎 Right Details

When you decide what to point at, you also know which details you need.

About the famous advice "Omit needless words," Burkeman jokes: "If I knew they were needless, I would not write them!"
