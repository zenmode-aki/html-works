If it takes 2 minutes, do it now, because later costs more ⏱️

⏱️ Two Minutes

If it takes 2 minutes, do it now.

📝 Heavy Memo

Writing it in a memo can be more work than just doing it.

🌙 Real Cost

The real time is 2 minutes, plus time to think, time to worry, and time to remember it at 3 a.m.
