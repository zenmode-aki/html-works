What you do for others comes back to you like an echo ⛰️

✨ Golden Rule

Do for others what you want them to do for you.

💬 Talk First

People who talk to others get talked to.

😊 Smile First

People who greet with a smile get a smile back.

🌟 Praise and Help

People who praise get praised, and people who help get helped.

⚡ TRY IT
Echo mountain
Send something to the mountain. What comes back?
🐧
Tap a button and send it to the mountain. Yoo-hoo…! 💬 Someone talked to you! 😊 You got a smile back! 🌟 Someone praised you! 🤝 Someone helped you! 💢 Oops! The grumble came back too.
🧺 Echoes you got back
💬 😊 🌟 🤝
⛰️ All 4 came back! Kindness echoes.
⛰️ Like Echoes

Like an echo in the mountains, it really comes back.
