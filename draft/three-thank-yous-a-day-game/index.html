A daily game: try to hear "thank you" 3 times a day 💮

🎮 A Small Game

There is a good game to practice being thoughtful.

📏 The Rule

The rule is to hear "thank you" from people 3 times a day.

🚪 Small Is OK

Small things like holding a door or picking up something someone dropped are fine.
