When your breathing is slow, your brain thinks you are safe 🌬️

📝 Self And Heart

The kanji for "breath," 息, is made of 自 (self) and 心 (heart).

自+心=息
🧠 No Windows

Your brain is inside your head, so it cannot see outside.

⚡ TRY IT
Tell the brain in the dark room, "You are safe"
The brain cannot see outside. It only feels your breathing.
💡💡💡
❓ Am I safe? 🙂 Getting safer… 🚨 Danger?! ✅ I am safe now
🧠‼️
Which breathing will you show the brain? Breathe in… Breathe out slowly… Huff, huff, huff! The brain relaxed. 😌 3
3 slow breaths light 3 lamps.
✨ Slow breath = "safe" message for your brain.
🛡️ Safe Signal

So it seems the brain watches your breathing, and when it is slow, it thinks, "I am safe now."

🌬️ Breathe Out

When you feel worried, maybe breathe out slowly and tell your brain, "It is OK."
