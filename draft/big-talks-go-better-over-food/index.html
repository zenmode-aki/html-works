Big talks go better while you eat something tasty together 🍰

🪃 Boomerang Effect

It seems people want to argue back when someone tries to change their mind.

This is called the "boomerang effect."

😋 Yummy Time

But when we are eating something tasty, the feeling to argue gets weaker.

🍽️ Talk Over Food

So it is good to talk about important things over tasty food.

This is called the "luncheon technique."
