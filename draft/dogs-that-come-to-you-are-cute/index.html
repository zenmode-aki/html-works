People who come over and talk to you first look cute 🐶

🐶 Pet Shop

At a pet shop, a dog that wags its tail and comes to you looks very cute.

🐧 People Too

People are the same. It seems people who come over with a smile and talk to you first look cute.

⚡ TRY IT

🐧 Now it is your turn!
