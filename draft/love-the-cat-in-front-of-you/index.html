Love the cat in front of you, not its kittens' kittens 🐈

📜 Keynes Wrote

The economist Keynes wrote about a certain kind of person. That person does not love their cat, but its kittens.

🐾 Kittens' Kittens

No, really, they love the kittens of the kittens.

🔭 Always Someday

They always look at "someday," so they never love the cat in front of them.
