Say your weak point first, and it will not hurt when others say it 🛡️

📣 Say It First

It seems good to say the weak point you worry about first, by yourself.

🧭 For Example

For example, say first, "I am really bad with directions."

⚡ TRY IT
The "say it first" shield
🐧
🛡️
"I am really bad with directions!" 😅
Lost again? 😏 Wrong way, huh? Do you need a map? Going around in circles? Where are we going? 😂

Teasing will fly at you 5 times. Tap "Yep, yep!" to bounce it back.

😣 Ouch! It hurts when it comes as a surprise. Press "📣 Say it first" and try again.

🛡️ No Attack

Something you said first is not an attack anymore.
