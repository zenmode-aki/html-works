Random thoughts keep your brain idling like a car engine 🚗

💭 Random Thoughts

It seems a lot of your brain's energy is used for random thoughts.

🚗 Engine Idling

It is like a car that is idling: it is not moving, but the engine is running.

🥱 Still Tired

So sometimes you are still tired even after you rest.

⚡ TRY IT
Quiet the brain engine while you wash dishes
Tap the soap bubbles. That is "just looking at now."
🚗 Brain engine80%
🤫 Quiet🔥 Busy
🤫
🫧 Bubbles you looked at: 0
Thought clouds will come. You don't have to fight them.
Look at the bubbles. Let the clouds float by.
😅 Oops, you thought about it more. The engine got louder.
🤫 The engine is quiet. Now your brain can really rest.
🍽️ Just Look

When you wash the dishes, just look at the bubbles, not "I have to go to the post office."

⏳ Start Small

Even 3 minutes is OK.

📚 I learned this from the Japanese blog "Panda no Ondo".
