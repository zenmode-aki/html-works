Be kind first, copy back, and sometimes forgive: the winning rule 🔁

🏟️ The Contest

Robert Axelrod held a contest where computer programs with many strategies played against each other.

🏆 The Winner

The winner was a very simple rule.

🔁 The Rule

Be kind first, and if the other side betrays you, do the same back next time.

⚡ TRY IT

Each round, you both secretly choose: share the snack, or keep it.
