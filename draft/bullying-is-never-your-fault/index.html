Bullying is never the fault of the person who is bullied 🌱

🤍 Never Your Fault

With bullying, the one who bullies is 100% wrong. You can try not to bully, but you cannot try not to be bullied.

🕊️ No Fighting Needed

You don't have to hold on until your limit, or fight.

🌱 TRY IT
3 doors you can open
You can open any door. Take your time.
👆 Tap a door. It opens slowly.
🛏️ Rest Staying home to rest is OK. Keeping yourself safe comes first.
💬 Talk to someone Family, a teacher you trust, a school counselor, or a helpline. You don't have to carry it alone.
🏡 Change places Another class, another school, another place. There is a place where you can breathe.
🌱 Any door is OK. Whichever door you choose, you are not wrong.
🚪 Other Ways

It's OK to rest, and it's OK to change places. It is also OK to talk to a professional.

🌱 Later Is OK

Studying can be caught up later.

📚 I learned this from the Japanese blog "Panda no Ondo".
