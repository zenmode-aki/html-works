Call people by their name, and you get closer 🏷️

🏷️ Add Their Name

Try adding the person's name to your greetings and thanks.

👋 Two Examples

Rather than "Hi!", say "Hi, Sato-san!"

Rather than "Great!", say "Great, Sato-san!"

🧲 Much Closer

When people hear their name, they feel, "This person sees me," and you get much closer.

🌱 First Names

It is said that a first name can feel even closer than a family name.
