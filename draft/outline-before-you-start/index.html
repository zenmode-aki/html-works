Before you start, decide 3 things: the goal, the wall, and the fix 🧺

✋ Before You Start

It is said that it is good to decide 3 things before you move your hands.

🎯 1 The Goal

The 1st is the goal. It is the result you want to get.

🧱 2 The Wall

The 2nd is the wall. It is the problem between you and the goal.

🔧 3 The Fix

The 3rd is the fix. It is the way to break that wall.

⚡ TRY IT
Plan a picnic in 3 lines
Open the blocks in order. You can go any time!
☁️ 💧💧💧 🐧🐧🐧🧺
🌀 Where are we going? Nobody knows… We got lost.
🌧️ Rain! We had a goal, but forgot the wall.
☔ We knew it may rain, but had no fix. Soaking wet!
☀️ It rained, but we laughed together under the roof!
💯 The Big One

Of the 3, the goal decides 90%, they say.
