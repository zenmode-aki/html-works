Before you start, write 3 lines and show them to the person who asked 🎂

✋ Wait First

It is said that you should not start a request right away.

📝 3 Lines

First, write 3 lines (goal, wall, fix) and show them to the person who asked.

⚡ TRY IT
Bake a birthday cake for a friend
💭 In my friend's head
🍓🎂
💭 In my head
🍫🎂
🎯 Goal: a cake my friend loves
🧱 Wall: I do not know the flavor.
🔧 Fix: ask before I bake.
📮 Friend: "🍓 Strawberry, please!"
The birthday is tomorrow. What do you do first?
🔥 Baking…
😮 Friend: "Oh… that is not it."
🎉 Friend: "Yes! This is it!"
⏱️ Time used: 0 hours · ↺ Redos: 0
🖼️ No Gap

There, you remove the gap between the pictures in your heads.

✅ No Redo

Then you almost never hear "That is not it" later.
