A bright voice makes people feel cared for 🍜

🥀 Weak Voice

If a shop worker says "Welcome" in a small, weak voice, you may worry, "Are they angry? Am I a bother?"

☀️ Bright Voice

If they say it in a bright voice, you feel cared for. Even the udon may taste better somehow.
