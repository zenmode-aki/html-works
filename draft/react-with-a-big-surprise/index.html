A talk gets exciting when you react with surprise 🎉

😲 Surprise Decides

It seems surprise decides if a talk gets exciting or not.

🍃 Flat Replies

If you only say "Oh" and "I see" in a flat voice, the room goes cold.

🔤 Say AIUEO

I recommend "a-i-u-e-o."

"Ah!" "Nice!" "Hmmm, I get it!" "Eh?! Wow!" "Oh, good for you!"

あAh! いNice! うHmmm! えEh?! おOh!
⚡ TRY IT
Surprise combo
Your friend tells a story. React with a-i-u-e-o and keep the combo going!
🐧💭
Press start. I have a story for you! "I went to the aquarium yesterday." "A penguin walked right up to me!" "It looked at my ice cream…" "…and it took 1 lick!" "Then the staff gave me a new one." "And it was a double scoop!" "…Well, anyway. Never mind." "Thanks for listening! That was so fun to tell!"

💬 They Talk More

When you are surprised, the speaker feels happy and wants to talk more.
