Looking at your phone with a friend feels like whispering secrets next to them 📱

📱 A New Word

It seems using your phone when you are with someone is called "phubbing."

🤫 Secret Talk

The other person's brain feels like "someone is whispering secrets next to me."

And little by little, they stop trusting you.
