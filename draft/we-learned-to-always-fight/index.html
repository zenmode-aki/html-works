We learned to fight from games where only one side wins 🍰

🏆 Never Give Up

In Japan, "Never give up and fight to the end" is often seen as a good thing.

📝 Win or Lose

Entrance exams and club tournaments are games where if someone wins, someone loses.

🔁 Again and Again

We played games like that many times, so we may believe, "I must fight even if I cannot win."
