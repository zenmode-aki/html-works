With strangers, a small bow and "Oh, hi" makes talking easier 🎫

🤨 Too Sudden

If you suddenly say "Nice weather!" to a stranger, they may feel a little careful.

🙂 Oh, Hi

I recommend a small bow as you come near, and "Oh, hi."

🌤️"Nice weather!"
🐧*small bow* "Oh, hi."
⚡ TRY IT
Get a "ticket to talk"
🕰️ A WAITING ROOM⏳ 5 MINUTES LATER…
🐧YOU
🐧😶STRANGER
How do you start? "…Um, do I know you?" 🤨 "…Hello?" (a little careful) "Oh, hi." 🙂 (a small bow back) "Sure, please sit!" 😊
🎫 Ticket to talk: GET!
You can talk to them later, too.
Try all 3. Which one gets a ticket?Now use your ticket.
🎉 Easy! The ticket worked, and you look polite too.
🎫 Talk Ticket

This becomes a "ticket to talk," and it stays easy to talk even some minutes later.

🪑 This Seat

Just asking "Is this seat free?" also makes you look like a polite person.

📚 I learned this from the Japanese blog "Panda no Ondo".
