Put bad habits 20 seconds farther and good habits 20 seconds closer ⏱️

⏱️ The Rule

There is something called the "20 seconds rule."

🍪 Bad Habits

For a bad habit, add 20 seconds of trouble before you start.

📖 Good Habits

For a good habit, cut 20 seconds of trouble before you start.

🏠 For Example

For example, put snacks in a drawer in a far room, and put the book you want to read on the table.

🍪+20 sec: far drawer
📖−20 sec: on the table

✨ Just 20 Seconds
