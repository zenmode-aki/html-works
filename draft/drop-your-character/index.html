You do not need to hold back because it is "not your character" 🥟

🤐 Holding Back

Is there something you really want to try, but you hold back because "It is not my character"?

🌈 For Example

For example, bright hair color, or singing your favorite song out loud.

🟢 The Green Pea

Your "character" is like the green pea on top of a shumai.

Without it, it is still tasty.

⚡ TRY IT
Take off the green pea
← "my character"
😋 Taste:⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Tap the green pea 👆 😋 Still tasty! Now open the locks you kept shut. 🐧 This is me, the way I like! ✨
🐧🌈🎤🧣
🎤 Just Try It

Throw away your character, and try what you really want to do.

📚 I learned this from the Japanese blog "Panda no Ondo".
