Link a new habit to a habit you already have 🔗

🔗 The Trick

A new habit is easier to keep if you link it after a habit you already have.

🚌 On The Bus

For example, "When I get on the bus, I open a book."

🚌 bus🔗📖 book
🪥 At Night

"After I brush my teeth, I stretch, then I sleep."
