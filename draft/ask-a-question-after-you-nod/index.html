Ask a question after you nod, and the talk keeps going 💬

🌊 A Friend Says

Let's say a friend tells you, "I went to the sea."

If you only say, "Wow, nice," the talk stops there.

❓ Add One Question

Try adding 1 question: "Nice! Did you eat seafood?"
