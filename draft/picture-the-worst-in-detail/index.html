Imagine the worst in detail, and the brain says "Only this much" 🔦

☁️ Blurry Worry

A worry looks bigger when it is blurry.

☁️Blurry = big
🔍Clear = small
🔍 Look Closely

So I try to imagine the worst thing in detail, on purpose.

📝 For Example

For example, if I fail an exam: When is the next exam? Who can I ask? Will it be a funny story in 3 years?

⚡ TRY IT
Shrink the fog monster
😱 What if I fail the exam?!
☁️ Fog: 100%
  • 📅 Next month. I can try again.
  • 🐧 A friend who passed it, and a good book.
  • 😂 "I failed once!" It is a funny story now.
Tap the questions. Look at the worst in detail.
😌 Oh… even the worst is only this much.
😌 Only This

Then it is said the brain feels safe: "Oh, even the worst is only this much."

📚 I learned this from the Japanese blog "Panda no Ondo".
