Keeping things you do not use is the real loss 📦

😖 Fear of Loss

It seems we cannot throw things away because we do not want to lose.

🔄 The Real Loss

But really, keeping things you do not use makes you lose space and time.

"If I throw it away, I lose."
"If I keep it, I lose."
