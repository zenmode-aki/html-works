When you get nervous, narrate it and hop lightly 🎤

🚫 The Wrong Way

They say telling yourself "Calm down" when you are nervous makes it worse.

🎙️ Narrate It

Instead, narrate it in your head, like "My hands are getting cold."

When you see yourself from the outside, you start to calm down.

⚡ TRY IT
